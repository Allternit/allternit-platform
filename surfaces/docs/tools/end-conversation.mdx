title: 'end_conversation'
description: 'End a chat after sustained abuse or on request'
---

## Overview

`end_conversation` ends one of Gizzi's own chats. It is used only after a warning and failed redirects, or after you ask and confirm. It is never used when someone may be in crisis. You can always start a new chat.

## Parameters

| Parameter | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `reason` | `sustained_abuse` or `user_requested` | Why the chat ends |
| `warned` | boolean | True when the assistant warned you in an earlier reply |
| `summary` | string, optional | One neutral line for the record, up to 500 characters |

## Result

The reason and `ended: true`. An ended chat refuses new messages (HTTP 409, "This chat has ended. Start a new chat."), and the apps show **Start a new chat**.

## Permission

It is on for Gizzi's own chats only, never for bots or customer channels. It is allowed in every mode.

## Surfaces

Desktop, web, phone layout, PWA, iOS and the terminal show the ended state. Android follows the PWA. Family: `session`.
