Agreement with another model is not evidence of correctness. A primitive gains authority only through measured calibration on held-out data.

## Architecture contract

Initial gate per primitive:

- A calibration artifact bound to the exact model, revision, runtime, question and candidate schema.
- Expected calibration error at or below 0.05 on held-out data.
- At most 5% observed error on the auto-act subset.
- A statistically meaningful held-out sample reported with confidence bounds.
- Reversible, low-consequence decisions only at first.
- Hard policy and deterministic verification stay authoritative.
- Abstain or escalate outside the calibrated coverage region.

Tracked metrics: accuracy, F1, Brier score, negative log-likelihood, coverage at risk, and flip and order sensitivity. Authority expands per primitive, only after that primitive passes its own eval.

## Current implementation

The decision runtime is on main and S1 runs shadow-only: it records what it would have decided and takes no action. No primitive has been promoted out of shadow.
