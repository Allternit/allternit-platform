**Status: PREVIEW (internal; not exposed through api.allternit.com).** See [status labels](/architecture#status-labels).

A divergence report says whether a replay matched its recording and whether any difference was expected.

## Architecture contract

`DivergenceReportV1` names the cassette and the replay run and gives a verdict: `IDENTICAL`, `EXPECTED_DIVERGENCE` or `UNEXPECTED_DIVERGENCE`, plus the list of divergences. Expected divergence is recorded in advance, for example a timestamp. Unexpected divergence is a defect signal.

## Current implementation

On main, internal only. Recorded-only replay produces a divergence report against the frozen schema. It is not reachable through `https://api.allternit.com` yet.

## Developer relevance

When replay lands, treat an unexpected divergence as a failing test.
