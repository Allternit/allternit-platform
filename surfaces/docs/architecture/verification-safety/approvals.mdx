An approval is a recorded decision by a person, tied to a specific action. It does not weaken a hard deny.

## Architecture contract

Policy decisions are `ALLOW`, `ALLOW_WITH_LIMITS`, `ASK` and `DENY`, with a precedence of hard deny, hard allow, soft, cognitive, then fail-closed. `ASK` creates an attention request; the answer becomes a scoped, expiring grant. Escalation is driven by confidence, consequence, irreversibility, cost, novelty and verification failure, not by blanket review. Money and client communication are always human-approved.

Missing calibration, low confidence, a judge timeout or a judge error all resolve to `ASK`, never `ALLOW`.

## Current implementation

The attention gate is on main inside the work runtime. Agency API attention endpoints are a preview contract.

Design actions so an approval answers one specific question. Do not batch unrelated effects behind one approval.
