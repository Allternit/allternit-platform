**Status: AVAILABLE (contract) · PREVIEW (spawn caps in the drive runner).** See [status labels](/architecture#status-labels).

A budget turns "keep going until done" into a bounded, auditable contract.

## Architecture contract

`ResourceBudget` has `max_cost_units`, `max_tokens`, `max_wall_ms`, `max_tool_calls`, `max_attempts` and `max_spawns`. Budgets are enforced by the system, never by the component being budgeted. Exhaustion is an explicit `BUDGET` error family outcome, and hard limits are deterministic. A retry must change something, so attempts cannot loop silently.

## Current implementation

The drive runner, an opt-in operator command, applies spawn caps. A general per-run budget enforcer is not claimed as on main.

## Availability

| Part | Status |
|------|--------|
| Budget contract | **AVAILABLE (contract)** |
| Spawn caps in drive runner | **PREVIEW (opt-in)** |
| Uniform per-run enforcement of every limit | **DESIGN — NOT YET IMPLEMENTED** |

## Developer relevance

Set budgets on every run you start through the API, and treat a budget error as a result, not a fault.
