A decision is only allowed to act when its confidence is **calibrated**: when a stated 0.9 is right about 90% of the time on data the component has not seen. Calibration is measured per primitive and recorded in a manifest.

## Architecture contract

### The calibration manifest

`DecisionCalibrationManifestV1` is the deployed unit of calibration. It is bound to the exact component revision, tokenizer, quantization, serving runtime, question, candidate schema, candidate set and threshold profile, and carries a fingerprint of that scope. If anything in the scope changes, the manifest no longer binds and the decision falls back.

### The go-live gate

S1 stays in shadow until calibrated. For each primitive, going live requires:

- a calibration artifact bound to the exact scope above
- expected calibration error (ECE) of at most **0.05** on held-out data
- at most **5%** observed error on the subset the decision would act on automatically
- a statistically meaningful held-out sample, reported with confidence bounds
- reversible, low-consequence decisions only at first

Hard policy and deterministic verification stay authoritative. Outside the calibrated coverage region the decision abstains or escalates. Each primitive's authority expands only after its own evaluation passes.

**Agreement with another model is not a go-live criterion.** Two models agreeing says nothing about whether either is right.

Tracked metrics: accuracy, F1, Brier score, negative log-likelihood, ECE, coverage at risk, and sensitivity to flipping or reordering candidates.

Why: a decision that acts automatically is only as safe as its confidence is honest. A per-primitive gate on held-out error lets S1 take on work one question at a time, with evidence.

## Current implementation

On main, the decision runtime has manifests with scope fingerprints and exact-binding checks, the go-live gate as code (including minimum sample floors, bounds on the auto-act error, and the reversible-only rule), and the metrics above. The gate cannot take agreement with another model as an input. **No calibration manifests exist yet**, so no primitive has passed and every S1 result is shadow-only.

## Developer relevance

If you want a decision primitive to act, you need labelled held-out data for that exact question and component. Until then it runs in shadow and its results are recorded, not used.
