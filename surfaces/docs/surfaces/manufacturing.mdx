## Overview

**Allternit Manufacturing** is the physical production arm of Allternit. It is a strategic planning surface inside the platform shell that describes how Allternit designs, prototypes, and produces its own hardware — from compute boxes and battery systems to rack accessories and robotics parts — while selling excess manufacturing capacity to local businesses.

The surface lays out the six operating divisions, the staged build plan, and the target revenue mix for turning the microfactory into an AI-driven manufacturing operating system and, eventually, a distributed manufacturing network.

<Note>
  Manufacturing is currently marked as **Strategic Draft**. The numbers, phases, and service mix are planning targets rather than live production data.
</Note>

## How to access it

Manufacturing runs inside the Allternit platform shell:

```text
https://app.allternit.com/shell?view=manufacturing
```

Open it programmatically from shell code:

```typescript
open('manufacturing');
```

## Key concepts

### Divisions

Allternit Manufacturing is organized into six divisions that cover the full hardware lifecycle:

| Division | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Design Studio** | Industrial design, CAD modeling, reverse engineering, and material selection. |
| **Prototype Lab** | Same-day iteration for Allternit hardware and customer projects. |
| **Digital Manufacturing** | Material-specific print farms for PLA/PETG, ABS/ASA, engineering nylon, and resin. |
| **Product Manufacturing** | Allternit's own catalog of physical products. |
| **Manufacturing Services** | B2B production for replacement parts, jigs, fixtures, and low-volume runs. |
| **Hardware R&D** | Supports the broader Allternit ecosystem with portable AI stations, edge servers, and robotics platforms. |

### Build phases

The roadmap is split into three phases:

| Phase | Timeframe | Goals |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1 — Validate** | Months 1–3 | 3 printers, 1 workstation, 1 shipping station; first internal compute box prototype; 3–5 pilot B2B customers; $2k–$5k monthly revenue. |
| **Phase 2 — Scale** | Months 4–12 | 10 printers + resin capability; standard service packages; internal manufacturing queue software; $10k+ monthly revenue. |
| **Phase 3 — Automate** | Year 2+ | 40 printers across material farms; AI scheduling and failure detection; customer portal live; $30k+ monthly revenue. |

### Target revenue mix

| Stream | Share |
|---|---|
| Internal product manufacturing | 30% |
| B2B industrial printing / MaaS | 30% |
| Engineering & CAD services | 20% |
| Consumer / catalog products | 10% |
| Prototyping for startups | 10% |
