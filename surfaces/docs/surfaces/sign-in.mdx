## What you see

Every place you sign in to Allternit shows the same short animated story next to the sign-in form:

| Where | Page |
| --- | --- |
| ai.allternit.com on a computer | `/sign-in` and `/sign-up`, showcase on the left, form on the right |
| Allternit Desktop | The first-run screen and `/sign-in` inside the app |
| Terminal login (`gizzi login`) | The browser page the CLI opens |
| ai.allternit.com on a phone and the m.allternit.com app | Showcase at the top, sign-in form below it |

The story has three slides:

1. **Talk it through.** A listening microphone, a running clock, and a transcript card that fills in word by word.
2. **Unbox a team of bots.** A box opens, four bots (Research, Inbox, Code, Sales) jump out with confetti, then their role tags appear.
3. **Hand off the busywork.** A run card counts up the finished steps, fills its progress bar, ticks each step, and shows the finished work.

Slides change every 5.6 seconds. Swipe left or right on a phone, or press the ticks under the picture to jump to a slide. The slides stop changing while your mouse is over them or a tick has keyboard focus, and while the tab is in the background.

If your device asks for reduced motion, each slide appears already finished and the slides only change when you swipe or press a tick.

On the phone and in the m.allternit.com app the showcase follows your light or dark theme. The web sign-in pages are always light.

## For developers

The showcase is one component, `SignInShowcase` in `allternit-ai/src/components/auth/SignInShowcase.tsx`, with its styles in `sign-in-showcase.css` next to it. It replaced the older `AuthPreview` card.

```tsx
<SignInShowcase variant="wide" />                 // web and Desktop sign-in pages
<SignInShowcase variant="wide" tone="auto" />     // Desktop first-run (OnboardingFlow)
<SignInShowcase variant="phone" tone="auto" />    // phone sign-in (fabric-session App.tsx)
```

- `variant` sets the size. Each scene is drawn on a 320 × 300 canvas and scaled: 1.3× for `wide`, 1× for `phone`. A container query brings `wide` down to 1× under 460 px and both down to 0.92× under 340 px.
- `tone="auto"` reads `html[data-theme="dark"]`. `tone="light"` (the default) keeps the light colours.
- Each slide is remounted when it comes on screen, so its framer-motion delays play from the start. A scene's `still` prop renders the finished state for reduced motion.
- The sign-in form itself (`PlatformSignIn`, Clerk) is not part of the showcase and does not move.
- Tests: `SignInShowcase.test.tsx` covers the ticks, auto-advance and swipe.
