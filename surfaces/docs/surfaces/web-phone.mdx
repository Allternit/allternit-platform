## Overview

The **Allternit web phone** is the phone experience of the Allternit agent workspace. It comes in two forms:

- **The installable PWA at m.allternit.com.** Open it in the phone browser and add it to your home screen. It supports push notifications and an install coach that walks you through adding it.
- **The phone layout of ai.allternit.com.** On a small touch screen, the workspace automatically switches to the phone layout. No separate app or account is needed.

Both forms are the same UI with the same account, sessions, bots and computers as Desktop. Anything you do on the phone is there when you sit down at the desktop, and the other way around.

## Navigation

Navigation on the phone is a left drawer plus a back stack.

- **Left drawer.** Tap **☰** in the header, or swipe in from the left edge at the root, to open the drawer. It holds the mode wheel (**Home**, **Bots**, **Code**, **ACI** — the same four modes as Desktop), each mode's sections, and your recent chats. See [Phone drawer and modes](/surfaces/mobile#phone-drawer-and-modes) for the full layout.
- **Back stack.** Screens you open stack on top of each other. The header's back button and the left-edge swipe both go back one screen. At the root there is nothing to go back to, so the left edge opens the drawer instead.
- **Close the drawer** by swiping from the right edge of the drawer or the dimmed area beside it.

## Gestures

Every gesture has a tap equivalent — the gestures are shortcuts, not requirements.

| Gesture | How | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Delete a recent chat | Swipe the chat left in the recents list, tap **Delete**, then **Confirm** | Removes the chat |
| Reorder recents | Long-press a recent chat and drag it | Moves it in the list. The order stays on this device |
| Refresh sessions | Pull down on a chat list | Reloads the sessions |
| Peek a message | Long-press the message | Opens a preview with **Copy**, feedback and **Retry** actions |
| Reply to a message | Swipe the message right | Quotes it in the composer |
| Resize a bottom sheet | Drag the handle up or down | Moves the sheet between its sizes; drag down past the smallest size to close it |
| Go back | Swipe in from the left edge | Pops one screen off the back stack; at the root it opens the drawer |
| Close the drawer | Swipe from the right edge of the drawer or the scrim | Closes the drawer |
| Dismiss a toast | Swipe it sideways | Removes the notification |
| Open an image full screen | Tap an image in the chat | It grows out of the thumbnail into a full-screen view — drag it down, tap the backdrop, or press Escape to close |
| Dismiss the keyboard | Drag the composer down | Closes the keyboard and springs the composer back |
| Give at a scroll edge | Pull past the top or bottom of a list that is already at its end | The list resists and springs back |
| Close a sheet with its buttons | Tap the X or the area outside the sheet | The sheet animates out the same way as dragging it down |

## Accessibility

All gestures are optional: every gesture action also has a tap or click equivalent. When reduced motion is enabled on the device, the app uses instant transitions instead of the tracking animations, without changing what any control does.
