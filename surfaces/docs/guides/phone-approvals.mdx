When a bot uses a linked phone, some actions wait for you. The bot stops, a request appears for you to review, and the action runs only if you approve it. This applies to a plain-English task, a saved phone skill, a routine, and a bot that taps on its own.

## What the bot must ask about

Allternit looks at the button the bot is about to tap, the field it is about to type into and the app it is in. It asks you before these:

- **Paying or buying.** Buttons such as Pay, Buy, Checkout, Place order or Transfer.
- **Deleting.** Buttons such as Delete, Remove, Erase or Uninstall.
- **Sending.** Send, Post, Reply or Submit, and the enter key in a message box.
- **Calling.** Call, Dial or FaceTime.
- **Passwords and codes.** Typing into a password, passcode, card number or security code field, showing or copying a password, and reading your texts, which can hold sign-in codes.
- **Bank, wallet and broker apps.** Opening one, or tapping inside one. One approval covers that app for the rest of the run.
- **Security settings.** Two-factor, passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, screen lock and similar.

Texts and calls the bot places with the phone tools ask you too.

The bot's own model can also flag a step as sensitive and add a request. It cannot remove one the rules made, so text on the screen cannot talk the bot out of asking.

## What you see and where

The request shows in **Needs you**:

- on ai.allternit.com and the m.allternit.com app
- in the Allternit phone app
- as a dialog in Allternit Desktop

It shows the phone, the app, what the bot is about to do and a picture of the screen at that moment. On an iPhone, the notification opens **Review**. There is no one-tap Approve in the notification, because you should see the screen first.

### On the phone being driven

On an Android phone linked with Phone Link, the Phone Link app shows a quiet notification, **Waiting for your approval in Allternit**, while a request is open. It says what the bot wants to do and in which app, in plain words, for example "Your bot wants to send a message in WhatsApp". It never shows screen content or anything the bot typed. It goes away when you approve or deny, when the request expires or the task ends, and within about a minute if the link to your computer drops (it comes back by itself if the link does and the request is still open). It needs no new permission. On an older version of Phone Link the notification does not appear and nothing else changes. iPhones show no notification of this kind.

## Approve and Deny

- **Approve** lets that one action run, once. Before it runs, Allternit looks at the screen again. If the screen changed since the request, the approval is not used and you get a new request.
- **Deny** ends the task. The bot does not try another way to reach the same result.

If you approve and the action is not used within 10 minutes, you are asked again.

## When nobody answers

- **A task** waits up to 5 minutes. If you have not answered, it stops and reports that approval timed out.
- **A routine** stops at that step and shows one item, "Routine stopped at a step that needs you", with a **Retry** button.

The time a task spends waiting for you is not counted as phone minutes.

## Who can approve

Only you, signed in, in the app. An API key cannot approve a request. A reply of "yes" to a text message does not approve one either, because a text sender can be faked.

If Allternit Desktop cannot check an action, for example when the runtime cannot be reached, the action does not run.

## Limits of this version

- The words Allternit looks for are English. In another language, only the bot's own flag and the bank, wallet and broker app rule catch a sensitive button.
- An action that no rule recognises, and that the bot does not flag, can still run without a request. This is a safeguard, not a promise that every sensitive action is caught. Keep sensitive apps off a phone you link, or sign out of them, if you do not want a bot near them.
- A bot that takes messages from other people can try a phone action because of such a message. A sensitive action still waits for you, so read the request before you approve it.
- Approvals apply to phones driven from Desktop. A bot loop that runs on the phone itself is not part of this version.
