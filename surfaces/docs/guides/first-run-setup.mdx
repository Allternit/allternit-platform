The first time you open Allternit with a new account, Gizzi and A:// set up your workspace with you, one question at a time. On Desktop and ai.allternit.com the left side shows **Your workspace**, a graph that builds as you answer; the right side is the setup rail. On a phone the graph sits on top and the rail below it. The [Allternit iPhone app](/guides/iphone-app) runs the same setup, with the same steps, after you sign in.

There are three questions and then a view of everything that is connected: your AI, your phone, and your main bot. There is no computer step and no theme step. You can go back, skip any question, and change every answer later in Settings. Setup uses the same rows and sign-in sheets as [Connections](/guides/connections).

## 1. Your AI

Gizzi asks which AI to think with, and says to use the one you already pay for. The choices are in three groups, the same ones as Settings → Connections:

- **Allternit Cloud**: models hosted by Allternit. It works with no computer. If you pick nothing, Allternit Cloud is the brain, so **Next** is always open.
- **Your plans and accounts**: ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Kimi, Gemini and GitHub Copilot. Each row opens the shortest sign-in the vendor allows. The tile says **Connected** only when the login is ready. Some plans need your own computer; the row says so and offers a fallback, such as an API key. See [Use the AI plan you already pay for](/guides/use-your-plan) for what works where. If a plan you connected earlier is not the brain yet, **Use this** makes it the brain.
- **More ways**: an API key, or a local brain that runs on this computer.

A choice that is not finished shows its real state (connecting, needs you, or the reason), never a success it did not have. **Skip** leaves you on Allternit Cloud; connect another brain later in Settings → Connections.

## 2. Your phone

Scan a QR code with your phone. It opens the Allternit app and signs in, and the row says **Connected** with the phone's name. Skip it if you do not want your phone connected yet.

Under **Later, if you need it**, one **Add a computer** row opens the ways to add one: your own Mac or PC, or a computer you subscribe to. Chat works without one; running agents needs one.

## 3. Meet your bots

Set up your bots in one card: see how bots hand work to each other, add bots from the template library to your team (drag them onto the grid or tap them), pick your main bot (**A://** by default, Gizzi, the Gizzi YouTube cast, or a vendor bot you already use), see and change when your bots work on an editable calendar, and give them a first job. Nothing is created until **Start the job**; the last page lists what worked and lets you retry what didn't. See [Meet your bots](/guides/meet-your-bots) for every page.

Skip leaves your bots for later; the Bots rail offers **Set up your main bot** until you have one, and the card opens on your first visit to Bot Mode.

## Agents need a computer

Chat with Allternit Cloud models works right away. To run agents, an account needs a computer: link your own, subscribe, or buy credits for a hosted computer. Free accounts do not get a free hosted computer. Setup never starts one for you. See [Choose how agents run](/guides/choose-how-agents-run) and [Add a connection](/build-on-allternit/add-a-connection).

## What Gizzi reports

After each choice A:// prints a short result card with what really happened, for example `▸ Your phone  Connected`. Finished steps stack above the next question as a log. A choice that is not finished yet shows **Chosen**, **Waiting** or the error, never a success it did not have.

## Everything connected

The last screen is a map of everything your account can reach right now: brains (including connected subscriptions), computers and your phone, your main bot and other bots, messaging channels, Memory Drive, Projects and connectors. Things you set up just now are highlighted; things already connected are solid; the rest are marked **Available** so you know what you can add. Hover or tap a node to read about it. When a computer is not reachable (for example on the web with no computer online), the parts that live on a computer say so.

Select **Open Allternit** to finish. Setup is recorded for your account, so it does not run again on your other devices.

## The hand-off into Allternit

**Open Allternit** plays a short hand-off of about four and a half seconds before Home opens. Everything you connected turns into a block, and the blocks fly in to build the **A://** mark. The coral core lands, a pulse goes out, and **WELCOME** with your name types under the mark. Then the **://** fades and the **A** flies into the Allternit logo at the top of Home. It looks the same on Desktop, on ai.allternit.com on a phone, and in the m.allternit.com app.

The name is the one you gave Allternit, exactly as you typed it. That's your **Preferred name** in Settings → Profile. Without one, it's the first word of your **Full name** there, then the first name you signed up with. With no name at all, it just says **WELCOME**.

Tap, click or press Escape to skip it. With **Reduce motion** turned on in your system settings, you see the A:// mark and your name for under a second instead of the animation.

## Run it again

Setup runs for an account that has not finished it. To change an answer later, use Settings: Connections for your AI, phone and computers, and Appearance for the theme (a new account starts on System). To change your main bot, use a bot's **⋯** menu or drag another bot onto the top of the Bots rail.

## Check what works

The last screen of setup is the [setup checklist](/guides/setup-checklist): each item your Allternit depends on, checked live, with a button to fix any gap. Find it again any time in Settings, then Setup.
