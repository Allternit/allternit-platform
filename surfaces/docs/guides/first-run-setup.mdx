title: First-run setup
description: Set up your workspace with Gizzi and A:// the first time you open Allternit, on Desktop, ai.allternit.com and your phone.
---

The first time you open Allternit with a new account, Gizzi and A:// set up your workspace with you, one question at a time. On Desktop and ai.allternit.com the left side shows **Your workspace**, a graph that builds as you answer; the right side is the setup rail. On a phone the graph sits on top and the rail below it.

There are four steps and then a view of everything that is connected. You can go back, skip any question, and change every answer later in Settings.

## 1. Where it runs

Choose where your agents run:

- **This Mac / This PC** (Desktop): Allternit looks for its engine on this computer. The tile says **Detected** only when it actually finds one. When you pick it, setup pairs the computer to your account and the result card says **Paired**. If the engine is not running, the card says so and offers the Desktop download.
- **Allternit Cloud computer**: an always-on computer that keeps working when yours sleeps. Setup checks your plan and your account's cloud computer and reports what it finds: its size and status when you have one, or that it comes with a paid plan. If you need a plan, **Open Allternit** at the end takes you to Settings → Billing.
- **Your own server**: in Allternit Desktop, connect a machine with a backend URL, over SSH, rent a VPS, or control a remote desktop. The same connection forms as Settings → Computers open inside the rail. On ai.allternit.com, on your phone and in the m.allternit.com app, you pair the server instead: press **Get a pairing code**, install the Allternit CLI on the server and run the command shown there. Setup says **Paired** only once the server shows up on your account. Codes work once and expire; if one expires, get a new one.

On a phone, **Your computer** lists the computers paired to your account and lets you pick the one your phone uses. A server is paired from Allternit Desktop.

## 2. Style

Pick **Light**, **Dark** or **System**. The change applies at once and is saved on this device; change it any time in Settings → Appearance.

## 3. Your AI

Pick the brain Gizzi uses by default.

- **Use your subscription**: ChatGPT, Claude or Kimi through your own plan. Connect runs the same flow as Settings → Connections inside the rail: you sign in on the vendor's own page on your Sessions computer, and you read and accept the provider-terms disclosure before the subscription becomes your default. The tile says **Connected** only when the login is ready. Without a Sessions computer, or while the sign-in is still pending, setup says so instead.
- **Allternit Cloud**: models hosted by Allternit. Pick one from the live catalog.
- **Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini**: connect with an API key, a key Allternit already holds for you, or (on Desktop) the vendor's command-line sign-in.
- **Local model** (Desktop): use a model already on this computer or download one.
- **More brains**: Qwen, Kimi, Z.ai and command-line tools found on this computer.

On a phone, local models and command-line sign-ins are added from Allternit Desktop; the phone says so on those rows.

## 4. Meet your bots

Set up your bots in one card: see how bots hand work to each other, add bots from the template library to your team (drag them onto the grid or tap them), pick your main bot (**A://** by default, Gizzi, the Gizzi YouTube cast, or a vendor bot you already use), see and change when your bots work on an editable calendar, and give them a first job. Nothing is created until **Start the job**; the last page lists what worked and lets you retry what didn't. See [Meet your bots](/guides/meet-your-bots) for every page.

Skip leaves your bots for later; the Bots rail offers **Set up your main bot** until you have one, and the card opens on your first visit to Bot Mode.

## What Gizzi reports

After each choice A:// prints a short result card with what really happened, for example `▸ Where it runs  This Mac  PAIRED`. Finished steps stack above the next question as a log. A choice that is not finished yet shows **Chosen**, **Waiting** or the error, never a success it did not have.

## Everything connected

The last screen is a map of everything your account can reach right now: brains (including connected subscriptions), computers and your phone, your main bot and other bots, messaging channels, Memory Drive, Projects and connectors. Things you set up just now are highlighted; things already connected are solid; the rest are marked **Available** so you know what you can add. Hover or tap a node to read about it. When a computer is not reachable (for example on the web with no computer online), the parts that live on a computer say so.

Select **Open Allternit** to finish. Setup is recorded for your account, so it does not run again on your other devices.

## The hand-off into Allternit

**Open Allternit** plays a short hand-off of about four and a half seconds before Home opens. Everything you connected turns into a block, and the blocks fly in to build the **A://** mark. The coral core lands, a pulse goes out, and **WELCOME** with your name types under the mark. Then the **://** fades and the **A** flies into the Allternit logo at the top of Home. It looks the same on Desktop, on ai.allternit.com on a phone, and in the m.allternit.com app.

The name is the one you gave Allternit, exactly as you typed it. That's your **Preferred name** in Settings → Profile. Without one, it's the first word of your **Full name** there, then the first name you signed up with. With no name at all, it just says **WELCOME**.

Tap, click or press Escape to skip it. With **Reduce motion** turned on in your system settings, you see the A:// mark and your name for under a second instead of the animation.

## Run it again

Setup runs for an account that has not finished it. To change an answer later, use Settings: Computers, Appearance, Models, and Connections. To change your main bot, use a bot's **⋯** menu or drag another bot onto the top of the Bots rail.
