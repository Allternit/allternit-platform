title: Allternit Bots
description: The bots-only Allternit app. It opens straight into Bot Mode and installs on your phone as its own Home Screen app.
---

Allternit Bots is Allternit with only your bots in it: bot chats, group chats, bot projects and routines. It is the same app and the same account as full Allternit, so your bots, chats and settings are the same in both. It just leaves out Code, Cowork, computers and the other modes, and opens straight into Bot Mode.

## Open it

| Where | Address |
|---|---|
| Any browser | [bots.allternit.com](https://bots.allternit.com) |
| ai.allternit.com | [ai.allternit.com/bots](https://ai.allternit.com/bots) |
| Phone | [m.allternit.com/bots](https://m.allternit.com/bots) |
| Allternit Desktop | the `/bots` page of the app |

Sign in with your Allternit account. There is no separate sign-up.

## Put it on your phone

Open [m.allternit.com/bots](https://m.allternit.com/bots) in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) and use **Add to Home Screen** (Safari's Share menu) or **Install app** (Chrome's menu). The icon is named **Allternit Bots** and opens into Bot Mode, not into full Allternit. You can have both on your Home Screen; they share your sign-in.

To put one bot on your Home Screen as its own icon instead, see [Bots on your Home Screen](/guides/bot-home-screen).

## What's different from full Allternit

- **Opens into Bot Mode.** There is no mode dial. Links that open a thread or a bot (for example from a notification) stay inside Allternit Bots.
- **Its own Settings list.** Settings shows only the pages a bots user needs, in this order: Profile, Account, Notifications, Memory, Response style, Voice, Connections, Connectors, Agent rules, Usage, Billing, Privacy, General, About. Settings for Code, Cowork, computers and admin pages stay in full Allternit.
- **Open full Allternit.** The **Open full Allternit** row (under the Bots rail, and in Settings) takes you to the full app. From bots.allternit.com it opens ai.allternit.com; elsewhere it opens the same site without `/bots`.

Notifications, including [quiet hours](/guides/notifications#quiet-hours), are the same for your whole account, so changing them in Allternit Bots changes them in full Allternit too.

## For developers

The bots app lives in the allternit-ai repo. `src/lib/bots-app.ts` decides it once per page load (`isBotsApp()`: host `bots.allternit.com` or a `/bots` path), holds the Settings allow-list (`BOTS_APP_SETTINGS`, ids from `settings.config.ts`) and `fullAppUrl()`, and points the page head at `public/bots.webmanifest` (`name` "Allternit Bots", `start_url` `/bots`) so installing adds the bots app. `src/providers/mode-provider.tsx` pins Bot Mode; the phone build (`src/fabric-session/`) hides the mode dial in `PhoneDrawer.tsx` and filters `SettingsScreen.tsx`. On the PWA host, `fabric-session-pwa-functions/_bots-app.js` and `fabric-session-pwa-functions/bots/` serve the app at `/bots`. Like every phone feature, the m.allternit.com side ships with the separate PWA deploy.
