Open Settings from the **Settings…** menu item or from your account menu. A sidebar on the left lists the pages. A search box at the top finds a page by its name or by words inside it. The same pages are in the web app at ai.allternit.com. On a phone the layout is different, see [Settings on a phone](/guides/phone-settings).

Each page in the sidebar has a small white tile with an icon. The icon moves once when you open the page or press its row. Turn **Reduce motion** on in **General** and the icons stay still.

## Settings

The pages about you and your account.

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Your name, picture and how you appear to others. |
| **General** | Theme (Light, Dark or System), shown as small pictures of each look, plus Reduce motion and other app preferences. |
| **Account** | Sign-in details and how you sign in. |
| **Organization & access** | Your organizations, members and what each role can do. |
| **Privacy** | What is stored, what is shared, and how to export or delete it. |
| **Billing & usage** | Your plan, what you have used and payment details. |
| **Setup** | Steps to finish getting a new account or computer ready. |
| **Notifications** | Which alerts you get and where. See [Notifications](/guides/notifications). |
| **Meetings** | Meeting notes, when your account has them on. |
| **Status** | Whether Allternit services are running. |

## Customize

How the assistant and your bots behave.

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Connections** | The subscriptions and accounts your bots use. |
| **Voice** | Dictation and spoken replies. |
| **Memory** | What the assistant remembers about you, and a way to edit or clear it. |
| **Response style** | How long and how formal replies are. |
| **Open models** | Models that run on your own machine. |
| **Gizzi Code** | Settings for the terminal tool. |
| **Chat** | Chat window preferences. |
| **Agent rules** | What bots may do on their own and what needs your approval. |
| **Skills** | Reusable instructions your bots can use. |
| **Subagents** | Helper bots a main bot can hand work to. |
| **Connectors** | Apps and services a bot can reach. |
| **App approvals** | Which connected apps a bot may use, and what you approved. |
| **Your twin** | The profile your twin works from. |
| **Plugins** | Add-ons you installed. |
| **Lens Context** | What a bot can see about the screen it is working on. |

## Desktop app

These pages are about this computer. They show in the desktop app.

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Devices** | Phones and other devices paired to your account. |
| **Computers** | The computers your bots can work on, including this one. |
| **Desktop pet** | The small helper that sits on your screen: when it offers help, which apps it may read, and a mute and block list. |
| **Extensions** | Browser and editor extensions. |
| **Developer** | Logs and checks for finding a problem. |

## Advanced

A collapsed group for operator tools most people never need: Models, Image providers, Video providers, MCP, API Keys, Permissions, Allternit Mobile, Infrastructure, Security, Agents and Webhooks. Open the group in the sidebar to see them.

## About

**About** shows the app version, links to Terms and Privacy, and **Keyboard shortcuts**.

### Keyboard shortcuts

Under **About › Keyboard shortcuts** a table lists each action and its shortcut. Click a shortcut, press the new keys, and it is saved. The defaults are also listed in [Allternit Desktop](/surfaces/desktop#keyboard-shortcuts).

## Theme pictures

On **General**, Light, Dark and System each show a small picture of the app in that look. Click one to switch. Light is the default until you pick another. Dark uses graphite colours.

## Reduce motion

**Reduce motion** replaces large movements with short fades and turns the icon motion in Settings off. It applies as soon as you switch it. If your system already asks for reduced motion, the app follows that too. How the icons work is in [AnimatedIcon](/architecture/animated-icon).
