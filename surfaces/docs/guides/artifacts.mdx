## What an artifact is

An artifact is something Allternit makes that you'd show someone: a doc, a
deck, a sheet, a design, a dashboard, a motion piece, a web page, an
interactive card, a diagram, an image or a code file. Each one is saved to
your Allternit account, so it's the same artifact on Allternit Desktop,
ai.allternit.com, the phone layout and the m.allternit.com app.

Every artifact has a kind, a title, and versions. Each save is a new
version; nothing you saved is overwritten.

Artifacts are **private** until you share them.

## Starting an artifact

There are four ways in:

1. **Ask in any chat.** "Write a launch brief", "make a deck from these
   notes", "draw the signup flow". Allternit makes an artifact on its own when
   the result is long (about 15 lines or more), stands on its own, or is
   something you'll edit or reuse. Short answers stay in the chat. You can
   always say "make this an artifact".
2. **Pick an Output in the message box.** Click **Output** under the message
   box and choose **Artifact** (Allternit picks the format), **Docs**,
   **Slides**, **Sheets**, **Design**, **Dashboards** or **Motion**. The choice
   applies to your next message, then clears. Click the × on the chip to
   clear it yourself.
3. **Type a slash command.** `/docs`, `/slides`, `/sheets`, `/design`,
   `/dashboard` and `/motion` pick that Output. You can type the request
   straight after it: `/slides a 6-slide pitch for the Q4 launch`.
4. **Make something new** on the Artifacts tab (see below).
5. **The + next to Artifacts** in the sidebar lists every kind you can make,
   grouped as Office (Docs, Sheets, Slides), Create (Dashboards, Motion,
   Design, Page, Build) and Generate (Deep Research, Image, Video). Kinds your
   plan doesn't include show the plan they need.

Each kind has its own Allternit icon (a cream tile with its block letter and
a coloured page), used the same way in the Output menu, the sidebar, the
Artifacts tab, chat cards and the artifact window.

Which Outputs you see depends on your plan and your organization:

| Output | Plans |
|---|---|
| Docs, Slides, Sheets, Design | Every plan, including Free |
| Dashboards | Paid plans |
| Motion | Super, Ultra and Team |

Organization admins can turn templates off for everyone in the org. A turned
off template doesn't appear in the Output menu. See
[For organization admins](#for-organization-admins).

Dashboards are live: each tile reads your connected apps and warehouses as you.
See [Dashboards](/guides/dashboards).

Interactive cards (the live answers with sliders, tables and buttons) stay in
the chat. To keep one, hover it and choose **Open as artifact**.

## The artifact window

An artifact opens in its own window beside the chat. On a phone it opens
full screen.

- **Title.** Click it to rename (if you can edit).
- **Versions.** Editors can open any earlier version from the clock menu.
  "Back to latest" returns to the current one.
- **Share** (the dark button at the top right) opens the sharing settings
  (below). The settings open on top of the artifact window.
- **Copy** copies the artifact's content (an image copies its link). The arrow
  next to it opens the **Download** menu.
- **Turn into motion.** On a doc or a dashboard (Super, Ultra and Team), the
  **Download** menu also has **Turn into motion**, which asks Allternit for a
  short animation of it. See [Motion](/guides/motion).
- **Download** lists the files this kind can save, each with its file type.
  Every artifact has **Source file**. Docs export to **Word**, **PDF** and **Markdown**;
  Slides to **PowerPoint** and **PDF**; Sheets to **Excel** and **CSV**;
  Design to **HTML**, **.zip**, **PowerPoint** and **PDF**. Diagrams also
  export **PNG** and **SVG**; pages export an **HTML file** (and, on
  Allternit Desktop with HyperFrames, an **MP4 video**); dashboards export a
  **PNG image** and a **PDF** through the print window; motion exports an **MP4 video** on every surface, in the cloud where the
  browser can't encode it (see [Motion](/guides/motion)).
- **Fix with Gizzi.** When a diagram, a motion piece or a dashboard tile fails
  to render, the error has a **Fix with Gizzi** button. It sends the error to
  your open conversation, which reads the artifact and saves a fixed version.
- **Ask Gizzi about your artifacts anywhere.** In the terminal, a bot or a
  channel, Gizzi can find your artifacts by name, read and change them, post
  comments, and (after you approve) change sharing or delete one.
- **Comments** and the avatars of anyone else who has it open appear for
  Docs, Slides, Sheets and Design. See
  [Comments, presence and versions](/guides/artifact-collaboration).
- Docs, Slides, Sheets and Design are edited right in the window and save on
  their own; see [Docs, Slides, Sheets and Design artifacts](/guides/artifact-editors).
- On Desktop and the web, drag the window's left edge to resize it, or use
  the full-width button to give it the whole view. Press the × to close it.

Web pages run in a sandbox: they can't read your Allternit data, cookies or
other artifacts. A page can still save its own data, ask AI and use your
connected apps if you approve it; see
[Pages that act like apps](/guides/artifact-apps).

If someone else saves a newer version while you're editing a text artifact,
Allternit merges your change onto theirs. When it can't (for example, both of
you changed the same lines), the window asks whether to keep yours or use
theirs.

## Sharing

Only the owner can change sharing. Open an artifact and click **Share**.

**Who can open it**

- **Only you** — the default.
- **Specific people** — add people by email.
- **Your org** — everyone in your organization can view it.
- **Anyone with the link** — no sign-in needed; view only.

**What they can do**

- **Can view** — open it and read comments.
- **Commenter** — also comment.
- **Can edit** — also change it and save new versions.

Docs have **Can view** and **Can edit** only, and are shared with your org
(or a link, if your admin allows external sharing), not by email.

**Which version they see.** With **Always share latest version** on, viewers
see each new version. Turn it off to keep showing the version you shared
while you keep editing.

**When link sharing is off.** "Anyone with the link" is unavailable when:

- the artifact uses AI or connectors while someone views it, or
- your organization has external sharing turned off.

The Share window says which one applies.

**People outside your organization.** Invites by email to people outside
your org work only if your org allows outside invites (personal accounts
always can), up to 50 people per artifact. An outside invite expires after
30 days if it isn't accepted.

On phones, sharing is read-only: you can see who has access and copy the
link; change it from Desktop or the web.

**Copy link** copies a link to the artifact. People you shared it with open
it in Allternit; with "Anyone with the link", anyone can open it.

## The Artifacts tab

**Artifacts** in the sidebar (or the Artifacts section on the phone) lists
everything you own and everything shared with you, newest first.

- **Search** by title.
- **Filter by** kind, and by owner: *Everything*, *Mine*, *Shared with me*.
- Switch between **grid** and **list**.
- Labels show where an artifact came from: **Cowork**, **Code**, **Bot**, or
  **Legacy** (see below). Shared items show the owner's avatar.
- **Previews**: each card shows the artifact itself: the image, the first
  page of a doc, the top rows of a sheet, the first slide, a snapshot of a
  design, page or card, the diagram, or the first frame of a motion piece.
  Previews load as you scroll. A snapshot never runs scripts. If a preview
  can't be drawn, the card shows the artifact's thumbnail or its kind
  illustration.
- The **⋯** menu on your own artifacts has **Delete**. Deleting is permanent:
  there's no trash, and people you shared it with lose access.

### Make something new

The row of animated tiles above your artifacts is the template gallery. Hover
a tile to see what it makes and replay how it's built.

- **Docs, Sheets, Slides, Dashboards, Motion, Design, Page**: opens a new chat
  with that Output picked. Describe what you want and send. A tile for a
  template your plan doesn't include shows a lock and the plan that unlocks
  it. Templates your organization turned off aren't shown.
- **Research, Build, Image, Video**: opens a session for that type.

On a phone, the tiles slide sideways.

### Start from your connected tools

Below the tiles, chips start a chat from the tools you've connected:
**What needs my attention** checks all of them, and **Catch me up on** a tool
summarizes that one. With nothing connected, **Connect your tools** opens the
connector settings.

### Design and Office

**Design** in the header opens Allternit Design and your design systems (start
a new one, open one, or browse the marketplace). **Office** opens Allternit
Office in its own window.

If you're signed out, the tab asks you to sign in; offline, it says so and
shows your artifacts again when you reconnect.

## Older artifacts (Legacy)

Artifacts you made before this update — Office docs, sheets and decks,
Design projects, saved chat artifacts, canvas outputs and Cowork's local
artifacts — are copied into your account automatically the first time you
sign in on each device. They show the **Legacy** label. Nothing is deleted:
the originals stay where they were.

Legacy artifacts open, can be shared and exported, and keep working as they
did. Office docs and Design projects have **Open in editor** to edit them in
their original editor.

The copy runs in the background and never blocks the app. If a device is
offline, or Allternit Desktop isn't running when you sign in on the web, the
rest is copied the next time the app starts.

## For organization admins

Open **Settings → Organization → Artifacts** (Desktop and web; on a phone the
section only says to manage this on desktop or web). Admins can change it;
other members see the same values read-only.

- **Artifacts.** The master switch. Off, members can't create artifacts and
  the organization's public links close.
- **Sharing outside the organization.** Allows "Anyone with the link". Docs
  can only be shared by link when this is on.
- **Invite people outside the organization.** Allows email invites to people who
  aren't members. Each invite expires after 30 days until accepted.
- **Show who's viewing.** Presence on open artifacts.
- **Pages can use connected apps.** The connector kill switch. Off, no page in
  the organization can call a connected app, even one a viewer approved.
- **Templates.** One switch each for Docs, Slides, Sheets, Design, Dashboards,
  Motion and Page. Dashboards and Motion are on for Team and off for
  Enterprise until an admin turns them on. A turned off template disappears
  from the Output menu and the gallery.
- **Shared outside.** Every artifact from the organization that can be opened
  by link or by invited email addresses, with its owner. **Allow outside**
  lets that one artifact be shared by link even when sharing outside is off
  (not for Docs); **Remove exception** takes it back.

Pages that use AI or connected apps are never available to people outside the
organization, and can't be shared with a public link, whatever these switches
say. See [Pages that act like apps](/guides/artifact-apps).

## For developers

- Client code: `allternit-ai/src/lib/artifacts/` — `kinds.ts` (kind
  registry), `api.ts` (`/api/v2/artifacts` client), `store.ts`
  (`useArtifacts`), `legacy-import.ts`, `share-rules.ts`, `plans.ts`,
  `output-choice.ts`.
- The one window: `src/components/artifacts/ArtifactWindow.tsx`; hosts in
  `ArtifactWindowHost.tsx` (overlay/sheet at the app roots, docked in
  ChatView) and `ArtifactPaneWindow.tsx` (Cowork and project panes).
- Model contract: the turn carries `output: {kind}` when an Output is picked,
  and the system prompt carries the artifacts manual
  (`ARTIFACTS_MANUAL`), the motion body manual when Motion is on for the user,
  plus one line for the picked Output. The model creates
  artifacts with `artifact_create` / `artifact_update`; the transcript renders
  each call as a card and saves `persisted:false` results under the same id.
- API, schema and sharing rules: `allternit/docs/design/artifacts-v2.md`.
