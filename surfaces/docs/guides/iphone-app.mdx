title: Allternit for iPhone
description: "The native Allternit app for iPhone. Sign in, find your way around, reach your computer, let your bots see the iPhone's screen and use its browser, and manage or delete your account."
---

Allternit for iPhone is the native app version of [m.allternit.com](https://m.allternit.com). It uses the same account as ai.allternit.com and Allternit Desktop, so your chats, bots, projects and settings are the same everywhere. It needs iOS 17 or later.

## Sign in

Install **Allternit** from the App Store and open it. The sign-in screen is the same one m.allternit.com shows, with its moving preview of what Allternit does. You can:

- **Continue with Apple**, **GitHub** or **Google**,
- **Continue with email**, or
- **Create an account** (under **New here?**).

The first time you open it, **Meet your bots** sets up your team:

1. Pick bots for your team from a list you can filter by category. Bots already on your account aren't made twice.
2. Choose who leads: A:// as it comes (you can rename it, up to 24 characters), A:// dressed as one of the cast with a name you choose, a bot you already have, a bot from another service you connect now, a new bot you make (a job, a name and a look), or an imported bot. Import takes a team file (.json) or a bot package (.zip) exported from Allternit, from Files or a link. Bots from a team file come in for chat, without the file's rooms, routines or playbooks. Before a package comes in, the app shows its name, description and what it holds (memory, skills, tasks, docs and so on). Its files are copied to the bot's folder on your computer, so a computer must be connected; any file that can't be copied is listed after the import. A package can hold up to 50 MB of files. If you already have a main bot, this step is skipped. A bot from another service (Claude, Duet, ChatGPT, Grok or Meta AI) connects right there: pick how to connect, read what it can do and the terms, and tap connect. **Make it my main bot** is on unless you turn it off.
3. Turn each bot's routines on or off, and tap one to set when it runs: every day, weekdays or chosen days, at a time. You can also add your own routine. The calendar above shows them by day, week or month: tap an empty time to add a routine there, or drag one to move it in 15-minute steps.
4. Allow notifications, so you hear when a bot needs you. You can change this later in **Settings → Notifications**.
5. Pick a first job and edit it.

Nothing is created until you tap **Start the job**. The last step shows what was made, with the reason for anything that failed, and **Retry what didn't finish** tries only those again. The first job is sent to your main bot's chat. If your computer is offline, you can still pick the team and finish later.

## Getting around

The app looks and works like m.allternit.com: the same white (or graphite, in dark mode) screens, header, drawer and composer.

The header shows ☰ on the left, the screen's title, and on the right the number of open chats and the new-chat button. Tap the number for **all chats at a glance**, as a list, a grid or a deck. Each card shows **Working** while its reply streams and **New reply** when something arrived since you last looked. Tap ⋯ on a card to open, rename or archive it, tap **X** to archive it, or swipe a deck card up.

Tap ☰ (or swipe from the left edge) to open the drawer. Press and hold ☰ to see your bots and open one. The drawer has:

- the **A://** button, which switches between **Home**, **Bots**, **Code** and **ACI**, the same modes as the phone web app,
- **Inbox** (the tray button at the top): everything your bots are waiting on you for, such as approvals, held messages, missed calls and failed runs. Tap the button on a row, or swipe right for it; swipe left to snooze it for an hour or dismiss it,
- **Search**,
- the mode's own rows. In Home these are **Projects**, **Artifacts**, **Automation Tasks**, **Customize** and **More**. In ACI they are **MCP Apps**, **ACI Extensions**, **Computers** and **Teach**. **More** has **Show in rail** switches to hide or show rows; a dot on **More** means something is hidden,
- **Teammates** (in Home): the bots that are working or need you right now. Tap one to open its chat,
- **Recents**, with **See all** and a filter for type, status (All, Active, Completed, Archived) and last activity. A chat shows **Working** while it answers and a dot when it has something new. Swipe left on a chat to delete it, swipe right to pin it, and press and drag a pinned chat to move it,
- **New chat** at the bottom. Press and hold it in Home or Code and pick **Continue CLI session** to bring a Claude Code, Codex, Grok or other CLI session from your computer into Allternit. The app copies it and leaves the original as it was,
- **Phones** (the phone button at the bottom, with a count of unread messages): your bots' phones. Open one to see its number, email and conversations, unread first. **Give a bot a phone** connects one (see [Connect a bot](#connect-a-bot)),
- your account, which opens **Settings**.

### The composer

Home's composer is the same as the web's: **+** to add files, the **Auto** pill for how freely the chat works (Auto, Ask before actions, Read-only), the model, and voice. Under it, **Agent mode**, **Plugins** and **Artifacts** open their decks. On a new chat, the **Docs**, **Slides**, **Sites** and **Sheets** tabs below it list templates to start from. The Bots home uses the same composer, with a **To:** row to send to your main bot as a new project or to one bot.

### Bots and their colours

On the Bots home, **Your team** shows each bot in the same round tile, with a ring while it works and an amber dot when it needs you. Your main bot, A://, shows as the Allternit orb, and its name is drawn as the pixel A:// mark wherever it appears. When you pick one bot in **To:**, or open a bot's chat, the screen and the drawer take on that bot's colour, and your messages in its chat do too. The colour is the one set for the bot, or else the main colour of its picture. Bots with a black-and-white picture keep the plain screen. In a bot's own chat, its picture stands in for Gizzi over the greeting.

### Code mode

The Code header has ☰ on the left and the sessions button (a task list) on the right. On the Code home, the gizzi code logo sits between them and **What's new** sits under the sessions button. In a session, the session's name takes the middle: tap it to rename the session, copy its ID or archive it.

The sessions button opens your code sessions. Tap one to open it. **Terminal** and **Shell** at the top right of that sheet open a thread in its terminal look and a plain command shell on your computer. To leave the shell, open the sessions sheet again and pick a session.

The Code composer shows its controls as icons so all of them fit:

- **Above the `❯` line:** the computer it runs on, on the left. On the right is the context meter: five blocks and a percent that show how full the session's context window is. Tap the meter to see what fills the window (messages, system prompt, tools, MCP tools, memory files and skills) and your plan's limits. Before a session has its first reply, the meter shows your plan's usage instead. It is hidden when there is no figure yet.
- **Under the `❯` line:** the working folder, the branch, the worktree switch (on the main branch only) and the permission mode on the left. The model and the **Chat · Terminal** switch are on the right.

**+** adds files. The **Chat · Terminal** switch is the one way to change how a thread looks. **Chat** shows it as messages. **Terminal** shows the same thread as a terminal: a dark screen, your messages as `❯` lines, each tool step as `●` with its result under it, and a **Working…** line with a timer while the agent runs. The app remembers the choice for each thread.

In a session, the **Panes** button beside the sessions button lists the same panes as Allternit Desktop. Each opens as a sheet:

| Pane | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Session panel | The agent's plan as a checklist (Progress); the computer, folder, branch and permission mode; the context window; the session's pull requests; what it made (artifacts, published pages and files it wrote); how many background tasks run; the agent tree its folder sits in, with running processes and checkpoints; and the connectors the session loaded |
| Terminal | The computer's shell |
| Changes | The files the session changed, with their diffs |
| Browser | The dev server running in the terminal |
| Computer | Your computers, to watch or drive one |
| Files | The project's files |
| Artifacts | Your artifacts library |
| Transcript | Every message in the session, as plain text |
| Background tasks | The shells and subagents the session started, with their output. **Stop** ends a running one |
| Code map | The project's most important files, with how many files import each one |

## Your computer

Code, Automation and a bot's computer run on a computer paired to your account: Allternit Desktop on your Mac or PC, or a cloud computer. The app reaches it through Allternit's cloud, the same way m.allternit.com does, so the phone doesn't need to be on the same network.

If you don't have a computer paired yet, those screens show **Connect a computer**. Pair one in Allternit Desktop, or open **Settings → Devices** to see what is paired.

## Let your bots use this iPhone

The app can also let your bots use your iPhone in two ways:

- **See the screen.** When you tap **Share screen**, the app streams the iPhone's screen to your Allternit Desktop, from anywhere. You can watch it in Allternit on your computer, on ai.allternit.com and in the m.allternit.com phone app, and your bot can take screenshots of it.
- **Use the app's browser.** While the Allternit app is open, your bot can open web pages in the app's own browser, read them, click, type, scroll and go back.

You don't need a cable, a jailbreak, an Apple developer account or Developer Mode. The iPhone only connects out to Allternit, so it works on any network.

### What works and what doesn't

| | Without the Bluetooth board | With the Bluetooth board |
|---|---|---|
| Seeing the screen while you share it | Yes | Yes |
| Your bot using the Allternit app's browser | Yes | Yes |
| Taps, swipes and typing in other apps | No | Coming with the Bluetooth board |
| Opening other apps, Home, Back | No | Coming with the Bluetooth board |

In Allternit these iPhones are marked **Screen only**. If a bot tries to tap one, it gets the answer "Taps on native apps need the Bluetooth board". To let a bot tap and type on an iPhone today, use the [Allternit USB Agent](/guides/usb-agent).

### Pair the iPhone

You need Allternit Desktop running on your computer, signed in to the same account as the app.

1. Install **Allternit** from the App Store and sign in.
2. In the app, open **Settings → Computers → Let your bots use this iPhone**, and pick your computer.
3. The app shows a 6-digit code. Allternit Desktop shows a card with the iPhone's name and the same code.
4. Check that the two codes match, then choose **Approve** on Desktop. Choose **Deny** if you didn't start this or the codes differ.

The iPhone now shows in **Settings → Computers → Phones** and in the rail's phone list. You can see the steps there too, under **Connect with the Allternit iPhone app**.

### Share the screen

Tap **Share screen** in the Allternit app, then **Start Broadcast**. iOS asks each time; only you can start it. While it's on, the app shows "Your bot can see this screen" with a **Stop** button, and iOS shows its own recording indicator.

When the iPhone isn't sharing, its row says "Open the Allternit app on your iPhone and tap Share screen". A bot can ask you to start sharing: the app then shows the Share screen sheet, and you decide.

The iPhone only sends video while someone is watching. When nobody is, the broadcast pauses.

### What your bot can do

Your bot uses the same phone tools it uses for every phone. These iPhones have the serial `app:` followed by an id, and `phone.devices` lists them with the route `iphone-app-store`.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `phone.screenshot` | The latest picture of the shared screen. |
| `phone.screen.request` | Asks you to share the screen (shows the Share screen sheet in the app). |
| `phone.web.open` | Opens a page in the Allternit app's browser. |
| `phone.web.snapshot` | The page's address, title, a picture and its elements, each with a ref. |
| `phone.web.click`, `phone.web.type` | Click or type into an element by its ref from the latest snapshot. |
| `phone.web.scroll`, `phone.web.back`, `phone.web.wait` | Scroll, go back, or wait for the page to settle. |

The browser tools work only while the Allternit app is open on the iPhone. Otherwise the bot is told to ask you to open it. The app shows "Bot is using the browser" with a **Stop** button while a bot works there; Stop ends that bot's turn. Element refs are only good for the snapshot that gave them, and the bot can't run its own scripts in the page.

Taps, swipes, `phone.open_app`, `phone.home`, plain-English `phone.task` runs, texts and calls are not available on these iPhones.

### Privacy

- The screen goes from the iPhone to your own Desktop through Allternit's relay, sealed with a key only the iPhone and your Desktop hold. Allternit doesn't store it.
- Each iPhone gets its own key. Remove the iPhone in the app, or in Allternit, to revoke it.
- Nothing is shared until you tap **Start Broadcast**, and **Stop** ends it at once.

## Bots

Bots mode lists your bots with their status. In the drawer, the **Bots** row shows how many threads are waiting on you. Open a bot for its threads, tasks, artifacts, routines, memory and channels. A bot's **Routines** has an **On a schedule** section where you can change when each one runs. Group chats and the live stage are there too. A group chat can have up to 6 bots, including bots that run on another service, such as Grok or Poke; those show **Runs on …** and answer through their own connection. If one of them needs approval or doesn't answer, the room says so. If a bot has a computer, you can watch it and take control from the phone. If that computer's screen sharing has a password, the app asks for it. On a Mac that is the name and password of an account on the Mac, not your Allternit sign-in. The app keeps it only while the screen is open.

## Connect a bot

**Connect** on a bot's page (also **Give a bot a phone** in Phones, and the bot's Phone and Channels screens) gives it a way to reach people:

- **Phone number:** search by area code, local or toll-free, and buy one. Or choose **Port my existing number** to move a number you already have: enter it with the country code and the app follows the carrier's progress, which usually takes a few business days. Your number keeps working until the port completes. Then register it for texting as a business or as an individual; the individual path texts you a code. Carrier review shows its status as it goes.
- **Email:** the bot gets an address. You can give it a different name (the old address stops working), choose who can email it, how it replies and who it may answer. **Company domain** adds an address like support@yourcompany.com: the app shows the four DNS records to add (press and hold to copy) and checks them live, then moves the bot to the domain once they verify. You can skip the check and come back later.
- **Telegram:** name the bot, tap **Confirm** in Telegram, then **Start**. If your computer can't do that, paste a bot token instead.
- **Slack, Discord, Teams and WhatsApp Business:** the sign-in page opens in the app. When you close it, the app waits for the connection. Discord then lets you pick channels.

**Bots from other services** (Connect a vendor bot): accept the terms, then sign in on the window that opens on your computer. The phone checks for up to about 5 minutes. Some products ask for API keys. You can make the connected bot your main bot.

Not on the phone: WhatsApp personal. Use Desktop or ai.allternit.com for it.

## Artifacts

**Artifacts** in the drawer is your library: the things you and your bots made, filtered by kind and by mine or shared. Artifacts made in a chat are saved to it. Every kind opens in the app. Documents, sheets, slides and PDFs open natively. Dashboards, ops sheets, motion pieces, cards, canvas designs and pages that use stored data, AI or connectors open in the same viewer the web uses, signed in, inside the app. AI those pages run is billed to you, as on the web. Links that leave Allternit open in Safari.

Tap **Edit** on a document, sheet or deck you can edit:

- **Documents:** edit paragraphs, headings, list items and the text in table cells that hold one paragraph. Images show in place with their description, which you can edit; to swap the picture itself, paste a new image URL on Desktop or the web. Dividers and cells with more than one paragraph stay locked. Markdown documents edit as plain text. Tabs show as pills along the top; a dot marks a tab you changed, and **Save** keeps all of them. **Add tab** (or **+ Tab**) adds one. Tap the open tab to rename it, and tap **×** next to it to delete it. A document always keeps at least one tab.
- **Sheets:** edit any cell (start with `=` for a formula), and add rows and columns.
- **Decks:** edit the text on each slide.

**Save** makes a new version that every device sees. If someone else saved first, the app tells you and offers **Get the latest**.

Exports and downloads from the viewer, such as MP4, CSV, XLSX, DOCX and PNG, open the share sheet so you can save them to Files or Photos. **PDF** opens the print sheet, which can save a PDF. PDFs, images and videos have a **Save or share** button. Files over 512 MB can't be saved on the phone.

## Calls

A bot can call you. While the app is open, the call rings full screen with the caller's name, **Answer** and **Decline**. The ring respects the silent switch, but the phone still vibrates. Tapping a ring notification opens the same screen. If the call has already ended, the notification opens that conversation instead.

On a call you have a timer, **Mute**, **Speaker** (the earpiece is used until you turn it on) and **End**. The call keeps going if you leave the app. A call you didn't answer shows **Missed call**, with **Open conversation**. A call answered on another device stops ringing.

The app needs microphone access to answer. If it's off, the app says so and offers **Open Settings**. When the app is closed, a call arrives as a notification, not the full iPhone call screen. To have calls vibrate without ringing, turn off **Settings → Notifications → Incoming calls → Ring sound** (this iPhone only).

You can call people who joined through a bot's invite: tap the phone button in the bot's chat or **Call** on its page, then pick the person. The call screen shows **Calling…** with **Speaker** and **Cancel**, and you hear a ringing tone until they pick up. If the person has a bot that answers for them, it connects at once. Otherwise the call ends with **No answer**, **Declined** or **Busy**.

## Settings

Settings opens from your account in the drawer. Its groups and order are the same as on m.allternit.com, and the card at the top shows your name and email.

Most Settings screens work in the app:

- **Notifications:** this iPhone's switches (response completions, approval requests, incoming calls and sound, plus the iOS permission with **Send a test**), then your account's phone notifications per event, critical alerts by text (after a 6-digit code check) and quiet hours. Account changes apply to every device.
- **General → Chat font:** Default, Serif or System, for your bots' replies.
- **Customize → Discover:** the Allternit skill directory (From Allternit, For you, More skills and Categories). **Add** installs a skill on your computer.
- **Skills and Plugins:** what's installed on your computer, with a switch for each and **Remove from this computer**. In **Skills**, **+** writes a new skill: a name, when to use it, and its instructions, saved to `~/.allternit/skills/` on your computer. Uploading a skill or plugin package and the plugin marketplace need Desktop or the web.
- **Subagents:** the built-in types and your own. Create one, edit its name, when to use it, model, step limit, tools, instructions and face, reset a built-in or delete your own.
- **Your twin:** edit how your twin speaks and what bots may say for you. Accept, edit or dismiss what bots suggest, and add, edit or delete memories.
- **MCP servers** (also in Customize): the servers on your computer, each with its status (Connected, Not connected, Failed, Needs sign-in or Off) and a switch. Add or edit one (name, description, command, arguments one per line, tags), see its tools, connect or disconnect it now, or remove it. Servers that come from another app's settings, such as Claude, Codex or Gemini, are read-only here; the screen says where to change them.
- **Memory Drive:** pick a drive, then read, edit and save files. If a file changed meanwhile, the app offers to reload it. **History** lists a file's versions: see what changed, compare with the current file, or **Restore this version**, which saves it as a new version so nothing is lost. **Keep a copy** copies the drive to a folder on your computer or to your own git repository; the access token is stored sealed and never shown again. **Delete memories forever** asks you to type *delete forever*. Imports stay on Desktop and the web.

## Notifications and links

Notifications from your bots, threads and calls arrive as iPhone notifications. Tapping one opens the chat, thread or bot it is about. An approval opens **Decisions**, where you can answer it. Your [notification settings and quiet hours](/guides/notifications) apply to the iPhone too.

Links to ai.allternit.com and m.allternit.com open in the app when it is installed: a bot (`/b/…`), an invite (`/i/…`), Settings (`/settings`) or a chat (`?session=…`).

## Plans and credits

In the United States, **Settings → Billing** links to the web to buy credits or change your plan. Elsewhere, manage your plan on [ai.allternit.com](https://ai.allternit.com). The app shows your plan, credits and usage in every country.

## Delete your account

Go to **Settings → Account → Delete account**, read what happens, type `DELETE` and tap **Delete my account**.

- You are signed out on every device and can't sign back in to that account.
- Deleting your account doesn't cancel a paid plan. Cancel it in Billing on the web first so you aren't charged again.
- Credits left on the account are not refunded.
- To have stored chats, bots, memory and files erased right away, email privacy@allternit.com.

Want a copy first? Export your data from **Settings → Privacy → Export data** before you delete. You can also delete your account on the web: see [Delete your account](/guides/delete-account).

## For developers

The app is SwiftUI in the platform repo at `surfaces/allternit-mobile/ios` (bundle id `com.allternit.mobile`, project generated by XcodeGen from `project.yml`). It talks to cloud-api at `api.allternit.com`. Calls that belong to a computer go through the runtime relay (`POST /api/v1/runtime-devices/:id/proxy`, `Core/API/RuntimeRelayClient.swift`). Push is described in [iPhone push (APNs)](/api/ios-push). Call audio uses the LiveKit Swift SDK (`Core/Calls/CallAudio.swift`) with the `{token, url, room}` cloud-api returns from `/api/v1/phone/…/answer`. Web-rendered artifacts load `ai.allternit.com/a/<id>` in a WKWebView that receives the record and the Clerk token from the app (`Features/Artifacts/Views/ArtifactLiveWebView.swift`). Meet your bots uses the web's template catalog, generated into `Features/Onboarding/MeetBotCatalogData.swift` by `Scripts/meet-bot-catalog/`. Universal Links come from `public/.well-known/apple-app-site-association` in allternit-ai and are parsed by `Core/DeepLinkRouter.swift`.

The phone node routes, the pairing handshake and the viewer stream format are in the [Phone Stream Relay API](/api/phone-stream-relay#iphones-with-the-allternit-app).
