---
title: Messaging channels for your bots
description: Connect Telegram, Email, a phone number, Discord, Slack, Teams or WhatsApp once, switch it on per bot, and use @mentions to pick who answers.
---

Connect a messaging channel once and your bots can answer there. A connection belongs to you; each bot has its own on/off switch for it.

Everything on this page works on **Allternit Desktop**, on **ai.allternit.com**, and on the **m.allternit.com** phone app. On a phone the connect wizards open as full-height sheets; the one exception is [connecting an Android phone](/guides/phone-for-your-bot), which is Desktop only.

## Connect a channel

Open **Customize → Connectors → Messaging**, or open a bot's **Agent Gateway → Messaging** and use **Connect…**. A connection made from a bot's settings is switched on for that bot straight away. One made from Customize is not: open the bot and switch it on.

Each channel has a short wizard: intro, one action (usually the platform's own authorize page), live status while it connects, and a **Done** step with **Send test message**. You never paste a secret unless the channel needs one, and you can cancel at any step.

| Channel | What you do | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Telegram** | Name the bot, tap **Confirm** in Telegram once. Until Allternit's managed Telegram bot is switched on, the wizard asks for a BotFather token instead. | Available (token) |
| **Email** | Your bot gets its own address named after it (for example `researcher@bots.allternit.com`) that is live as soon as the wizard finishes. To pick a different name, use **Change address** on the wizard's address step, or type a new name in the bot's Identity settings and click **Change address**. The old address stops working once you change it. | Available |
| **Phone number** | Buy a local or toll-free number (paid plans), or port yours. Calls work immediately; texting follows carrier registration. See [Calls and texts in threads](/guides/calls-and-texts). | Available |
| **Discord** | Add the Allternit app to your server, then choose which channels the bots may use. | Coming soon |
| **Slack** | Install the Allternit app in your workspace. | Coming soon |
| **Microsoft Teams** | Sign in with Microsoft, then the wizard records Teams on your computer so you can switch bots on right away. If your organisation requires admin approval for apps, the wizard tells you what to ask your admin for. | Coming soon |
| **WhatsApp** | A WhatsApp Business number you own, through Meta's signup page (sign in, pick your business, verify the number by SMS). Messages go to the computer you connected it from. Personal numbers ("Linked devices") aren't offered yet. | Coming soon |

"Coming soon" means Allternit's own app for that platform (Discord, Slack, Teams, WhatsApp) isn't switched on yet; each needs a one-time setup with that platform, and some need the platform's review. The wizard checks live (`GET /api/v1/channels/availability`) and shows a disabled **Coming soon** panel instead of an error, and turns into a working wizard as soon as the app is on.

## Discord channels

After the app joins your server, the wizard lists the server's text channels, all ticked. Untick the ones the bots should stay out of and save. Messages in unticked channels are dropped by Allternit's cloud and never reach your computer; threads follow their parent channel, and direct messages and slash commands always work. Keeping every channel ticked means no limit, so channels you add later work too. To change the list, run **Connect Discord** again.

## Switch it on per bot

Open a bot, then **Agent Gateway → Messaging**. Each connection has a switch.

- **On** means this bot answers on that connection.
- **Answers new chats** marks the one bot that takes every new conversation. Other bots on the same connection answer only when named.
- **Off** with "Used by 2 other bots" tells you who else is using it.

Several bots can share one connection.

## @mentions

In a chat on a shared connection, start or include `@name` and that bot answers, for example `@engineer can you check the build?`. Without a mention, the bot marked **Answers new chats** answers.

### The "Turn on" card

If you mention a bot that is not switched on for that connection, it cannot answer. The thread shows a card: "*engineer* was mentioned but isn't switched on for this Telegram chat" with a **Turn on engineer** button. Tap it, then mention the bot again. If it fails, the card tells you where to switch it on by hand (bot → Agent Gateway → Messaging).

## Email replies

Email wizards include a reply setting for each bot:

| Mode | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Approve** | The bot drafts and you approve every reply. |
| **Known senders** | Replies send automatically to senders on your allowlist; everyone else waits for approval. |
| **Auto** | The bot replies without approval. |


## Limits

- Replies to regulated or consequential messages should be checked by a person; set Email to **Approve** while you are testing.
- Allternit cannot guarantee delivery. Each platform decides what it delivers.
