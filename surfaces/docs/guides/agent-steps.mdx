## Watching an agent work

While an agent works, the reply shows its plan in one grey line, then a header such as "[app logos] Using 3 tools · 14s". Under the header, on a thin line at the left, are the tool steps: up to six, with older ones folded into "+N earlier steps" (click it to see every step and thought). There is no box around them.

- **Each step** shows its icon, what it did ("Searched Slack for messages since yesterday"), what it found and how long it took ("4 results · 1.2s"), then a check. The step running now has a moving label instead.
- **A failed step** is red. The reason shows under it without opening it, and the header adds "· 1 failed". On the latest finished reply, the row has **Retry**, which runs the turn again (the same as the reply's Retry).
- **A step that needs your approval** gets a blue outline, says what it will do (for example "Sends as Raven to 48 people in #release") and has **Deny**, **Always** (or **For this task**, when the request allows it) and **Allow**. The header changes to "Waiting for approval". The approval no longer shows a second time as a card or above the composer. An approval that no step can show, such as a send to your subscription, keeps its card.
- **Click the header** to fold the steps away and again to show them.

If the agent only thinks, there is no header: the plan and the latest thought show, with a timer.

Some things stay in view as chips the whole time:

- **Sources:** the websites it searched, for example "Searched 9 websites" with the sites' icons. Click to list them.
- **Files** it made. Click a file to open it, or its arrow to download it.
- **Failures:** "1 step failed". Click to see which.

When it finishes, the steps fold into one line, such as "[app logos] Used 4 tools · 12s · 1 failed" (or "Thought for 12s" if it only thought). A reply from before step timing was recorded reads what it did instead, for example "Searched the web, read 3 pages". The chips stay under it. Click the line to see the full trail: the plan, every step on a tree line with its icon and what it did, and each search's sources. Click any step to see what went in and what came back.

The agent's task list is no longer drawn in the reply. It lives in [Progress](/guides/progress-pane), in the right pane (on a phone, a sheet), opened from the chip at the top right of the conversation. Interactive questions still show as cards. Approvals show on the step they gate (see above); see [Composer, replies and questions](/guides/chat-composer#answering-an-agent-question).

Under the last reply, a reply that used tools has **Run again** in words next to the other actions; it runs the turn again, tools and all. Other replies keep the plain retry icon.

This works the same in Chat, Code, bot threads and project threads, on Desktop, ai.allternit.com (including the phone layout) and the m.allternit.com app. In Memory Drive, a Dream's **Steps** opens straight into the trail; see [Nightly Dream](/guides/memory-drive#nightly-dream).

## Steps in your connected apps

A step that reaches one of your connected apps (Settings → Connectors), such as Slack, Gmail, GitHub, Linear or Granola, shows the app's logo and name: "Searched **Slack** for messages since yesterday", "Read **GitHub** pull requests".

- **What it found.** When the step finishes, it shows the first three things it found, such as messages, emails, pull requests, issues or notes. Each row shows who or what it is, one line of text and when, with "+N more" for the rest. A row with a link opens the item in its app.
- **While the assistant works,** the newest step shows what it found. When the reply is done, the steps fold back into the summary line, which ends with the logos of the apps used.
- **Full results.** When the reply is done, the full result appears as a table or card under the steps, as before. Click a closed step to see what it found along with its raw input and result.
- A step whose result is plain text, or not a list of things, shows no preview. Click it to see the result.

## The task list

With Codex, every search, page it opens, command, file change and connector call is a step, the same as with Claude Code. ACP agents (Kimi, Grok, Kiro and others) and OpenCode show their steps the same way. CLIs that only return a final answer (Pi, OMP, Antigravity) have no live steps. The short notes Codex writes between steps ("I'll check each product's pricing…") show as the plan line and notes in the steps, not in the answer. Any agent's short notes before or between its steps fold into the steps the same way; the answer stays below them.

The agent's task list (Claude's TodoWrite, TaskCreate and TaskUpdate, gizzi's todowrite, Codex's plan or an ACP agent's plan) shows as one checklist in [Progress](/guides/progress-pane), not in the reply. Each update changes the same list there, and the chip at the top right reads "Step 2 of 3" while it works and "All 3 done" when finished.

## Web searches and their sources

A web search step shows the icons of the sites it found and the number of results, for example "Searched the web feature flags rollout · 7 results". Click it to see the pages: each row shows the site's icon, the page title and the site. Click a row to open the page. The first six show, with "+N more" for the rest. On a phone the site name is hidden to leave room for the title.

A search whose result has no links still opens to its raw result.

## Source pills and chips in the answer

Web pages the agent cites show as small pills with the site's icon and name, like "github", and several in a row become one pill, "github +2". The pill comes from the model's own citation, not from guessing at the reply text; see [How long answers are laid out](/guides/interactive-answers#how-long-answers-are-laid-out) and, for developers, [Response parts](/guides/response-parts).

When the answer links to something in one of your apps, such as a Slack message, a GitHub commit, a Linear issue, a Gmail thread, a Notion page or a Google Drive file, the link shows as a small chip with the app's logo. You can see at a glance where each point came from. Clicking a chip asks before it opens the link, the same as any other link in a reply.

## When the agent makes an image or a video

While the agent is making an image or a video, the chat shows a frame the same shape as the picture you asked for. If you asked for a size, the frame matches it. If you didn't, it's square for an image and 16:9 for a video. Small squares pulse out from the middle of the frame. The foot of the frame says **Generating image** or **Rendering video**, with a timer showing how long it has been working. Under the frame you can see what the agent is doing right now.

When the image or video is ready, it appears in the chat itself and comes into focus. It no longer shows as a file row. Under it are three buttons:

- **Download** saves the image or video.
- **Open** shows it in the artifact panel.
- **Copy prompt** copies what you asked for, so you can reuse or change it. It only shows when the request had a prompt.

You can also click an image to open it. A video plays in place with its own controls. The chat's **Artifacts** pane lists every generated picture in a gallery; see [The chat's Artifacts pane](/guides/artifacts#the-chats-artifacts-pane).

If your device is set to reduce motion, the frame shows still squares and the image appears without the fade.
