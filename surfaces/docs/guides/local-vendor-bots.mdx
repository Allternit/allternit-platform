These 16 projects connect through each vendor's own authenticated API or supported inbox. Most run on your connected Sessions computer; Rakazo and FrockBot connect to a server origin you configure. They connect to the vendor's existing agents, rather than selecting a model from Brains. Each adapter follows the vendor's source at a pinned commit, noted in its README. OpenMausBot, Codync, Hivekeep, Invisible Dots and CopilotKit OpenDots have source-based transport tests; the others have static checks only. Live vendor-account verification is pending for all of them.

## Before connecting

Run the vendor app on the same computer as the subscription gateway. In Settings → Connections, choose the vendor, review permissions, and enter its own credential. Credentials are stored encrypted. Never paste credentials into a chat or support report.

On Desktop, the website phone layout, and the PWA, the same connection flow uses your connected computer. A cloud-only runtime cannot reach an app on your laptop's loopback address. If the adapter is unavailable on your chosen runtime, finish Sessions setup first.

## Use your bot

After the credential check succeeds, pick an agent returned by that vendor instance. Allternit retains its vendor identity and uses the normal bot chat flow. Replies are matched to the accepted vendor request or turn. An accepted message alone is never shown as a completed response.

OpenMausBot and Codync can forward explicit human approval decisions. Hivekeep's external API does not expose approval controls. Invisible Dots approvals are not forwarded by this adapter because a conversation message cannot safely claim ownership of a task approval; review them in the vendor app. Closing an Allternit connection does not stop a shared vendor bot.

## When a request fails

An uncertain send or a wait timeout can mean the vendor received your message. Check its conversation before sending a new message. Retrying the same send ID within the running gateway reuses the prior result. Gateway restarts do not restore these in-memory send records or contexts.

Authentication failures require reconnecting with the correct credential or restoring its scope. Schema changes fail explicitly instead of interpreting an unrelated reply as your bot's answer.

## Projects without a connector

The project catalog covers the 33 projects from the supplied tier list. Grok Bot, OpenAI Dots and Muse connect through their existing Grok, ChatGPT and Meta vendor entries. The rest of the catalog shows **No supported connection** with a one-line reason, and those cards cannot be selected as connected accounts:

- **Hosted apps with no outside control API:** Cue, Noodle, Pally, Instinct, Slashy, Iris, Poke, Flip and Gemini Spark. Use them in their own apps.
- **Sign-in or keys we cannot use:** CopilotKit OpenBot (web session sign-in only), AbacusAI Bot (desktop-internal controls), Happy (end-to-end encrypted to your own devices).
- **No reachable interface:** Omnibots. The Mac app pairs with your iPhone through an encrypted relay with per-device credentials, and no documented local or account API exists.
- **Cadu** is a phone client for Hermes. Connect your Hermes agent instead.

A catalog card and logo identify a project; they are not a claim of partnership. If a vendor ships a supported API later, its card can move to a connector.
