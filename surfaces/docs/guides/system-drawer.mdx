description: "A developer tool: see what needs you, your bots, what is running and what broke across every session, and approve requests without opening each one."

The system drawer is a developer tool. It shows what is happening across the whole app: every session, every mode, this computer and the cloud. It is off unless you turn it on, so most people never see it.

## Turn it on

Go to **Settings → Developer → System drawer** and turn it on. Turning it off closes the drawer and removes every way into it.

The drawer never opens by itself. You open it.

## Open it

| Where | How |
| --- | --- |
| Any mode | **⌘J** (Ctrl+J on Windows and Linux) opens and closes it. |
| Gizzi, on Desktop | When something needs you or broke, a small dot appears on the Gizzi desktop pet: amber for needs you, red for problems. Hover the dot to see a short summary above Gizzi: the counts, the first waiting item, and how to open the drawer, one line each. Click the dot to open the drawer on that tab. Clicking Gizzi itself still opens quick chat. |
| Menu bar, on Desktop | Right-click the A:// icon. The menu starts with a **System** section that lists what is waiting, and **Open System Drawer**. Pick an item to open the drawer on it. A left-click still opens the HUD. |
| Phone and PWA | The round pulse button in the menu's footer, next to Phones. Its badge shows what needs you, or else what broke. The drawer opens as a sheet; tap outside it to close. |

On Desktop the drawer sits along the bottom, beside the rail. Drag its tab row to make it taller or shorter.

## Tabs

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs you** | Every approval and question waiting on you, from any chat, code session, bot or the Factory. Each shows where it came from and how long it has waited, with **Approve** and **Deny**. Agent requests also have **Open session**. |
| **Bots** | Every bot, one line each: its face, its name, what it is doing or why it stopped, and **Open**. **Lanes** shows the Terminal bots' [hand-offs](/factory/agents#hand-off-lanes): a card per bot and a lane per team link holding the newest campaign's nodes. **Graph** draws who works on what: a Factory campaign's steps and the bot on each, or, with no campaign, the Terminal bots as a team. |
| **Workspace** | The [Office](/guides/office-view) itself, laid out as on a phone: the office loft in 3D with one panel across the bottom. The panel lists your bots (bots that need you first); tap one and the camera flies to it and the panel becomes its chat, with its question and **Approve** at the top when it is waiting on you. The floor is drawn by 3dfacility.allternit.com, so it needs an internet connection; if it does not load in 25 seconds it says so, with **Try again** and **Open in a tab**. |
| **Running** | Sessions that are replying right now, and dev servers on this computer, each with **Open** and **Stop**. |
| **Problems** | What broke: routines that keep failing, Terminal bots that stopped with an error, and failed typechecks, lint, tests or builds from the code sessions active in the last day. A row with a file opens it at that line. |

On a phone the same four tabs open in a sheet, and the graph draws as an indented list.

## For developers

- **Setting:** `useDevToolsStore` (`src/lib/dev-tools.store.ts`, stored as `allternit-dev-tools`) holds `systemDrawer`, off by default. `ConsoleDrawer` renders nothing while it is off. ⌘J and the phone button exist only while it is on.
- **Mount:** `ConsoleDrawer` (`src/drawers/ConsoleDrawer.tsx`) wraps `DrawerRoot`. `ShellApp` mounts it in every mode, and `PhoneShell` mounts it lazily inside `.phone-root`. Open state, height and tab live in `useDrawerStore`.
- **Tabs:** the console registry holds them. The built-ins are `needs-you`, `bots`, `running` and `problems` (`builtin-tabs.tsx`). An unknown saved id falls back to the first tab.
- **Data:** `system-model.ts`. `useNeedsYou` merges `usePermissionStore` requests with Factory approvals. `useSystemCodeSessions` derives problems and ports for code sessions that are streaming or updated in the last 24 hours. Bots read `useAgentStore` with `useBotStatus`, plus Factory Terminal bots.
- **Workspace:** `WorkspaceTab.tsx` frames `https://3dfacility.allternit.com/?embed=workspace` (`&theme=dark` in dark mode). After the frame posts `allternit:workspace:ready`, the tab posts `{ type: "allternit:workspace", bots }` to that origin, and posts again when the roster changes. Each bot has `id`, `name`, `role`, `color`, `status` (from `useRosterLive`), `task`, `question`, `chief` and `avatar` (`sheet` is the pet sprite sheet as an absolute `https://ai.allternit.com/avatar-packs/…` URL, or an image avatar with `layout: "still"`). The frame's `allternit:workspace:action` (`answer` or `open`) opens that bot's chat; messages from any other origin or window are ignored. `public/_headers` allows the frame (`frame-src`) and lets the facility load `/avatar-packs/*` as WebGL textures (`Access-Control-Allow-Origin`).
- **Desktop bridge:** the app window sends a summary with `allternitCompanion.system.report` (`companion:system-report`): counts and up to five waiting items. The main process checks it (`system-summary.ts`) and passes it to the pet (`companion:system`) and the menu bar menu. The pet's dot calls `system.hover` (bubble on and off) and `system.open`. The main process then shows the window and sends `companion:system-open` with the tab.
- **Placement:** `ShellFrame` writes the rail column width to `--shell-rail-col` on the document root, and the drawer uses it as its left inset.
