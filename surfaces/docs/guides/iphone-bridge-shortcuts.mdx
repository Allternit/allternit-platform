---
title: iPhone shortcuts for your assistant
description: Add the Allternit Actions shortcut and your own app shortcuts so your assistant can do things on your iPhone through Apple's Shortcuts app.
---

Apple's Shortcuts app is the supported way for an app to ask your iPhone to do things. Your assistant uses it for the Shortcuts and Home route. See [How your assistant uses your iPhone](/guides/iphone-routes) for the other routes.

You add each shortcut yourself. Allternit cannot install a shortcut for you, and it cannot run one you have not added.

## Where to add them

1. Open the Allternit app on your iPhone.
2. Go to **Settings → Device Access → iPhone actions**.
3. Turn on **Shortcuts**.
4. Tap **Add the Allternit Actions shortcut** and then **Add Shortcut** in Apple's Shortcuts app.

If a shortcut is missing, your assistant says so with this message: "Add the Allternit Actions shortcut in Allternit Settings > Device Access > iPhone actions."

## The Allternit Actions shortcut

**Allternit Actions** is one shortcut that does many small jobs. It uses only built-in Apple actions. Your assistant sends it a short request that names the action, such as setting a timer or turning on a Focus, and the shortcut runs that action and returns the result.

Because it is one shortcut, you add it once. When Allternit adds new actions, they work through the same shortcut, so you do not add more.

## Your own shortcuts

Your assistant can also run any shortcut that is already in your Shortcuts app. You choose which ones it may see.

1. Make the shortcut in Apple's Shortcuts app.
2. In the Allternit app, open **Settings → Device Access → iPhone actions** and pick the shortcut from the list.
3. Give it a plain name, such as "Good night". Your assistant runs it by that name.

## Per-app shortcuts

Many apps add their own shortcuts to Apple's Shortcuts app. Add the ones you want, then pick them in **iPhone actions** the same way. Your assistant can then use them by name.

## Safety

- Every shortcut that sends something, buys something or changes security is approved by you on your computer before it runs.
- A shortcut runs only while the Allternit app can reach your computer. If the app is not open, your assistant asks you to open it.
- Turn **Shortcuts** off in **Settings → Device Access** at any time. Your assistant cannot run anything after that.
- Shortcuts only return what you build into them. A shortcut that returns nothing shows no result, and your assistant says so.
