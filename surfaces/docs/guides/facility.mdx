The facility is a walkable introduction to Allternit. Open [3dfacility.allternit.com](https://3dfacility.allternit.com) and choose a character before your first walk.

## Choose a character

Search the starting looks by name, age or style. Choose a person, customize their appearance, and enter the campus. Robot bodies are available under **Browse robot bodies**. Your character is saved in this browser for the current tab identity.

## Find a room

Use **Rooms** while walking, or the directory below the campus. The directory includes the main building, upper floor, South Block and grounds. **Visit** enters a room; **Website** opens its associated website in a new tab. A link with `?room=office-annex` takes you to that room after character creation.

Move with WASD, drag to look, and use Shift to run. Phone controls appear on the campus. Close an exhibit before returning to movement.

## Project Studio

The renewal adds a glass studio west of Spaces, connected through the pavilion's west entrance. Choose **Open Project planning** to draft a project name, goal, notes and reference link. **Save brief** saves it only in this browser. It does not upload the brief or share it with a team. **Clear brief** requires a second confirmation.

Reference links must use HTTP or HTTPS. If browser storage is unavailable, copy the text before closing. Open Allternit separately to continue the project in your account; the local brief is not transferred automatically.

## Workspace floor

The public floor runs a labeled demo. In the Allternit app's [System drawer](/guides/system-drawer), the Workspace tab can display the roster supplied by the app. **Live** means the frame has received that roster; an empty connected floor says that no bots are present.

The workspace starts in 2D. Choose **2D**, **2.5D** or **3D** for the same team. Day, Evening, Night and Live control the 3D lighting. **Open thread** opens the bot's conversation in the app. **Review question** opens its question; it does not approve the requested action.

When the Office host provides them, desk screens show bot activity, replay shows recorded events, and sharing creates a floor-plan picture. Replay is labeled and does not approve requests. The Office can hide the frame's team panel and synchronize its selected bot with the floor.

## Exhibits and recovery

The arcade opens Gizzi Brawl in a new tab. Service exhibits link to current contact and scope information. Store displays are concepts and do not accept orders or payments. The courtyard controls play the films included with the site.

If graphics fail, retry the character preview or reload the campus. The workspace also offers a 2D view. Local saved character and project data remain in browser storage when that storage is available.

<Note>The Project Studio and renewal changes are implemented locally and await release. The public site may still show the previous version.</Note>

## Updated human looks

The renewal revises some starter outfits and hairstyles and improves human eye, skin and wardrobe rendering. Your saved customization stays as saved. Choose a starter again or use **Reset to start** to adopt its current look.

### Workspace bot avatars

In the resident creator, select **Bot avatars**, then search by character or pack.
Choose a card, enter a name, and save to walk the campus. Your selection stays in
this browser and returns when you edit your resident. The catalog shares the
Workspace avatar art and approved 3D models. Characters without an approved solid
model retain their own art. Choose **Human** to return to the person controls.

If a bot model cannot download, the creator keeps the selected artwork visible.
Choose **Retry 3D model** to retry the model download without losing your name or
character choice.
