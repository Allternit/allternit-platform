---
title: 'Write in my style'
description: 'Drafts you send as yourself, such as emails, messages and posts, are written the way you write.'
---

When you ask an agent to write something you will send as yourself (an email, a message, a post, a reply or a draft), it writes it the way you write: your greetings and sign-offs, how long your sentences are, how you use capitals, punctuation and emoji. The agent's own replies to you are not affected.

This works the same in Allternit Desktop, on ai.allternit.com (including its phone layout) and in the m.allternit.com app, and for every brain, including CLI brains that read the workspace `STYLE.md`.

## Turn it on or off

**Write in my style** is in Settings → Response style. It is on by default. Turning it off keeps your saved style, so you can turn it back on later without learning it again.

## Learn your style

Choose **Learn from my messages**. Allternit builds a short description of how you write from:

- Text you paste in (up to 10 pieces, such as a few emails you have sent), and
- Your own recent messages in Allternit (about the last 300 you wrote).

No model is used for this step. Short messages, code, links on their own and pasted logs are skipped. The result lists your habits in plain words and a few short examples of your writing. Email addresses, phone numbers and anything that looks like a password or key are removed from the examples.

If there is not enough of your own writing yet, Allternit says so. Paste a few emails or messages you have written and try again.

You can read and edit the description afterwards. Clearing it removes the style until you learn it again.

## What the agent is told

When the setting is on and you have a style saved, each chat includes this after your profile:

```text
Writing as the user: when you write something the user will send as themselves (emails, messages, posts, replies, drafts), write it the way they write. Their style:
(your saved style)
Don't use this style for your own replies to them.
```

Always read a draft before you send it. The agent copies your style, but the facts in a draft are still yours to check.
