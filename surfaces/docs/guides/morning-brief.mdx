---
title: Your bot's morning brief
description: What the daily brief shows, how to ask for it, and what your bot does the first time you meet.
---

The first time you open a bot's chat each day, a brief is already there. It is made once per day for each bot, so opening it on your phone and then your laptop shows the same brief.

## What is in it

- **Needs you**: what is waiting on you and why, one line each. Tap **Start** on a row and the bot takes it on as a task in a new thread.
- **Can wait**: things that are open but not urgent.
- **Today**: your calendar for the day.
- **Couldn't check**: anything the bot could not look at, such as Gmail that is not connected, with a **Connect** button.

## Ask for it any time

Tap **Brief me** above the message box. It shows today's brief, or builds it if there is none yet.

## The first time you meet

When you open a new bot for the first time it speaks first. If Gmail and Calendar are connected it reads recent mail and this week's calendar, then sends three short messages: who it thinks you are, three ways it can help, and a request to correct it. Tap **Sounds right**, or **Something's off** and type what to fix. Corrections are saved to the bot's memory. If nothing is connected, it says so, promises nothing about your mail, and shows the Connect buttons. There is no brief on this first day; it starts the next day.

The same works in the Desktop app, on ai.allternit.com (phone layout included) and in the m.allternit.com app. Developers: see the [Morning Brief API](/api/morning-brief).
