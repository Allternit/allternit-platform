---
title: Agents on one canvas
description: Let several of your bots restyle frames of one design canvas at the same time, see who is working on which frame, and keep or undo each change.
---

On a Design canvas, several agents (Gizzi or any of your bots) can work on
different frames at the same time. Each agent takes one frame, changes it,
and leaves the change for you to keep or undo. Two agents never change the
same frame at once.

This works on design artifacts that are canvases. An older HTML design opens
read-only; choose **Convert to canvas** first. You need to be signed in, and an
agent can only change a design you can edit.

## See who is working where

- **Tags on frames.** Each agent working on a frame shows as a name tag on that
  frame, in the agent's own colour: its name, its model cost so far, and a
  short task line such as "Dark restyle". The frame gets an outline in the
  same colour while the agent holds it. Colours stay the same for each agent.
- **One agent per frame.** An agent claims a frame before it changes it. While
  it holds the frame, other agents can't change it; they pick another frame
  or wait. A claim ends when the agent releases the frame, or a few minutes
  after the agent stops working.

## Keep or Undo each change

Each agent turn on a frame arrives as one change for that frame.

- **In review.** The canvas shows the frame's new look right away, marked
  **In review**. Nothing is saved until you decide.
- **Keep** saves it as a new version of the design.
- **Undo** throws the new look away and puts the frame back as it was.
- **Done changes** keep an **Undo** for 24 hours. If the frame has changed
  since, Undo says "changed since, can't undo" and leaves it alone.

## The activity rail

Open the rail from the canvas toolbar. On a computer it sits on the left of the
canvas; on a phone it is a sheet from the bottom of the screen. It has three
groups:

- **In review · N**: thumbnails of frames with changes waiting for you.
- **Doing**: what agents are working on now, with the agent, the frame and
  **Working**. A frame handed out with Split shows **Split 2/3**.
- **Done · N**: changes you kept or undid, with their time, and **View all**.

Click a row to select the frame, zoom to it and open its card.

## The frame card

Click a frame that has a change to open its card next to it. It shows:

- the agent, its reason ("Made the eyebrow and heading dark") and the cost.
  When the frame has several changes, **1/2** pages through them;
- a **Before/After** slider over the frame: drag it to see the old frame on
  the left and the new one on the right;
- **Keep** and **Undo**;
- a follow-up box. What you type goes to that agent: to its bot thread when
  the change came from one, otherwise to your current chat, starting with the
  frame's name.

## Split frames across bots

Select several frames and choose **Split to bots**. The dialog has one row per
frame: the frame's name and a bot picker showing each bot's current load and
cost. Write one instruction for all of them, such as "Restyle in dark mode",
then confirm.

- Each frame becomes a task in the project's [Delegate](/guides/project-delegate)
  list, assigned to the bot you picked, or to **Auto** (whoever is free first).
- The **Doing** group tracks the tasks while the bots work.
- When the design doesn't belong to a project, the dialog first asks which
  project's bots to use. With no bots yet, it links to making one.

## On a phone

On the ai.allternit.com phone layout and the m.allternit.com app you can watch
and review: tags on frames, the rail as a bottom sheet, and a tap on a frame
opens its card as a bottom sheet with Keep, Undo, the Before/After slider and
the follow-up box. Split to bots is on a computer only.
