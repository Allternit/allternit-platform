A computer on credits is a hosted computer you pay for from your credit balance instead of a plan. Current prices are shown in the app.

## Start and stop

Open **Billing** in the [console](https://platform.allternit.com/billing) and find **Computer on credits**. It shows the price per hour, your balance, and how long the balance lasts while the computer runs. Choose **Start** or **Stop**.

- Starting needs at least $5 in credits. With less, the app says: Starting needs at least $5 in credits.
- Starting takes about a minute.
- Stop ends the running charge. The stopped price applies while the computer is stopped.

The API is `POST /api/v1/provisioned-instances/credit` with `{"size":"medium"}` and `POST /api/v1/provisioned-instances/credit/stop`. See [Account compute status](/api/account-compute-status).

## What it costs

| Size | Running | Stopped |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Medium, 2 cores, 8 GB | $0.10 an hour | $0.005 an hour |

These are the current prices; the app shows the live ones. Billing is per minute. Runway is your balance divided by the running price.

## States

- Starting: about a minute.
- Running.
- Asleep: after 30 idle minutes. It wakes when an agent needs it.
- Out of credits: the computer stops and the disk is kept 14 days, with an email when it stops, after 7 days and the day before deletion. Add credits to start it again.

You get an in-app notice at about 24 hours and about 2 hours of runway left. [Auto top-up](/guides/credits-and-auto-top-up) can refill the balance.

Hosted computers run on Allternit servers. They sleep when idle.
