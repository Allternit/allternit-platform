## What a test case is

A test case is a saved chat plus a verdict. You say whether it went well or went wrong. Gizzi keeps the prompts, the steps it took, and a copy of your project files from when the chat started.

Later you can run your cases again. This tells you if a change made Gizzi worse at something you care about.

## Save a chat as a test case

| Where | How |
|---|---|
| Desktop and web | Open the session menu and choose **Save as test case**. |
| Phone, iOS and Android | Open the ⋯ menu on the chat and choose **Save as test case**. |
| Thumbs-down | After you rate a reply down, use the **Save as test case** link. |
| Terminal | Type `/testcase`, or `/testcase bad too slow` to set a verdict and a note. |

The save sheet asks four things:

- **Went well** or **Went wrong**.
- **What should have happened?** This note is optional.
- For a bad case, a quick check: **Wrong answer**, **Broke a file**, **Ignored instructions**, **Too slow or costly**, or **Other**.
- Nothing else. Saving takes a few seconds.

## Needs a check

When you save a case, Gizzi grades the saved chat against its own checks. If a good case does not pass, or a bad case does not fail, the case shows **Needs a check**. Open it and fix the note or the label, or delete it.

## Where cases live

Cases are files on your own computer. Nothing leaves your account, and nothing is sent to Allternit.

Chats without a computer, such as plan chats, can't be saved. The menu shows **Needs a computer**. If your computer can't be reached, you see **Couldn't reach your computer, try again**.

A suite holds up to 100 cases.

## Run your cases

Go to **Settings › Test cases**. You see your cases, their last result, and two buttons.

| Button | What it does | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Run checks** | Replays each saved chat and grades it again. No model runs. | Free |
| **Run live…** | Runs your prompts again in a copy of your project and grades the new result. | Uses your plan, or metered billing |

Before a live run, Allternit shows the price: the number of runs, about how many tokens and minutes, and either **Uses your plan** or **About $x**. Nothing starts until you press **Run**.

Live runs work in a temporary copy. Files and shell commands work there. Connectors and outside tools stay off.

## What replay can and can't tell you

Replay grades the chat you already saved. It does not call a model.

- It can tell you the checks still work, and that a change to Gizzi did not break how a saved chat is graded.
- It can't tell you whether a new prompt or setting makes Gizzi better. Only a live run can.
