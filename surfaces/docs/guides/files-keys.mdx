---
title: 'Add a device to your Files key'
description: 'Approve a new phone, browser or computer for your Files drive with a 6-digit code, remove a device, and what happens when the key is rotated.'
---

Your Files drive copies are sealed with a **Files key** that only your own devices hold. Allternit servers keep only sealed copies of it and cannot open them. A new device needs one of your existing devices to approve it before it can read your files.

<Note>
This ships dark with the Files drive. It works only when the `files_drive` flag is on for your account.
</Note>

## Your first device

Open Files on your first device. It makes the key on its own; there is nothing to approve.

## Add a new device

1. Sign in on the new device and open Files. It shows **Waiting for approval** and a 6-digit code.
2. Open Files, then **Devices**, on a device that already has the key. Under **Approve devices** you see the new device's name and a code. On iPhone this is **Settings > Files > Devices**. On Android, open Files settings and tap **Files key**.
3. Check that the two codes are the same, then click **Approve**. Click **Decline** if you don't recognize the device. Decline ends the request on all your devices and is recorded in your account's audit log. The device can ask again.
4. The new device unlocks by itself within a few seconds.

Only a device that has the key can approve or decline. A request that waits more than 10 minutes expires; ask again from the new device. If a request is marked **Invalid code**, the device's code does not match its key. You can't approve it, so decline it and try again.

## Give a computer the key

Open **Devices** in the computer's Files tab and click **Give key** next to **This computer**. The computer must be awake. The app takes the computer's request, approves it, and sends the key to the computer over its secure connection.

## Remove a device

Open **Devices** in Files and click **Remove** next to a device. It loses access, and Files shows **Rotate key**. Click it on a device that has the key: Files makes a new key and seals it to every device still listed.

## If a protected folder is locked

A folder marked protected asks you to click **Unlock** each time. The click, not a background task, is what unlocks it.
