title: Use the AI apps on your phone (Phone Link)
description: Connect the ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Kimi, Copilot, Grok or Perplexity apps already signed in on your phone, so your bots can use your own subscriptions through them.

**Phone Link** lets your bots use the AI apps you already pay for and are already signed in to on your own phone. Allternit never asks for or stores your ChatGPT, Claude or other AI passwords. The bot uses the app the way you would, on your device.

<Note>
Phone Link is being built and isn't in the app yet. The setup screens arrive in onboarding and in Settings → Subscriptions and Settings → Computers → Phones in a coming release, Android first, then iPhone. The steps below are what setup will ask you to do.
</Note>

## Android

You need Allternit Desktop, a USB cable, and the phone on the same Wi-Fi as your computer.

1. On the phone, turn on **Developer options**: Settings › About phone › tap **Build number** seven times.
2. Turn on **USB debugging** in Settings › Developer options.
3. Plug the phone into your computer and start Phone Link. When the phone asks **Allow USB debugging?**, tap **Allow**.
4. Allternit lists the AI apps it finds on the phone, then switches the connection to Wi-Fi. When it says **Connected**, you can unplug the cable.

If the phone restarts, plug it in again to reconnect over Wi-Fi.

## iPhone

iPhone setup will be one cable session with your Mac. You'll tap **Trust**, sign in with your own Apple ID in Allternit Desktop, turn on Developer Mode, and trust the developer certificate. After that it works over Wi-Fi. You do **not** need to put the iPhone in DFU or recovery mode. If you'd rather use hardware, the [Allternit USB Agent](/guides/usb-agent) already works.

## For developers

Desktop main runs the setup as a state machine (`surfaces/allternit-desktop/src/main/phone-link.ts`). The renderer API is `window.allternit.phoneLink`; types are in `phone-link-types.ts`.

| Method | Does |
|---|---|
| `status()`, `onStatusChanged(cb)` | Current `PhoneLinkStatus` and changes |
| `start({ platform })` | `'ios'` or `'android'`; replaces any setup in progress |
| `cancel()`, `retry()` | Back to `idle`; restart the last platform |
| `continue()` | The user finished a step the phone can't report (`developer_mode`, `trust_certificate`, `usb_debugging`) |
| `submitAppleId()`, `submitTwoFactor()` | iPhone signing; credentials go to main once and are not stored in plain text or logged |

States: `idle`, `not_supported`; iPhone `needs_cable` → `trust_prompt` → `apple_id` → `two_factor` → `developer_mode` → `waiting_for_reboot` → `trust_certificate` → `installing`; Android `usb_debugging` → `pairing`; then `ready_wifi`, or `error` with `{ code, message, retryable }`. Each status carries a one-line `instruction` for the user. Once ready, `apps` lists the vendor apps found on the phone.
