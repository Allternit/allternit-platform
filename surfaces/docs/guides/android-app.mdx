Allternit for Android is the app version of [m.allternit.com](https://m.allternit.com). It uses the same account as ai.allternit.com, Allternit Desktop and the iPhone app, so your chats, bots, projects and settings are the same everywhere. It needs Android 8 or later and Chrome, or another browser that supports Trusted Web Activities.

There are two versions with the same app ID. Pick one:

- **Google Play** (most people): [Get Allternit on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.allternit.app). Updates come from Google Play.
- **With Phone Link**: from allternit.com/android. Use this one if your bots should use the phone's other apps. Allternit Desktop installs it for you when you link the phone.

The Play app is the PWA, so it gets the new phone layout when m.allternit.com updates: the folded composer deck, Permission in **+**, the Code sessions view, haptics and the new Settings. See [Composer, replies and questions](/guides/chat-composer#on-a-phone).

## Install from Google Play

1. On the phone, open [allternit.com/play](https://allternit.com/play), or search for **Allternit** in the Play Store.
2. Tap **Install**.
3. Open **Allternit** and sign in.

## Buy a plan or credits

The Google Play app can buy a plan or a credit pack through Google Play, billed by Google. Open **Settings → Billing**. Prices are higher than on the web because of Google Play fees (Plus is $24.99 a month against $20 on the web; credit packs cost $11.99, $29.99, $59.99 and $119.99), and you get the same credits. Tap **Restore Purchases** to link a plan you already bought. Change or cancel a plan in Google Play subscriptions. You never need two plans: if you already have one, the app tells you who bills it. Deleting your Allternit account does not cancel a Google Play subscription, so cancel it there first. Refunds go through Google Play. See [Buying in the iPhone or Android app](/guides/credits-and-auto-top-up#buying-in-the-iphone-or-android-app).

The version with Phone Link, below, is not on Google Play and does not sell anything.

The Google Play version also leaves out [Android live view](/guides/android-live-view) (screen share) and **Control my iPhone by Bluetooth**. Both are in the version with Phone Link. On-device models are in the Google Play version; a loaded model runs while the app is open.

## Install the version with Phone Link

1. On the phone, open [allternit.com/android](https://allternit.com/android). The download starts.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to **Install unknown apps**, then go back and tap **Install**.
3. Open **Allternit** from your home screen and sign in.

The app opens your workspace full-screen, with no address bar. Links to m.allternit.com open in the app too. Without a browser that supports Trusted Web Activities, it opens the same page with the address bar showing.

## Phone Link

The version from allternit.com/android is also the Phone Link helper. The Google Play version doesn't include Phone Link. Phone Link lets your bots use the apps that are signed in on this phone. To turn it on, connect the phone to your computer with a USB cable and follow **Link a phone** in Allternit Desktop. If the phone has the Google Play version, Desktop replaces it with the Phone Link version. You stay signed in. See [Phone Link](/guides/phone-link).

To see Phone Link's status (paired computers, **Stop** and **Start**, **Revoke**), tap its notification. You can also long-press the Allternit icon and tap **Phone Link**.

## Error offers

With the Phone Link version, your bot can notice when another app on the phone shows an error or crash dialog (for example "keeps stopping") and offer to help. It is off until you turn it on.

- **Turn it on:** open Phone Link's status screen (tap its notification) and turn on **Spot errors in other apps**. While it's on, the screen says it is watching for errors.
- **What you see:** a notification that says "Your bot spotted an error. Fix it?" The notification never shows the error text or the app's name, so nothing private appears on the lock screen.
- **Fix it:** opens m.allternit.com with the error text attached in your bot's quick chat. Nothing is sent until you send it. The text is passed in the part of the link that never reaches a server.
- **Mute:** tap **Mute** on the notification to stop offers from that app. The status screen lists muted apps and your block list, where you can add or remove apps.
- **Never read:** password managers, banking and payment apps, and messaging apps are skipped before anything is read. Password fields are never read.

Phone Link reads only dialog windows for this, and only the text the phone's accessibility service already sees. The Google Play version doesn't read other apps; it shows offers inside Allternit when a bot's run fails, the same as [m.allternit.com](/guides/bot-avatars#on-the-web-the-phone-layout-and-the-pwa).

## Shortcuts, sharing and notifications

Long-press the Allternit icon for four shortcuts: **Ask a bot**, **Hand off a task**, **Needs you** and **Call main bot**. Each opens the app at that screen.

To send a link or some text to a bot, tap **Share** in any app and choose **Allternit**. Text and links only. To send files and photos to one of your computers, choose **Save to computer** instead (see [Your computers in the Files app](#your-computers-in-the-files-app)).

Notifications appear under **Allternit**, with their action buttons, not under Chrome. On Android 13 and later the app asks for notification permission the first time the site requests it. If you turn it off, use **Settings → Apps → Allternit → Notifications**.

## Your computers in the Files app

Both versions put each of your Allternit computers in Android's file picker and the **Files** app, one location per computer, beside Drive and Downloads. Any app that can open or save a file can use them.

1. In the picker or the **Files** app, open the menu and tap **Allternit**, then tap **Sign in**. A sign-in page opens in your browser. When it's done, tap **Return to Allternit**.
2. Choose which computers show in Files. All of them are on at first. To change it later, tap **Share > Save to computer** in any app, then **Allternit Files settings**, or long-press a folder on a computer in the **Files** app and tap **Settings**.
3. In any app, choose **Open** or **Save**, then pick a computer from the menu.

- **Saving:** a file saved from another app is kept on the phone first, then uploads to the computer in the background, and a notification says **Saved to** the computer when it lands. If both the phone and the computer changed the same file, both are kept: yours gets a name like `Lease (conflict Pixel 9 2026-11-15 1042).pdf`.
- **A sleeping computer:** browsing it is free and never wakes it. You see the list from its last sync. Opening a file asks first: tap the **Wake** notification, then **Wake**. Tick **Wake this computer automatically from now on** if you don't want to be asked again for that computer.
- **Saving to a sleeping computer:** you don't have to wake it. The file stays on the phone and shows **Waiting to send**. It goes the next time the computer is on, and nothing wakes it for this. Tap the **Wake** notification if you want it sent now. If the file changed on the computer while yours waited, both are kept.
- **Offline:** files you opened stay on the phone (up to 1 GB, the oldest go first) and open without a connection. They show **On this phone**.
- **Protected folders** such as Memory Drive ask for your fingerprint, face or screen lock each time, and stay open for five minutes.
- **Share > Save to computer:** in any app, tap **Share**, choose **Save to computer**, pick the computer, then the folder, and tap **Save here**. The last folder you used is at the top.
- **Ask my agent to read it:** in **Save to computer**, tick **Ask my agent to read it** and save into the computer's **Inbox**. The file waits there as a task. Your agent reads nothing until you open **Inbox tasks** (the row on the computer list in **Save to computer**) and tap **Approve**; **Reject** leaves it unread. A finished task shows an AI-generated label with the agent and who approved it. The routes are in the Files drive Inbox API page (PR 2445).
- **Badges:** a file waiting to upload, a file waiting for its computer to wake, a failed upload, a conflict copy and a file made by an agent each get a coloured icon.

## Rating the app

If you installed Allternit from Google Play, the app may show Google Play's in-app review card when you open it, after you have used it several times over a few days. The app opens right after, whether or not you rate. Google Play decides whether the card shows. The app installed from allternit.com/android has no rating prompt.

The app keeps its counters on your phone only. Nothing about them is sent to Allternit.

## Updates

Allternit Desktop updates the app when it sets up or relinks the phone. To update it yourself, open [allternit.com/android](https://allternit.com/android) again and install over the old version. Your sign-in stays.

## Signing in to your computers' files

The first time you use the Files features (your computers in the Files app, **Save to computer**, **Inbox tasks**), the app opens a sign-in page in your browser at m.allternit.com/files/connect-android.

1. Sign in if you are not signed in. The page asks "Show your computers on" your phone, with your account name. Tap **Connect**, or **Cancel** to go back without signing in.
2. The page makes an access key for this phone and seals it so only this phone can open it. The key is sealed to a key kept in the phone's Android Keystore, which never leaves the phone. It shows up in your API keys as "Android Files" with your phone's name, and you can revoke it there.
3. Tap **Return to Allternit**. The app opens signed in and shows your computers.

Sometimes Chrome keeps the return link in the browser instead of opening the app. If you see a page with an **Open Allternit** button, tap it: that is one extra tap, and it finishes the sign-in. The same link is on the sign-in page as "App didn't open?". If the sign-in link is incomplete or came from somewhere else, the page says so and gives no key. The sign-in only returns to the Allternit app (`com.allternit.app`), never to another app.

## Moving from the old Phone Link app

Phone Link before version 0.4.0 was a separate app called **Allternit Phone Link**. When Allternit Desktop next sets up the phone, it removes the old app, installs this one and links the phone again. If you installed this app yourself first, uninstall **Allternit Phone Link** in **Settings → Apps**.

## For developers

- App ID `com.allternit.app`. The Kotlin classes keep the `com.allternit.phonelink` package. Source: `surfaces/allternit-mobile/android`.
- The launcher is `LauncherActivity` from `androidbrowserhelper` (a Trusted Web Activity around `https://m.allternit.com/`). Its asset links are served from allternit-ai `public/.well-known/assetlinks.json`, with the release cert `3F:8A:39:51:…:E0:67` (sideloaded builds) and the Google Play app-signing cert `8D:4C:4F:BE:…:F9:4B` (Play installs).
- Each build is published under `runtime.allternit.com/phone-link/android/<version>/` and pinned in Desktop's `phone-android-helper.ts`. The `/android` redirect in the www site's `public/_redirects` points at the current build. Move it with every new pin.
- Shortcuts are static (`res/xml/shortcuts.xml`, in `src/main`; the Phone Link build overrides the file with the same four plus **Phone Link**). They open `https://m.allternit.com/?allternit_open=ask|handoff|inbox|call` through `LauncherActivity`.
- The share target is the `METADATA_SHARE_TARGET` meta-data on `LauncherActivity` (JSON in the `share_target` string, GET `/share-target` with `title` and `text`) plus an `ACTION_SEND` `text/plain` intent filter. androidbrowserhelper 2.5.0 has no `url` parameter, so a shared link arrives in `text`.
- Notification delegation is `DelegationService` plus `NotificationPermissionRequestActivity` (both from `androidbrowserhelper`) and the `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` permission, declared in `src/main`.
- **Files drive** (both builds, `src/main/java/com/allternit/app/files/`): `AllternitDocumentsProvider` (authority `<applicationId>.files`, one root per computer) speaks [Files drive protocol v2](/api/files-drive) over the cloud relay (`POST /api/v1/runtime-devices/:id/proxy`, relay candidate from `GET /api/v1/computer-files/:id/endpoints`). Android is never the owning device and has no mesh in the Google Play build, so it always uses the relay. The contract types are compiled straight from `contracts/files-drive/gen/FilesDriveContract.gen.kt` (a Gradle source dir) and read with `WireJson`, so there is no serialization dependency.
  - Network work never runs on a binder thread: listings answer from the cache at once and refresh off-thread, then `notifyChange` reloads the picker. A long poll on the change feed keeps an open folder current and never counts as activity.
  - Browsing never sends a wake ticket. Opening or saving content checks the computer's wake setting: `auto` calls wake consent silently, `ask` posts the wake notification (`WakePromptActivity`), `never` fails with the setting's message.
  - Writes go to a staging file and upload when the writer closes it, with `base_rev`, `on_conflict: keep_both`, the device name and `utc_offset_minutes`. Up to 4 MiB uses the small write; bigger files use a resumable upload session with the pinned FastCDC chunker (`Chunker.kt`, checked against `contracts/files-drive/vectors/chunker-v1.json` by `ChunkerVectorsTest`). Queued uploads survive the process being killed and retry through `FilesUploadJobService` when the network is back.
  - Sign-in: a Keystore RSA-2048 key is made, a Custom Tab opens `m.allternit.com/files/connect-android?state&pk&alg=RSA-OAEP-SHA1&device&device_id&app`, and the page returns the cloud-api token encrypted to that key through the App Link `https://m.allternit.com/files/android-callback?state&sealed` (or `allternit-files://signin?state&sealed` for a build whose App Link is not verified, such as debug). The token is stored sealed with a Keystore AES-256-GCM key in no-backup storage. The TWA's `m.allternit.com` filter has priority -1 so the callback path goes to `FilesSignInActivity` with no chooser.
  - Protected roots use `BiometricPrompt` (screen-lock confirmation on Android 8) and send `x-allternit-unlock` for five minutes.

### Native app (in testing)

The Kotlin + Jetpack Compose app that will replace the Trusted Web Activity is built as a third flavor of the same module. Until the switch it is installed only by sideload; it never goes to a Google Play testing track. The Google Play and Phone Link builds above are unchanged. Plan and progress: `handoffs/android-native-2026-11-18` in the workspace.

- **Flavor `nativeapp`** (`app/build.gradle.kts`): same app ID and signing key, `versionCode` 1000 and up (the Trusted Web Activity builds keep 17 to 999 for fixes), version name suffix `-native`. It is tested by sideload (`adb install`) and is not uploaded to Google Play until it reaches parity with the web app; then it replaces the Trusted Web Activity on Play production with a staged rollout. Build it with `./gradlew :app:bundleNativeappRelease` (or `:app:assembleNativeappDebug`, installed as `com.allternit.app.ondevice`).
- **Launcher:** `com.allternit.feature.shell.MainActivity` (`src/nativeapp/AndroidManifest.xml`). The stock `LauncherActivity` stays in the build without the launcher category (the manifest placeholder `twaLauncherCategory`), so it can come back as a rollback switch. The native shell itself has no web-version button or row, the same as iOS.
- **Modules:** `:core:design` (colors, radii, icons, haptics, friendly states), `:core:api` (generated API client), `:core:auth` (Clerk, session state, API token), `:feature:onboarding` (sign-in screen), `:feature:shell` (main activity and the home shell: home, drawer, mode fan), `:feature:chat` (the chat screen: `ChatScreen`, `ChatViewModel`, message cards, the no-computer state). Only the `nativeapp` flavor depends on them; `scripts/android-check.sh` fails if Clerk or Compose reaches the Google Play or Phone Link build.
- **Toolchain:** Kotlin 2.3.21, Compose BOM 2026.06.01, OkHttp 5.4.0 (`gradle/libs.versions.toml`). Clerk Android needs them.

#### Sign-in

Sign-in uses the Clerk Android SDK (`com.clerk:clerk-android-api` 1.4.1) against the production instance `clerk.allternit.com`, the same instance as iOS and the web. `AllternitAuth` starts Clerk in `MainActivity.onCreate` and publishes the state (`Starting`, `SignedOut`, `Finishing`, `SignedIn`, `Unavailable`) as a `StateFlow`.

- **Screen:** the same layout as iOS `LoginGateView`. On top is the offline welcome loop: the A://TERNIT wordmark assembles, Gizzi arrives and the bot cast joins. It is the shared `surfaces/allternit-mobile/shared/login-arrival/login-arrival.html`, which `:feature:onboarding` copies into its assets at build time (task `syncLoginArrival`). It plays in a WebView that blocks network loads and navigation, and its CSP blocks every request, so no credentials ever reach it. It pauses while the email form is open or a sign-in is running, and shows one still frame when **Remove animations** is on. Under it, in the same order as the phone sign-in on m.allternit.com: the headline, the cream **Continue with Apple** button, GitHub and Google with their logos, an "or" line, the Email field with a cream **Continue**, "Secured by clerk" and the 18+ line.
- **Email:** the address gets a 6-digit code. A known address signs in; a new one signs up. The screen shows the 18+, Terms and Privacy line, as on iOS.
- **Google, GitHub and Apple:** Clerk OAuth in a Custom Tab, returning through Clerk's `SSOReceiverActivity` on `clerk://<applicationId>.callback` (allowed in the Clerk dashboard for `com.allternit.app` and `com.allternit.app.ondevice`). Clerk is started with the Frontend API proxy `https://allternit.com/__clerk` (`AllternitAuth.PROXY_URL`), because the Google client only accepts the proxy's `/v1/oauth_callback`; without it Google answers `redirect_uri_mismatch`. iOS uses the same proxy. On Android, Apple goes through the web OAuth flow; iOS uses the native Sign in with Apple sheet.
- **Organization:** the production instance requires one. A new account's session waits on the choose-organization task; the app uses the first membership or creates "First name's Organization", the same as iOS `finishOrganizationTaskIfNeeded`.
- **API calls:** `AllternitAuth.tokenProvider` goes into `ApiClients.okHttp(bearer = …)`. It sends the Clerk session token (about 60 seconds) and gets a new one from Clerk's cache 10 seconds before it expires.

#### First run

After the first sign-in on a phone, the native app runs the same setup as the iPhone app (`FirstRunSetupView`) before the workspace opens. It is a full-screen swap, never on top of the sign-in screen. The code is in `:feature:shell`, package `firstrun`.

- **When it shows:** the app asks your setup config (`GET /api/onboarding/config`, `user.onboardingComplete`). Done on the server: it never shows. Not done: it shows unless this phone already finished it. When the server can't answer in 8 seconds (no computer online to relay it), setup shows only for an account with no computer paired at all, so a returning user whose computer is asleep goes straight in and is asked again next launch. While it checks you see the A:// mark.
- **Where "done" is stored:** DataStore key `allternit-onboarding-complete` (the iOS UserDefaults key), set when you finish. Finishing also saves the config with `onboardingComplete: true` and your default brain (`POST /api/onboarding/config`); if that fails, setup still finishes on the phone.
- **Your AI:** the "Your workspace" graph on top (A:// in the middle, the five brains around it), Gizzi + A:// with A://'s state, the 30-block progress, then "Hi, I'm Gizzi. Which AI should I think with? If you already pay for one, use it here." The brains are connect-kit rows from `GET {cloud}/api/v1/connections`, merged with the rows your computer reports when one answers (`GET /api/v1/connections` on it, through the relay `POST {cloud}/api/v1/runtime-devices/<id>/proxy`, as on iOS). A computer row (the local brain, the computer's Codex sign-in, coding tools) wins only when it is on, and it connects on that computer. With no computer, those rows show the catalog fallback ("Runs on a computer"). in three groups: **Works on any device**, **Needs your computer** and **Coming soon**. **Allternit** is included and needs no computer: tap **Included** to make it your brain. **Gemini** and **An API key** take a key (paste it; it is checked with the vendor first). Plan rows (Claude, Grok, Kimi, ChatGPT, Copilot) sign in with a code in the browser and finish by themselves; they show **Chat only** because a plan answers only messages you send. A row the account can't use yet shows why and its fallback (**Use a key**, **Add a computer**). **More ways: an API key, a local brain** adds the last two rows.
- **Your phone:** this phone is already connected. **Add a computer** is offered under **Later, if you need it**, with "Chat works without it". There is no computer step: plan chats with no computer are the simple start.
- **Meet your bots:** between Your phone and Everything connected, the iOS flow screen for screen. On top, the step's picture: the bots-handing-work-on loop (the iOS video, a still under **Remove animations**), the team around the main bot, the leader on its colour, today's routines, a bell, and who does the first job. #### Testing sign-in

Debug builds accept a Clerk sign-in token as an intent extra, so Maestro and emulator checks need no password:

```bash
adb shell am start -n com.allternit.app.ondevice/com.allternit.feature.shell.MainActivity --es ticket SIGN_IN_TOKEN
```

Mint the token with the Clerk backend API (`POST /v1/sign_in_tokens`) for the test account.

Debug builds also read the iOS launch arguments as intent extras, for Maestro (`launchApp: arguments:`) and screenshots. Leading dashes are ignored, and values can be strings or booleans. Release builds ignore them all.

| Extra | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `skip-auth` | Opens the workspace without signing in (iOS `-skip-auth`). API screens show their signed-out or empty states. |
| `open-tab <id>` | Opens a section: a drawer row id (`projects`, `artifacts-library`, `automation-tasks`, `customize`, …) or an iOS tab name (`artifacts`, `automation`, `models`), or `settings`, `search`, `chats` (the session overview). |
| `sidebar` | Starts with the drawer open (also `calm-drawer-open`). |
| `shell-mode <home\|bots\|code\|aci>` | Starts in that mode (also the flags `chat`, `code`, `bots`, `browser`, `open-aci`). |
| `open-inbox`, `open-quick-task`, `open-continue-cli` | Opens that sheet on launch. `inbox-item <id>` marks the Inbox item a link asked for. |

```bash
adb shell am start -n com.allternit.app.ondevice/com.allternit.feature.shell.MainActivity --ez skip-auth true --es open-tab projects --ez sidebar true
```

- **All threads.** Filter by Needs input, Working, Ready for review, Done or Archived; tap the dots on a row to rename, mark reviewed or archive. **Permissions** sets Manual, Auto-accept edits, Plan mode or Read only.
- **No computer.** Every Code screen shows "Connect a computer or Allternit Cloud" until one is connected. **Computer** lets you pick which one runs your commands.

#### ACI (Computer)

Pick **ACI** on the mode fan (hold the menu button) to open ACI mode. The drawer's ACI rows pick the view: **MCP Apps**, **ACI Extensions**, **Computers** and **Teach**.

- **MCP Apps:** tabs **Installed**, **Discover** and **Build**, plus **Add by URL** and **Mini Apps**. Tap an installed app to set when it asks (Always ask, Ask before changes, Ask before important changes), see what it can do, and Remove it. Discover lists the Allternit catalog and the vendors' official servers; tap one to add it and sign in.
- **ACI Extensions:** your extensions with an on/off switch, the **Marketplace**, a category filter and **Upload Extension**. The list is kept on this phone.
- **Teach:** the skills the agent can replay, saved on your computer. Open a skill to edit its goal, steps and inputs, **Run** it (the agent replays it in the ACI browser), or **Delete** it. **Import** a .har recording, then **Analyze** to make a new skill. Teach needs a connected computer.
- **Mini Apps:** search, pick a category, **Open** an app or **Pin** it.
- **Computer detail:** tap a computer in Computers for **Desktop** (its screen) and **Files**: its folders, and **Inbox requests** where you approve or reject a file for your agent to read. An asleep computer shows its saved list.


- **Agent (the landing):** a dotted page with a title, the search bar and the **Allternit Computer Agent** toggle. With the agent off, the bar opens a site or a Google search in the in-app browser. With it on, the bar takes a task and starts an agent run. **Customize** lets you add (label, URL, emoji) or remove shortcut tiles; they stay on the phone. When you have computers, **Computer the agent uses** sits on top; tap it to pick one. Runs go to that computer through the relay.
- **Pair a computer:** with no computers, a card suggests pairing. Dismiss it once and it stays away.
- **In-app browser:** back, the address field, a progress line, back and forward, and **Extension Chat**. Extension Chat offers suggested tasks or your own, and starts an agent run.
- **Agent run:** the COMPUTER USE strip shows the status, the last message, the step and the adapter. The page shows the computer's screenshots with a box on the element the agent acts on, and the trace log below. When the agent asks first, **Approve** and **Deny** appear. **Stop** ends the run. Leaving the screen stops a live run. With no computer, the run shows the no-computer message and **Retry**.
- **Computers:** **Add a computer** (the three pairing steps), **View a computer's screen** (any computer on the account: cloud, a bot's or a paired Mac), your paired computers with online state and last seen, linked phones (with call audio for a cable-bridged phone), cloud computer backups and the workflow specs on a paired computer. Tap a computer to open its live screen.
- **A computer's screen:** the native VNC viewer. The bar says who drives. **Take control** gives you the pointer and keyboard; **Release** hands it back. An off computer shows **Start**. Pinch to zoom, drag to pan when zoomed, tap to click, two-finger tap or long-press to right-click, drag to move with the button held. The keyboard button shows the keyboard while you drive. A Mac's screen sharing asks for that Mac's account name and password; other VNC servers ask for a password. The sign-in stays in memory only.
- **A bot's desktop:** the bot's **Computer** page shows its desktop (running or not, and who controls it), **View desktop**, **Take over**, **Watch without pausing**, **Hand back** and **Start a desktop**, plus the computers assigned to the bot. The desktop viewer is the same VNC viewer; input only goes out while you control it.
- **Not yet on Android:** MCP Apps, ACI Extensions, Teach, mini apps and the Files drive rows of a computer. Use Allternit Desktop, the web or the iPhone app for these for now.

#### Automation

The drawer's **Automation Tasks** row opens the hub (in Home and Code). **Scheduled** and **Board** sit at the top.

- **Scheduled:** tabs for **All**, **Goals**, **Routines**, **Loops** and **Agent Heartbeats**, each with a count, and a **List / Graph / Calendar** switch. The list has search and a Date, Name or Type sort, and pull to refresh. Each card shows its type, schedule in words, the next run (or the live step while it runs), the last 14 runs as dots, **Run now** (and **Stop** while a routine runs) and an on/off switch. Tap a card for the detail: instructions, run history, **Run now**, **Pause**, **Change time** (the same time editor the Bots routines use), **Mark done** for a goal, and **Delete** (asks first).
- **Calendar:** Day, Week and Month over the next runs. Tap a run to open it, then **Change time** to move it; there is no dragging. Tap an empty day to start a task at 9:00 AM.
- **Graph:** goals with the routines and loops that serve them, then everything else, each with its recent runs.
- **New task (+):** pick Routine, Loop, Goal or Heartbeat, then fill the name, what it should do, which bot runs it, the goal it serves, and **When**. Nothing is created until you tap **Create**. A routine needs a bot.
- **On this computer:** the routines (a named list of commands) and loops (a command repeated until it exits clean) your computer runs itself, with their detail, **Run Now / Restart**, **Delete**, and **New routine** and **New loop**.
- **Board:** the Factory's tasks by column (In review, In progress, To do, Backlog, Done) with filter chips and a **Scheduled** link back.

With no computer, the computer-side parts (heartbeats, On this computer, Board) show "Connect a computer or Allternit Cloud". Goals, routines and loops come from your account. Not yet on Android: dragging a calendar run to a new time, and the heartbeat file editor.

#### Cowork

Open **Cowork tasks** from the **+** sheet for the task list: **Tasks** and **Agent Tasks**, **+** for a new task (title, description, Human or Agent), tap a task to change its status, see who has it or delete it. The Cowork launchpad starts a session or opens a recent one, and the antenna button opens **Fabric Transport**: approvals you can **Grant** or **Deny** and recent runs with their events. With no computer, each screen says so instead of showing an error.

#### Office

In **Bots**, open **Bots options** and tap **Office** for the bots' floor: the same 3D office as the web, with a character for each bot and its status. Tap a bot or its question to open its chat; a message typed in the floor's panel goes to the bot's main chat and the reply shows in the panel. If the floor is slow to load you see "The office didn't load" and **Try again**.

In the **Artifacts** library, **Allternit Office** lists your Docs, Sheets, Slides and PDFs, newest first (pull to refresh). A row opens the artifact. On a PDF artifact, **...** then **Sign PDF** opens the signing screen: draw your signature, tap **Sign & Share**, pick where to save the signed copy, then **Save to library** if you want it in Artifacts. **Sign PDF** in the documents list signs a blank page. On a record imported from the older Office store, **...** then **Open in Office** shows its sections and an **Interactive** button for the full editor. A PDF, text, Word or Excel file shared to Allternit from another app opens in a viewer; Word and Excel hand off to your phone's viewer. With no computer, Office screens say so instead of showing an error.

#### Phones and Connect

The drawer's **Phones** button opens **Your bots' phones**: one row per bot that has a number, an email address or a channel, with its unread count. Tap a row to open that bot's phone in Bots mode. **Give a bot a phone** picks the bot, then how people reach it; **Email** is built (the bot's address and how it replies), the other ways finish in Allternit Desktop for now. **Connect a subscription** lists the subscriptions you can bring in and runs the one-step connect. **Phone control** shows this phone's pairing, your Allternit Link boards and **Unpair**. **Android phone screen share** makes a six-digit code or approves a request from another phone.

In **Computers**, tap a linked phone that is live to watch its screen. The view is read-only until you tap **Allow taps**; then you can tap, swipe, type and press Back, Home and Apps. If the phone is not sharing, the screen says to tap Start on the phone. Nothing here starts a phone task: a phone task card only follows a run that already started, and **Needs your OK** opens the Inbox.

#### Models

The drawer's Models row (and the model picker's manage link) opens **Models**: the Freeweights catalog with search, **Fits this phone**, **Everything** and **Phone collection** filters, and list, grid or deck faces. Tap a model for its page: **Send to my computer** starts a download there and follows it, **Use in a chat** selects it and opens a new chat. The sliders button lists the models your account can use and opens **Benchmark**, which shows this phone and times one fixed prompt. **On this phone** is empty until the on-phone engine reaches the native app.
