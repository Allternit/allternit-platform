You can talk to a bot instead of typing, in any session. What you say becomes a normal message in the thread, so the bot's tools, approvals and memory work exactly as they do when you type. The bot's reply is read aloud while it appears on screen.

Voice works the same on Allternit Desktop, on ai.allternit.com, and in the Allternit phone app (m.allternit.com).

## Quick voice

1. In a session, with the message box empty, press the **voice** button (the waveform) next to Send. Or click the bot on its [stage](/guides/bot-avatars#your-bots-stage).
2. The message box turns into the voice bar. Start talking.

The first time you start voice — from the voice button, a bot's **Talk** button, or full-screen — a short sheet explains what voice mode is: where it runs, that the microphone is needed, that you can connect your own OpenAI or ElevenLabs account for a different voice, and that you can talk any time to interrupt. It shows once; **Choose where voice runs** opens Settings › Voice from it.

The bar shows:

- **The Allternit mark**, or the bot's own avatar when you talk with a bot. It moves to show what's happening: listening, thinking, or speaking.
- **A status line**: "Listening", "Thinking…", or while the bot talks, "Talk any time. Start speaking to interrupt."
- **Where voice runs**: "Voice on this device", "Voice on your OpenAI account" or "Voice by your ElevenLabs account".
- **Mute**, **Type instead** (ends voice and puts you back in the message box), the **full-screen** button, and **End voice**.

While you talk, your words appear in a dashed bubble at the bottom of the thread ("Hearing you…"). When you finish, your message is sent and labeled **Spoken** in the thread.

You don't need to press anything when you're done talking. Voice works out when you've finished your turn.

### Interrupting

Start talking while the bot is speaking and it stops, both the voice and the reply, just like pressing Stop. Then it listens to you. You can turn this off in **Settings › Voice › Let me interrupt the bot by talking**.

## Full-screen voice

Full-screen voice is also called live mode. Press the full-screen button on the voice bar, or the **Talk** button above a bot's message box. In a plain chat the Allternit mark fills the screen. When you talk with a bot, the bot's own avatar stands in the middle at full size, with a moving look around it. The look follows your voice while the bot listens and the bot's voice while it speaks, and it turns slowly while the bot thinks. A soft light in the look's colour falls on the avatar. Rings, wireframes and dot spheres pass in front of the avatar's lower body, so it sits inside the look rather than in front of a picture. The screen also has:

- A status label: **CONNECTING**, **LISTENING**, **THINKING** or **SPEAKING**. While voice connects, a small spinner sits on the avatar.
- **Bots working on this**: when the bot hands work to other bots, their avatars show in a row above it, marked **Working** or **Done**.
- **On a wide screen, the transcript beside the bot**: each turn of this call with the speaker's face and name, the turn in progress marked live, and the step the bot is working on. The thread keeps the full history.
- **On a phone, captions like TV captions**: a dark band above the controls with the speaker's name (in the bot's colour, or **You**). Words appear as they're spoken, and past two lines the text rolls up. Between turns it says, for example, "Goldie is thinking".
- The bot's steps as small chips while it works, for example "Calendar: moved Site visit to 2:00 PM · Done".
- **Captions** on/off (on a wide screen this shows or hides the transcript), **Mute**, **Type** (ends voice and puts you back in the message box) and **End**.
- **Look** at the top: pick this bot's own look and colour, or **Same as Settings**. A pick applies to that bot only.
- **Back to chat** at the top returns to the thread with everything in place. Voice keeps going in the bar.
- **Talking with …** shows which bot you're talking to. In a bot's thread you can pick another bot; its thread opens and you can start voice there.

The voice bar shows a small version of the same look around the bot's avatar.

On a phone the controls are larger and sit clear of the home indicator. If your device is set to reduce motion, the look and avatar stay still and the status is shown in words.

## Settings › Voice

### Where voice runs

Allternit doesn't run voice on its own servers. Voice runs on your device, or on your own OpenAI or ElevenLabs account if you connect one.

- **Automatic (recommended)**: voice runs on this device once its voice engine is ready. It's free, and your audio stays on this device.
- **This device**: the same, chosen on purpose. In a browser, this starts the one-time voice download if it hasn't happened yet.
- **Your own OpenAI**: OpenAI Realtime listens to you and OpenAI speech reads the replies, using your own OpenAI API key. Easier on slower devices. OpenAI bills you directly.
- **Your own ElevenLabs**: an ElevenLabs voice reads the replies, using your own ElevenLabs API key. Listening stays on this device. ElevenLabs bills you directly.

If this device can't run voice yet, voice says so and points you to Settings › Voice, where you can download the voice to this device or connect your own provider.

In the Allternit iPhone app, **Automatic** and **This device** run on the phone: the voice model downloads once, and until it's ready the iPhone's built-in voice is used. Your own OpenAI or ElevenLabs isn't in the iPhone app yet; use ai.allternit.com, the phone web app or Desktop for it.

### Your own provider

Optional. Paste your **OpenAI API key** or **ElevenLabs API key**, then choose **Your own OpenAI** or **Your own ElevenLabs** under **Where voice runs**. For ElevenLabs you can also paste a **voice ID** from your ElevenLabs voice library; without one, replies use Rachel, a voice every ElevenLabs account has.

- Your key is kept only on this device, in the app's local storage, the same way your image and video provider keys are. It's never sent to Allternit.
- Calls go straight from this device to OpenAI (api.openai.com) or ElevenLabs (api.elevenlabs.io). For OpenAI listening, the app uses your key to get a short-lived session key, and only that short-lived key opens the live connection.
- You pay OpenAI or ElevenLabs for what you use, under your agreement with them. Allternit doesn't add a charge.
- **Remove** deletes the key from this device. A key saved on one device isn't copied to your other devices.
- The bot's own model still writes every reply, so what you hear is what's in the thread.

### Voice pack on this device

The first time you use on-device voice, Allternit Desktop downloads a voice pack (about 100 MB for the Light pack). This screen shows each pack's size, whether it's installed, and download progress. **Get Accurate pack** downloads a larger pack that understands speech better. If the voice engine isn't installed yet, the screen says so.

### Voice in your browser

On the web app and the phone app, **This device** runs the voice engine inside the browser tab. The first time you use voice, it downloads the voice models by itself, with progress on the voice screen (about 210 MB: about 45 MB for listening and 166 MB for the voice), checks them against published checksums and keeps them in your browser, so later sessions start quickly and work offline. **Remove downloaded models** frees the space. Your audio stays in the tab. **Automatic** uses it on any device that can run it, phones included, as long as the device keeps up. A browser too old for WebAssembly SIMD, or a device with under 2 GB of memory, shows a message saying why instead. The in-browser voice engine includes GPL-3.0 code (espeak-ng); its source is at https://github.com/Allternit/allternit-tts.

### Speech to text

When voice runs on this device, **Automatic (system when available)** turns your speech into text with your device's own recognizer where it is good and private, and with Allternit's recognizer (Moonshine) everywhere else. **Allternit on-device** always uses Allternit's.

- **iPhone and iPad app**: Automatic uses Apple's on-device speech recognition, in your device's language. On iOS 26 and later the first conversation may download Apple's speech model in the background; until it's ready, Allternit's recognizer listens. The first time, iOS asks to allow Speech Recognition on iOS 17 to 25. Audio never leaves the phone.
- **Safari and iPhone/iPad browsers**: Automatic uses Safari's built-in speech recognition (Apple's speech engine) and shows your words as you talk. Apple decides whether Safari recognizes speech on the device or on Apple's servers.
- **Chrome, Edge, Firefox and the Desktop app** always use Allternit's recognizer. Chrome and Edge's built-in recognition sends audio to Google, so Allternit doesn't use it. The option isn't shown there.

If the system recognizer is turned off, denied permission or stops working, voice switches to Allternit's recognizer for the rest of the conversation without interrupting you. Replies are always read by Allternit's own voice.

### Voice

Pick how your bots sound. Voices have plain names that say how they sound — Gentle, Crisp, Calm, Warm — with the engine's voice id in small type under each. Press a voice's **Preview** button to hear one short sentence through the voice engine on this device; on the web the first preview downloads the voice models first (this happens once). Only one preview plays at a time. With **Your own OpenAI**, voices whose names match an OpenAI voice (Steady, Clear, Crisp, Low, Story) use that OpenAI voice; the others use Alloy. With **Your own ElevenLabs**, the ElevenLabs voice ID decides.

### Live mode

- **Speaker style**: what moves around a bot's avatar in live mode. **Motion** has Ring, Orbit, Sash, Signal, Swell and Pixel. **Allternit** has Blocks (A-block squares), ASCII (a ring of characters, like the ASCII backgrounds), Glow (a soft halo) and Still (no effect; the avatar lifts a little while it talks). Each tile moves when you point at it or pick it. The default is Ring.
- **Colour**: Spectrum, Bot's colour, Ember, Ocean, Aurora, Rose, Gold or Graphite. **Bot's colour** (the default) follows the bot you talk with. Muted and stopped calls turn grey.
- **Caption size**: Small, Medium or Large, for captions on phones.

A bot can have its own style and colour: press **Look** at the top of live mode. Settings is the default for every bot that follows it.

In the Allternit iPhone app, live mode with a bot shows the same avatar, all ten looks and the same captions. Set the look in **Settings › Voice › Live mode**, or for one bot from the gear in live mode.

### Listening

- **Let me interrupt the bot by talking**
- **Show live transcript**: shows your words as you speak, in the thread and in full-screen captions.

## Privacy

- **On this device**, audio is turned into text on your computer. Only the text of your message is sent, the same as if you'd typed it.
- **In Safari with Speech to text on Automatic**, Safari's own recognizer does the listening and Apple may process the audio. Choose **Allternit on-device** to keep it in the tab.
- **With your own OpenAI**, your audio and the bot's replies go from your device to OpenAI over an encrypted connection.
- **With your own ElevenLabs**, listening stays on your device; the bot's replies go to ElevenLabs to be read aloud.
- Allternit never receives your audio or your provider key.
- Either way, your spoken message is saved in the thread like any typed message.

## When something isn't working

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Microphone access is blocked." | Allow the microphone for Allternit. In a browser, use the lock icon in the address bar. On Desktop, open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone. Then press **Try again**. |
| "Voice engine not installed" | Update Allternit Desktop, or connect your own OpenAI account in Settings › Voice. |
| "The voice pack is still downloading." | Wait for the download in Settings › Voice. |
| "Voice on this device needs a one-time download." | Choose **This device** in Settings › Voice to download it, or connect your own provider. |
| "Add your OpenAI API key in Settings › Voice." | Paste your key under **Your own provider**, or switch back to **Automatic**. |
| "OpenAI did not accept your API key." / "ElevenLabs did not accept your API key." | Check or replace the key under **Your own provider**. |
| "Your … account is out of credit or over its rate limit." | Add credit or wait, in your OpenAI or ElevenLabs account. |
| "Reconnecting…" | The connection dropped. Voice reconnects by itself; the bot's reply keeps appearing in the thread. |

## Phone calls: Listen and Take over

When a bot answers a call on its phone number, or on its own phone wired to Desktop with a [call cable](/guides/bot-voice-on-linked-phone), the call shows in the caller's thread.

- **Listen** lets you hear the call live without the caller hearing you.
- **Take over** puts you on the call: your microphone is connected and the bot stops talking.
- **Hand back** returns the call to the bot, which carries on with the conversation so far.

The call screen shows the bot's own avatar: speaking while it talks, listening while the caller talks, and dimmed while you have the call.

Allternit says at the start of every call that the caller is talking to an AI, and whether the call is recorded. For the call screen itself, see [Calls and texts in threads](/guides/calls-and-texts).
