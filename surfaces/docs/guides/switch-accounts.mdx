## Switch organizations

The accounts card at the top of **Settings** on a phone (and the account menu on the web) lists you and each organization you belong to. Tap a row to switch. The active row has a check, and tapping it opens your profile. Each row shows its plan.

When you switch to another organization, the page reloads. Allternit clears the saved offline sign-in record and the cached lists, then loads the new organization. If you switch to a different person, it also clears the settings it keeps per person on this device. Push notifications register again after the reload. Plan labels come from a saved copy, so rows you are not using do not make extra calls.

## Add a second account

Adding a second signed-in account is not available yet. Log out signs you out of this session, then you can sign in as someone else.

## Settings on a phone

The phone Settings screen has 17 rows:

- **Accounts** card.
- **Account**: Profile, Billing (shows your plan), Usage, Notifications, Privacy.
- **Assistant**: Customize, Memory, Voice and dictation.
- **Bots and computers**: Connections, Computers and devices, Agent rules.
- **App**: **Haptic feedback** and **Reduce motion**.
- **Appearance** tiles. Light is the default; before this change a new install followed the system and could flash dark on first load.
- **Advanced**, which holds every other page in seven sections.
- **Log out**.

The **ⓘ** menu holds the help and legal links. Open links in a browser and Licenses are not in the web version.
