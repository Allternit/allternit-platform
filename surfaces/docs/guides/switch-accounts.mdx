This page covers two different switches: moving between organizations, and moving between people. Both are in the accounts card at the top of **Settings** on a phone, and in the account menu on the web. In the iPhone app the same card is at the top of **Settings**. Allternit Desktop (the Electron app) does not show the second part.

## Switch organizations

The accounts card lists you and each organization you belong to. Tap a row to switch. The active row has a check, and tapping it opens your profile. Each row shows its plan.

When you switch to another organization, Allternit clears the saved offline sign-in record and the cached lists, then loads the new organization. Plan labels come from a saved copy, so rows you are not using do not make extra calls.

- **Web and PWA:** the page reloads, and push notifications register again.
- **iPhone app:** the lists reload, push notifications register again, and widgets and Spotlight entries are rebuilt. The saved project, computer and Gizzi instance choices are reset, and so are the Decisions, bring-your-own-cloud and Code canvas lists, so you do not see the old organization's data.

## Switch to another person

Allternit remembers the accounts you have signed in to on this device. Below your organizations, the card lists the others with their name, email, picture and plan.

- **Tap one** to sign out and open the sign-in screen with that email already filled in. You then sign in the usual way (code, Apple, GitHub or Google).
- **Add another account** signs you out and opens an empty sign-in screen.
- **Remove from this device** (the ✕ on a row) forgets that account here. It does not delete the account.

Only one account is signed in at a time. Allternit stores no passwords and no sign-in tokens for the other accounts. What it remembers is a user id, a name, an email, a picture, a plan label and when you last used it, up to 8 accounts. The remembered list is kept when you sign out. If the sign-out fails, the screen says so in one line and you stay signed in. The plan label on a row is the one seen last, so it can be out of date.

### What is cleared when the person changes

Because a different person may be using this device, Allternit clears what belongs to the person before it signs out. Everything cleared for an organization switch is cleared here too, plus:

- **Web and PWA:** the settings kept per person in this browser (about 50 entries) and the cached lists.
- **iPhone app:**
  - Brain: the sign-in token, the saved ids and the local copy. Notes not yet sent are moved aside to a folder named `brain-unsent-` and a time, never deleted.
  - The ChatGPT sign-in, which is revoked, and its notice.
  - Voice keys, voice labels and voice opt-outs. Voice sync is off on this phone afterwards.
  - The mesh key for this device, the selected computer, project working folders, and local favorite and archived marks.

An old mesh entry for the iPhone stays on the server until it expires.

### What is kept

- The remembered account list itself, and settings that are not per person, such as Appearance, haptics and Reduce motion.
- Your computers. On the iPhone app the pairing key is stored for each account, so signing back in as the first person finds it again.

### Limits

Only one account is signed in at a time, so you cannot see two accounts side by side. On the web, PWA and phone layout the plan label on a row is the person's own plan. On the iPhone app it is the plan of the active organization at the time.
