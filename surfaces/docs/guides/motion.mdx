## What a motion artifact is

A motion artifact is a short animation made of scenes: a title card, a number
that counts up, a chart that draws itself, a logo, a list that builds line by
line. Allternit writes it from your request, you scrub and edit it in the
artifact window, and you export it as a video.

Motion is for text, numbers, charts and logos. It does not generate footage or
people.

Motion is available on **Super, Ultra and Team** plans. Organization admins
can turn it off for the org; on Enterprise it is off until an admin turns it
on.

## Making one

- Pick **Motion** under the message box, or type `/motion` followed by the
  request: `/motion a 20-second explainer of our Q3 numbers`.
- Or ask in any chat: "make a short animation of this".
- To start from something you already have, open a doc or dashboard artifact,
  open the **Download** menu (the arrow next to **Copy**) and choose **Turn into motion**. Allternit reads the artifact
  and uses only the facts and figures in it.

## Editing

The motion opens in the artifact window with the player on top.

- **Play, pause and loop.** Press the round button to play. Loop is on by
  default.
- **Scrub.** Drag the slider to any moment. Playback pauses while you scrub.
  The small ticks on the slider mark where scenes start.
- **Scenes.** The list shows every scene with its length. Click one to jump to
  it. For the selected scene you can move it up or down, duplicate it, delete
  it or change its type. **Add scene** inserts a new one after it.
- **Edit.** Change the text, numbers, colours and length of the selected
  scene. The preview updates as you type. For charts, edit the labels and
  values as rows. For a logo, paste an https or `data:image` link, or leave it
  empty to show the name as a wordmark.
- **Theme.** Set the background, text, accent and muted colours and the font
  (sans, serif or mono) for the whole motion.
- **Transitions.** Each scene after the first has a transition in: fade, wipe,
  slide or cut.

Edits save by themselves a moment after you stop typing. Each save is a new
version, like any artifact.

### Edit by asking

You can also ask in the chat: "slow scene 2", "change the figure to 1,250",
"use our green as the accent". Allternit changes only what you asked and saves
a new version.

### Reduced motion

If your device is set to reduce motion, the player opens on a still frame and
does not play by itself. You can still scrub and, if you choose, press play.

## Exporting a video

Open **Download → MP4 video** (the arrow next to **Copy**), or press **Export video** under the player. A
progress bar shows the render, and you can cancel it.

- **Allternit Desktop** renders each frame and encodes it with ffmpeg on your
  computer, then asks where to save the file. If ffmpeg isn't installed,
  Desktop encodes in the app like the web does.
- **ai.allternit.com, the phone layout and the m.allternit.com app** encode in
  your browser when it can encode H.264, and you get an MP4. This is the
  fastest way, and nothing leaves your device.
- **When your browser can't encode H.264** (for example an older iPhone or
  iPad), Allternit renders the video in the cloud instead and downloads it
  when it's ready. You don't have to choose.

The video has no sound. The picture matches the preview frame for frame.

### Export MP4 (cloud)

Press **Export MP4 (cloud)** under the player, or **Download → MP4 video
(cloud)**, to render on Allternit's servers even when your browser could do it
itself. It suits long clips and slow or low-power devices. It isn't shown on
Desktop, which renders locally.

1. Allternit saves your latest edits first, because the cloud renders the saved
   version.
2. The status line shows **Waiting for the cloud renderer** (with how many
   videos are ahead of yours) and then **Rendering in the cloud** with a
   percentage. You can cancel at any time.
3. When it's done, the MP4 downloads. Where a **Download MP4** button appears
   instead, press it to save the file.

Cloud export is on the Super, Ultra and Team plans, the same as Motion. The
cloud copy of a finished video is deleted after 24 hours.

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Frame size | 1920×1080 at most, either way round |
| Length | 120 seconds at 30 fps (3600 frames in all; a 60 fps clip can run 60 seconds) |
| Videos rendering at once | 3 per person, including ones waiting |
| Time for one render | 10 minutes, then it's stopped |

If a limit stops an export, the message says what to change: ask Gizzi for a
smaller size, a shorter video or a lower frame rate, or export from Allternit
Desktop, which has no such limit. Two things differ from the preview in a
cloud export: a logo given as a web address (not an embedded image) shows its
name as a wordmark, and the fonts are Allternit's own (Inter, Source Serif and
JetBrains Mono) instead of your device's.

## On a phone

The player fills the top of the screen. Scenes, Edit and Theme are tabs under
it. Everything above works the same, including export. Sharing settings are
only on Desktop and the web.

## Limits

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Scenes | 40 |
| Length of one scene | 0.5 to 60 seconds |
| Length in all | 180 seconds (cloud export: 120 seconds at 30 fps) |
| Chart points | 24 |
| List items | 12 |
| Frame size | 320 to 3840 pixels per side; 1920×1080 by default (cloud export: 1920×1080 at most) |
| Frame rate | 12 to 60; 30 by default |
