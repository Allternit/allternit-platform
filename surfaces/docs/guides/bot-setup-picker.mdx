One picker sets up your main bot and adds bots. You see it as step 4 of [first-run setup](/guides/first-run-setup) and in the Bots rail when you have no main bot yet (the main bot tile opens **Set up your main bot**). It works the same in Allternit Desktop, on ai.allternit.com and in the m.allternit.com app.

The top of the picker is a large stage showing the character you picked, acting on its own: A:// runs through its working shapes, Gizzi glances and winks, the cast blink, talk and work. Under it is a row of tiles, grouped:

| Group | Tiles | What picking one does |
|---|---|---|
| Allternit | A://, Gizzi, Goldie, Rimraf, Yessir, Spicy, Modem | Sets your main bot's look (or a new bot's look). |
| Vendor bots | Claude, Dots, Grok, Muse | Opens that vendor's connect steps. |
| Your bots | Your existing bots | Offers to make that bot your main bot. |
| Add | **+ New bot**, **Import** | Creates a bot, or imports a bot team. |

On a phone the tile row scrolls sideways and brings the tile you pick into view. The details (what your main bot can and can't do, how a vendor connects) open as a sheet from the bottom.

With reduced motion turned on in your system settings, the stage switches instantly and the characters hold still.

## Your main bot

1. Pick a look and type a name (it starts as the character's name).
2. Open **What your main bot can and can't do** to read the rules: it answers you directly or hands a job to the bot that owns it and tells you who has it; it sees the status of your bots' threads (titles and progress), not their conversations, and incognito threads stay private; it never sends, posts, pays, signs or deploys anything without your approval.
3. Select **Set up &lt;Name&gt;**.

Allternit creates your main bot from the main bot template, or reuses the A:// bot you already have, names it, and saves the look as its avatar. Picking A:// keeps the living Allternit orb. To use a bot you already have, pick it under **Your bots** and select **Make &lt;Name&gt; my main bot**.

If setup can't reach your bots (for example, no connection), it says so and nothing is created; try again or select **Not now** / **Skip**.

## Vendor bots

Pick **Claude**, **Dots** (ChatGPT), **Grok** or **Muse** (Meta, over WhatsApp) to bring that vendor's bot onto your team with your own account. The picker shows the same choices as the [vendor bot](/guides/vendor-bots) setup:

- **How to connect**: the vendor's sign-in options, with the recommended one first (for Claude: **Sign in to Claude**, **Connect the Claude desktop app**, **Use your own API key**).
- **It will be able to**: exactly what the connection allows, and whether it uses your own subscription.
- A warning when an option drives the vendor's app through its interface, which can break when the app updates and may conflict with the vendor's terms.

Then:

1. Select the start button (for example **Sign in to Claude**). A sign-in window opens on your Sessions computer; you sign in on the vendor's own site. Allternit never asks for your password or a session token.
2. While the picker waits, finish the sign-in there, then select **I've signed in**. The picker also checks again on its own every few seconds.
3. When the connection is confirmed, the result card lists the bots that joined your Bots rail.

If the vendor asks you to accept its terms first, the picker shows them and **I understand, connect**. If the sign-in doesn't finish, or the vendor shows a verification check, the picker says what happened and that nothing was connected, with **Try again**. Muse needs the WhatsApp details for the conversation it answers in. The Claude desktop app option opens the full step-by-step connection window.

Check **Make it my main bot** to make the first bot that joins your main bot (it's on by default during setup).

## + New bot

Give the bot a name, pick a job (**Researcher**, **Inbox**, **Sales**, **Writer** or **Custom**) and, above, a look. The bot uses your default brain; change it later in the bot's settings. **Create bot** creates it from the matching bot template (Custom starts blank) and opens its main chat. If you had no main bot yet, the first bot you create becomes it, and the result card says so.

## Import

- **From disk** or **From URL** opens the team import window for an OpenMausBot / BotMRR-compatible team file (`.md`, `.yaml` or `.yml`).
- **From a template** lists team templates (for example Content Engine) and opens the template's setup, which creates the bots and lays out the plan as threads in a project.

Imported bots ask before they send, post, pay, sign or deploy anything.
