Paid Allternit plans renew by themselves. This page says when, at what price, and how to stop it. The binding text is in the [Terms of Service](https://ai.allternit.com/terms).

## How renewal works

- A paid plan renews every month at the price shown next to its Subscribe button, until you cancel. The same line appears beside every Subscribe button: "Renews automatically every month at the price until you cancel."
- Credit packs are one-time purchases. They do not renew.
- Auto top-up is separate. It buys credits when your balance runs low, and you turn it off in the same place. See [Credits and auto top-up](/guides/credits-and-auto-top-up).

## Cancel a plan

Where you cancel depends on where you subscribed.

| You subscribed on | Cancel in |
|---|---|
| Web, Desktop or the m.allternit.com app (card, through Stripe) | **Settings → Billing**, then **Cancel in billing portal** |
| iPhone app (Apple) | iPhone **Settings**, your name, **Subscriptions**, then Allternit |
| Android (Google Play) | Google Play, **Payments and subscriptions**, **Subscriptions** |

Cancelling stops the next renewal. The plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. We do not refund or prorate the rest of a period, except where the law requires it. Apple and Google handle refunds for purchases made in their stores.

Deleting your account cancels a Stripe subscription at once and stops future charges. See [Delete your account](/guides/delete-account).

## Age

You must be 18 or older to use Allternit or buy a plan. Sign-in screens say so before you continue. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we close it. The [Privacy Policy](https://ai.allternit.com/privacy) explains what we do when a child's information reaches us.
