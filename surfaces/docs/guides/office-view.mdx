The Office view shows your bots at their desks in the Allternit facility's Office loft, in 3D. Each bot is its own 3D character. You see who is working, who is waiting on you, who is talking, and who is idle, and you can answer and chat without leaving the floor.

The loft fills the screen with one panel over it. With nothing picked, the panel lists your bots, each with its latest line and how long ago it happened. Pick a bot and the same panel becomes its chat. Desktop, ai.allternit.com, the phone layout, the m.allternit.com app and the iPhone app all have it. On a phone the panel is a sheet across the bottom half.

In the iPhone app, open **Office** from the Bots screen header or from the top of the Bots drawer. A message you write in the panel is sent to that bot's main chat, and the reply shows in the panel as it comes. The ⋯ button opens the full chat. Hire, replay, share and the film are on the web and Desktop for now.

## What you can do

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Move around** | Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom. The buttons at the bottom right zoom in, zoom out and reset the view. |
| **Your bots** | With nothing picked, the panel lists your bots. Each row shows the bot's latest event and how long ago it happened. Bots that need you come first, with their question in amber. Search finds a bot by name or role. Your main bot sits at the top. |
| **Select a bot** | Click a bot in the room or in the list. The camera moves to it and the panel becomes that bot: its chat, its Apps, its Routines and a button to its Computer. The back arrow returns to your bots. Click empty floor to let go. |
| **A:// is the orb** | Your main bot, A://, is the Allternit orb, not a character. On the floor it floats above its desk as the eleven ink blocks and coral core, turned to face you, and its face in the panel is the same orb. It moves the way the orb moves everywhere else in the app: the blocks run like a conveyor while it works, breathe while it waits on you, melt toward the core on a call, and hold still when idle. With reduced motion on, it holds still. If you gave A:// your own picture or picked another look, it wears that instead. |
| **Desk screens** | A bot's desk shows what it is doing: its latest status line, or, when the bot has a computer, a small picture of its screen (at most six bots, refreshed every 15 seconds, only while the Office is on screen). |
| **Needs you** | A bot waiting on you walks to the YOU desk and joins the line. Its real question shows on the desk, never the thread title. Answer it from the panel with the same approve, deny or reply actions as anywhere else. The bot goes back to its desk once you answer. |
| **Meetings** | Bots in the same group chat gather at the meeting board. |
| **Hand-offs** | When one bot passes work to another, you see it walk over. Only real hand-offs walk. |
| **Hire** | The **+** next to search, or a free desk, opens a role picker (Support, Finance, Research, Sales, Code, or a vendor bot). The create-bot screen opens set up for it. |
| **Replay** | The timeline button replays a day of events on the floor with a scrub bar, speeds of 1x, 8x, 32x and 120x, and Exit. Replay never changes live state. |
| **While you were out** | Open the Office after eight or more hours away, with three or more events, and a row says what happened with a **Watch** button that replays it. |
| **Share** | The Share button at the top right makes a 1200 by 630 picture of the floor (copy, download or post) and offers the live link. |

An older runtime without the floor routes still shows your team and their live status. Replay and activity history say they need an update.

## Sharing and privacy

The picture is made on your device and shows bot avatars, names and the A:// mark. Nothing is uploaded to make it.

The **live link** is off by default. Turn it on and Allternit gives you a public address that shows your floor read-only, refreshed about every 30 seconds.

What a public floor shows, and only this:

- each bot's name, role line, colour and avatar
- whether it is working, waiting, talking or idle, and when it last changed

What it never shows: task text, questions, thread titles, chat messages, desk screens, files, or who you talk to. The public page has no answer, open, hire or chat controls.

Turn the link off and it stops working at once. If Allternit can't reach the cloud at that moment, the app tells you the link is still turning off and keeps trying until it stops. Turning it on again makes a new address, so an old link stays dead. Anyone with the address can see the floor while it is on, so share it like a link to a page, not like a password.

## For developers

### Floor routes on the runtime

`allternit-api` serves four routes under `/api/v1/floor` (`cmd/allternit-api/src/floor_routes.rs`). Field names are camelCase and match `src/lib/office/floor-types.ts` in allternit-ai.

| Route | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /floor/state` | `{ bots: FloorBotState[] }` for every bot you own. `status` comes from the bot's non-incognito threads: `needs_you` is `waiting`, `working` or `planning` is `working`, otherwise `idle`. `lastKind`, `lastText` and `updatedAt` come from the newest thread event. `meeting` is not set by the runtime. |
| `GET /floor/events?since=&until=&limit=` | `{ events: FloorEvent[] }`, oldest first, times in milliseconds, `limit` up to 2000 (default 500, window default last 24 hours). `thread.started`, `thread.completed`, `thread.failed` and `thread.needs_user` become `started`, `finished`, `failed` and `needs_you`. Bot-to-bot hops from `agent_hops` become `handoff` with `toBotId`. Incognito threads are left out. Event ids are `be:<id>` and `hop:<id>`. |
| `GET /floor/share` and `PUT /floor/share` | `{ enabled, url, revokePending }`. `PUT { enabled }` turns the live link on or off. The runtime must be paired; otherwise `409 runtime_not_paired`. If the cloud can't confirm an off, `PUT` answers `502 revoke_pending`, `revokePending` stays true, and the runtime retries every 60 seconds. |

Migration `V283__floor_share.sql` adds an index on `bot_events(event_type, occurred_at)` and the `floor_share` table (whether the link is on, its token, and a pending revoke).

### Live link

1. `PUT /floor/share { enabled: true }` posts the projection to the cloud at `POST /api/v1/runtime/floor-share`, signed with the same runtime relay signature as `/api/v1/runtime/events`. The cloud answers with a random token (192 bits). The runtime stores it and returns `https://3dfacility.allternit.com/?embed=public&t=<token>`.
2. While the link is on, the runtime pushes the projection every 60 seconds.
3. The cloud (`routes/floor_shares.rs`, table `floor_shares`, migration `115_floor_shares.sql`) rebuilds the projection from an allowlist when it writes, so a runtime cannot store task text even by mistake.
4. `GET /api/v2/public/floors/:token` on the cloud API host (`https://api.allternit.com`) returns the floor with no auth. It is rate limited with the other public routes, answers `404` for an unknown or disabled token, and sends `Cache-Control: private, no-store`, `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` and CORS for `https://3dfacility.allternit.com` only.
5. Turning the link off sets it off locally first, then tells the cloud. If the cloud cannot be reached the call returns `502 revoke_pending` and the runtime retries every 60 seconds until the cloud confirms.

### Frame messages

The app talks to the facility frame by `postMessage`, always checking the origin. App to facility: `allternit:workspace` (bots, with optional `desk` and `meeting`, and `handoffs` deduplicated by id), `allternit:workspace:select`, `allternit:workspace:thread` (a bot's main chat for the panel: `botId`, `lines` with `id`, `from` (`bot` or `you`), plain `text` and `at` in ms, the last 20 messages oldest first, and `working: true` while a reply streams; each post replaces that bot's thread, and a failed send ends with a bot line saying so), `allternit:workspace:replay` (events with `from` and `to`, or `stop: true`) and `allternit:workspace:snapshot`. Facility to app: `ready`, `action` (`answer`, `open`, `select`, `hire`, `share`, and `message` with the `text` written in the facility's own panel) and `allternit:workspace:snapshot` with a PNG data URL. `?embed=workspace&ui=office` is the Office layout: the 3D loft full bleed with the facility's one panel (bots, then the picked bot's chat). Add `&chrome=none` when the app draws that panel itself (Desktop, ai, m), so only the floor and zoom show, and `&actions=chat` when the host handles only `open`, `answer`, `select` and `message` (the iPhone app), which hides hire and share. The iPhone app answers `select` with that bot's `thread` and sends `message` into the bot's main chat without leaving the floor, posting the thread again (at most 4 times a second) as the reply grows and once it ends. A bot whose avatar `sheet` is `https://ai.allternit.com/brand/allternit-orb.svg` is drawn as the orb (the facility's `OrbBody`, with keyframes ported from `AllternitOrb`); the app sends it for the bot `showsPrimaryOrb` picks, and the iPhone app for `isPrimaryTemplateBot`. `?embed=public&t=<token>` is the read-only public floor.
