Current prices are shown in the app and returned by `GET /api/v1/account/compute-status`. The tables below are the current prices and can change.

## Plans with an included computer

Each plan includes one hosted computer.

| Plan | Per month | Computer | Included running hours |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plus | $20 | 2 cores, 8 GB, 50 GB disk | 200 a month |
| Max | $100 | 4 cores, 16 GB, 100 GB disk | not decided yet |
| Ultra | $200 | 8 cores, 32 GB, 200 GB disk | not decided yet |

Sizes and hours for Max and Ultra are not final.

## Computer on credits

A hosted computer billed per minute from your credit balance (rate divided by 60 each minute).

| Size | Running | Stopped |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Medium, 2 cores, 8 GB | $0.10 an hour | $0.005 an hour |

Starting needs at least $5 in credits. See [Computer on credits](/guides/credits-computer).

## Sleep

Hosted computers sleep after 30 idle minutes and wake when an agent needs them. Hosted computers run on a third-party cloud provider, managed by Allternit. When that provider is full, a new computer starts on Allternit's own servers instead, at the same price.

## When credits reach $0

The computer stops and the account shows as suspended. The disk is kept for 14 days, then the computer is deleted. We email you when it stops, after 7 days and the day before it is deleted. Add credits and start the computer again to keep it.

## Free accounts

Free accounts do not get a hosted computer. Free accounts can link their own computer or chat with Allternit Cloud models. See [Choose how agents run](/guides/choose-how-agents-run).
