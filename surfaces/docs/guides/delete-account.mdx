title: Delete your account

You can delete your Allternit account yourself.

## Where

| Where | Steps |
|---|---|
| ai.allternit.com and m.allternit.com | **Settings → Account**, scroll to **Delete account**, then **Delete account…** |
| Allternit for iPhone | **Settings → Account → Delete account** |
| Allternit Desktop | **Settings → Account → Delete account** links to the same page on ai.allternit.com, because Desktop signs in through the app |

Read what happens, type `DELETE` in capitals and choose **Delete my account**.

## What happens

- Your Allternit account is closed and you're signed out on every device.
- You can't sign back in to it. Signing up again with the same email starts a new, empty account.
- Allternit closes the account on its servers and stops using its data. To have stored chats, bots, memory and files erased right away, email privacy@allternit.com.
- Deleting your account doesn't cancel a paid plan. Cancel it in **Settings → Billing** first so you aren't charged again.
- Credits left on the account are not refunded. This can't be undone.

Want a copy first? Export your data from **Settings → Privacy** before you delete.

If the delete doesn't go through, email privacy@allternit.com and we'll delete the account for you.

## For developers

The web section is `src/views/settings/DeleteAccountSection.tsx` in allternit-ai, shown at the end of the Account panel (`ClerkAuthPanel`), which Desktop, ai.allternit.com and the phone layout all use. It calls Clerk's `user.delete()` and signs out. The iPhone app does the same in `Features/Settings/DeleteAccountView.swift`. Clerk must allow users to delete their own account. cloud-api closes the account when Clerk sends the `user.deleted` webhook (`clerk_webhooks.rs`).
