title: Bot routines

A **routine** is work a bot does on a schedule: a morning briefing, a weekly report, a disk check. Each run is a turn in the bot's chat, so the output lands where you already read that bot. Routines run on the Allternit API on your computer, so they keep running with every window closed while the desktop app is running.

## Every routine has a bot

A routine runs as one of your bots, so every create form asks which bot runs it. The bot whose screen you are on is selected by default, otherwise your main bot (the one with the crown at the top of the Bots rail). You can pick any other bot from the list.

If you have no bots yet, the forms tell you to create one first. The API refuses a routine with no bot (`agent_required`), whether it runs on your computer or in the cloud; see [Automation API](/api/automation#routines-and-bots).

## The Routines tab

Open a bot and choose **Routines**. Each routine shows:

- **Schedule in plain words**, for example "Every day at 8:00 AM", "Weekdays at 9:00 AM" or "Every 6 hours".
- **Next run**, or **Paused** if it is not scheduled.
- **Last run result**: when it last ran, and whether it worked.
- **Run now** starts a run at once. The result appears in the bot's chat and in the history a few seconds later.
- **Pause / Resume** switches the schedule off and on.
- **Run history**: select a routine to see its last 10 runs. Each run shows its time, whether you started it, and any error. **Open thread** opens the chat that run wrote to.

Choose **New routine** to open the same scheduled-task form used by Automation Tasks, with this bot already selected. Saving refreshes the bot's routine list. Automation Tasks and the bot's Routines tab manage the same routines; the form includes advanced options such as cron expressions and goals.

The tab is the same on Desktop, on ai.allternit.com (including the phone layout) and in the m.allternit.com app.

### List, Graph and Calendar

The switch at the top of the tab shows the same routines three ways. The tab remembers your choice on this device.

- **List**: the rows described above.
- **Graph**: each routine as a flow from its trigger (schedule, next run, **Run now**) to the bot that runs it, to its last result (**Done**, **Failed** with the error, or **No run yet**). Lines are green after a good run, red after a failed one, and dashed when the routine is paused or has not run. A run is one turn by one bot, so the graph never shows multi-step chains.
- **Calendar**: a calendar you can edit, opening on **Day**. **Week** and **Month** are one click away, with **Today** and back/forward, plus an **Upcoming runs** list for the next two weeks. Run times are worked out from each routine's schedule in your timezone, with the same rules the scheduler uses.

**This bot / All bots** widens Graph and Calendar to every bot's routines. Each calendar entry then shows the bot's avatar, so you can read the whole team's schedule. Selecting a routine in the graph or in **Upcoming runs** opens its details: status, schedule, what it does, which bot runs it, a **Next 7 days** strip with the first run time of each day, and run history with each run's duration. **Run now** and **Pause / Resume** work there too.

### Edit routines on the calendar

Day and Week are hour grids from 6 AM to 10 PM. The grid opens at 7 AM and shows a line at the current time.

- **Add a routine at a time:** click an empty slot. The new routine starts at that time (snapped to the half hour), on weekdays if you clicked a weekday or on that day of the week otherwise. Choose which bot runs it and what it does. A new bot routine offers that bot's example asks as one-tap titles.
- **Change a routine:** click it. You can change which bot runs it, its title and when it runs:
  - **Every day**, **Weekdays** or **Some days** (pick the days), at a time.
  - **Monthly** on a day of the month (1 to 28), at a time.
  - **Every few hours**: every 30 minutes, or every 1, 2, 3 or 4 hours, between a start and an end time, on the days you pick.
  The editor shows the schedule in plain words and the cron it saves. **On / Paused** switches the routine off without deleting it. **Remove** deletes it.
- **Move a routine:** drag it up or down. It moves in 15-minute steps and saves when you let go.

A routine that runs every few hours is drawn as one striped band from its first to its last run, not as a separate entry per run, so a busy day stays readable. **Month** shows a dot per routine on each day it runs. Click a day to open it in Day view.

Changes save to the routine itself. The scheduler recalculates its next run from the new schedule at once. Routines the calendar can't place (at launch, once, manual, or a cron with specific months or uneven times) are listed under the calendar. Click one to open its details. The **New routine** form still offers every option, including raw cron expressions and goals.

On a phone the graph is shown as stacked cards (trigger, then result). The calendar uses single-letter weekdays, and the routine editor opens as a sheet from the bottom of the screen.

The same **List / Graph / Calendar** switch is on every other screen that lists routines, and they share one remembered choice:

- **Automation Tasks** (the rail, the **Routines** page and the phone's Automation Tasks screen), on the **All** and **Routines** tabs. These screens show every bot's routines. Goals, loops and heartbeats stay in **List**, because they have no run times to put on a calendar. The search box also filters the graph and the calendar.
- **Cowork → Routines**, with every bot's routines.
- A bot chat's **Routines** panel, which starts on that bot's routines and offers **This bot / All bots**.

Selecting a run on any of these screens opens the same details panel. The thread links in its run history open that bot's thread. The calendar is editable on all of them, the same way as on a bot's Routines tab. On screens that show every bot's routines, a new routine starts with your first bot selected; pick another under **Who runs it**.

## When a routine fails

Every failed run is recorded and reported, whether or not the routine has a bot, and whether or not an Allternit window is open. You are told in four places:

1. **A push notification** ("Something needs fixing") from your Allternit API, even with every window closed. If you have not fixed it, it is sent again an hour later and then every day at 9:00. Turn it off under Settings → Notifications → **Something needs fixing**.
2. **Inbox → Needs fixing** lists the failure with a **Retry** button (it runs the routine now) and **Dismiss**. The next run that works closes it for you.
3. **A notice at the top of the bot's Routines tab** that names the routine and the error.
4. **The bot's status line in the rail** shows the problem, with the attention badge, until the routine works again.

Each run in the history also says why it did not work: it timed out, the routine had no bot, a subscription needs sign-in, you ran out of quota, the model returned an error, or Allternit restarted mid-run.

### Timeouts

A run that takes longer than the routine's time limit is cut off and fails with the reason "timed out". The limit is 30 minutes unless the routine sets its own. A hung run no longer blocks the routine for hours.

### Missed runs

If your computer was off or asleep when a run was due, the routine runs once when Allternit is back, and the history shows a **Skipped** row for each run that was missed (up to 30 per gap). You get a "Something needs fixing" message saying how many runs were missed, or how late the run started. A run counts as missed when it starts more than 5 minutes late, or more than a tenth of its interval late if that is longer. The message closes when a later run starts on time.

### Automatic pause after 3 failures

After **3 failed runs in a row**, the routine pauses itself so it stops failing on every run. The notice says it was paused and shows the last error. Fix the cause (for example a missing key or an exhausted quota), then choose **Resume**. Resuming gives the routine another 3 tries. A run that works resets the count.

## Routines that need a bot

Routines made before bots were required may have no bot. They never run. They are listed under **Needs a bot** on the Routines tab, and your main bot shows "routines need a bot" in the rail and Inbox. Each one also sends a "Something needs fixing" message once, and stays in the Inbox (as **Needs fixing** items) until you assign a bot or dismiss it.

For each one, choose **Assign to *main bot*** or pick another bot from **Another bot…**. The routine starts on its normal schedule from then on.
