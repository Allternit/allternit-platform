---
title: Your twin
description: One voice and one shared memory for all your bots and their vendor bots: how they speak for you, what they may say, what they know, and where they learned it.
---

Your twin is the part of Allternit that stays the same across every bot you run. Instead of each bot knowing you a little differently, they share:

- **A voice.** The name your bots speak for, how they speak, how they sign, and what they may say about you.
- **A memory.** Facts, preferences, schedule rules, people and decisions, each with where it came from and who may see it.

Nothing is set up until you do it. With an empty twin, bots behave exactly as before.

## Open it

- **Desktop:** the account menu in the rail → **Your twin**, or **Settings → Customize → Your twin**.
- **ai.allternit.com on a phone and the m.allternit.com app:** **Settings → Customize → Your twin**.
- **On a bot's phone:** the **Your twin** app on the home screen.

All of these show the same page.

## Voice

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Name your bots speak for | "You speak for Eoj." |
| How it speaks | Tone and style, in your words. |
| Signature | How messages are signed. |
| What bots may say about you | The disclosure rule. Left empty, bots share nothing about you beyond what the task needs. |

Changes apply from each bot's next turn.

## What your bots know

Each fact has a type (fact, preference, schedule rule, person, decision), an optional subject, and **who can see it**:

| Visibility | Who reads it |
| --- | --- |
| **All bots** | Every bot you run, and the vendor bots (Claude, ChatGPT and others) working for them. |
| **One bot** | Only that bot. Vendor bots never see it. |
| **Only me** | Nobody but you. It is never given to a bot. |

Every fact shows **where it came from**: added by you, or learned by a bot, on which channel, when, and how sure the bot was.

### Suggestions from your bots

When a vendor bot (or any client of your runtime) learns something in a conversation, it **suggests** it: vendor bots do it with the `twin_propose` tool. A suggestion is not used by any bot until you **Accept** it. You can **Edit** it first (saving an edit accepts it) or **Dismiss** it. Up to 50 suggestions can wait for review; after that bots must wait.

## Where the twin is used

- **Every bot turn** the server runs for a bot: channel messages, routines and phone-call replies get the voice and the facts that bot may see.
- **Vendor tickets.** A ticket sent to a vendor app with no connector carries the voice and the shared (all bots) facts in its text. Connected vendor apps are told to call `twin_context` first.
- **Not yet:** the spoken greeting of a phone call is still written by the call worker and does not read the twin; the reply turns do.

## For developers

Routes on the runtime (allternit-api), all signed in as the owner:

| Route | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /api/v1/twin/profile` | `{ "profile": { displayName, speakingStyle, signature, ownerDisclosure, updatedAt } }` |
| `PUT /api/v1/twin/profile` | Partial update; omitted fields are kept. 400 if a field is over 2,000 characters. |
| `GET /api/v1/twin/memory?status=active\|proposed` | `{ "memory": [ { id, kind, subject, content, visibility, botId, status, provenance: { source, botId, channel, threadId, confidence, learnedAt }, createdAt, updatedAt, reviewedAt } ] }` |
| `POST /api/v1/twin/memory` | The owner adds a fact; it is active at once. Body: `{ kind?, subject?, content, visibility?, botId? }`. `botId` is required when `visibility` is `bot`. |
| `POST /api/v1/twin/memory/propose` | A bot suggests a fact (status `proposed`). Also takes `sourceBotId`, `sourceChannel`, `sourceThreadId`, `confidence` (0–1). An identical fact is returned, not duplicated. 429 when 50 are waiting. |
| `PATCH /api/v1/twin/memory/:id` | Edit; also accepts a proposal. |
| `POST /api/v1/twin/memory/:id/accept` | Accept as is. |
| `DELETE /api/v1/twin/memory/:id` | Delete or dismiss. |
| `GET /api/v1/twin/context[?botId=]` | Preview of what a bot (with `botId`) or a vendor bot (without) is told: `{ configured, context }`. |

Vendor connector tools: `twin_context` (read only) returns `{ configured, context }` for the vendor audience: the voice plus **All bots** facts. `twin_propose` `{ content, kind?, subject?, visibility?: all|bot, confidence? }` suggests a fact; it is always a proposal, never `owner`-only, and a `bot` fact is limited to that vendor bot.
