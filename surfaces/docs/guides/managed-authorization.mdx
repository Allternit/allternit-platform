---
title: Managed authorization for your organization
description: "Org admins turn on sign-in-free connectors for members, and let members of a trusted Claude or Okta org reach Allternit without a second sign-in."
---

Managed authorization lets an organization decide who can use which connector, so members don't each sign in to every app. It works in two directions:

- **Allternit signs members in to a connector.** An org admin turns it on for a connector. When a member connects, Allternit vouches for them to the connector's sign-in server, and the connector is connected with no sign-in screen.
- **Another app's org signs members in to Allternit.** An org admin trusts an outside issuer (a Claude organization, an Okta tenant). Members of that org who are also members of your Allternit org reach [your connectors from other AI apps](/guides/connectors-in-other-apps) and the agents server without signing in to Allternit again.

Both follow the MCP extension [Enterprise-Managed Authorization](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/extensions/auth/enterprise-managed-authorization) (an "ID-JAG": a short signed note saying who the user is, for one app).

## Who can change it

Only **org admins** of your Allternit organization can turn these on or off. Other members see the settings read-only. Someone who isn't in an organization only sees the [other apps](/guides/connectors-in-other-apps) card.

## Turn it on for a connector

The connector's own sign-in server has to trust Allternit first. Ask the vendor (or your own team, for a custom MCP server) to add your org's **issuer URL**, shown with a copy button in the panel. It looks like:

```text
https://api.allternit.com/oauth2/org/<your org id>
```

The vendor gives you a **client ID** (and usually a **client secret**) for Allternit. Then, in **Settings › Connectors**, open the connector's **Managed authorization** panel and fill in:

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| Roles | Which org roles may use it (for example admin and member). |
| Scopes | What Allternit asks the connector for. |
| Client ID, client secret | What the vendor gave you. The secret is stored sealed and never shown again. |

Press **Save & turn on**. For a server that isn't in the directory, use **Custom MCP server** and also enter its sign-in server (issuer), token address and MCP address.

Allternit doesn't mark any directory connector as ready for this until that vendor documents that it accepts an outside issuer. Until then every connector needs the vendor's setup above.

## What members see

A member whose role is allowed presses **Connect** as usual and is connected with no sign-in screen. The token renews itself; nobody is asked again.

If it fails, Allternit says why in plain words (for example "Your role in the organization isn't allowed to use managed sign-in for this connector" or "Verify the email address on your Allternit account") and offers **Connect with sign-in instead**, the connector's normal sign-in. Allternit never switches to that by itself.

Managed sign-in is only issued to a member whose email address is verified, whose role is allowed, and only while the setting is on. Turn it off and members' next renewal fails; they can still connect with their own sign-in.

## Trust another app's organization

Open **Settings › Connectors › Outside AI apps** and add an issuer:

- **Claude:** your Claude organization's issuer URL, from Claude's organization settings.
- **Okta:** your Okta tenant's issuer URL.
- **Other:** any issuer that follows the extension, with its keys address if it isn't the usual one.

Pick what its members may reach: **Connectors**, **Agents**, or both. Bots stay per person. A member is matched to an Allternit account only by a verified email address that belongs to exactly one member of your org. No match, or more than one, and the sign-in is refused; Allternit never guesses.

Claude's own issuer format is not published yet. Allternit implements the extension as written and treats Claude-issued sign-ins as checked only once Claude's connector setup guide confirms the claims it sends.
