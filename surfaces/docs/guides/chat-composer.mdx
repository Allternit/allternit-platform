These work the same in Allternit Desktop, on ai.allternit.com (including its phone layout) and in the m.allternit.com app.

## The text box

The text box is a white card. When you reply to something, or attach files on Desktop, a soft grey band appears around it: the reply strip above, the attachment cards below. A thin line separates what you type from the controls:

- **Add files** opens the menu for files, folders, screenshots, GitHub and tools. On a phone it is the round **+** button. See [Attachments](/guides/attachments).
- The **model** name opens the model picker.
- The **microphone** starts voice mode.
- The **send** button stays grey until there is something to send.

## Reply to part of a reply

Choose **Reply** under any reply, or select some text in a reply and choose **Reply** in the bar that appears. A **Replying to** strip shows the quote above the text box. Press **✕** to cancel it.

When you send, the quote goes at the top of your message as `Replying to:` and a quoted block, so the agent sees exactly what you mean. Your message in the conversation shows the quote above your words. The quote is part of the message text, so it stays after a reload and on your other devices. Quotes longer than 600 characters are shortened.

## Answering an agent question

When an agent asks you something, the question card shows the question, then numbered choices:

- Click a choice, or press its number key (**1**–**9**) on Desktop, to answer. A single-choice question is answered as soon as you pick.
- Questions that allow several answers or your own words have a **Next** or **Send** button.
- **Skip question** skips only that question. When several are asked, the dots show where you are, and you can click an earlier dot to go back.
- **✕** declines the whole set.

The agent receives the text of the choices you picked.

## While an answer is being made

- A small rounded pill shows what the agent is doing now, such as "Creating report.csv".
- While the agent writes a file, a grey placeholder in the file's shape holds its place: a table for CSV and spreadsheets, a page for documents, lines for code, a frame for web pages. After 3 seconds a soft light runs around its edge. With reduced motion turned on, nothing moves.

## Interactive answers

Some replies include a small interactive card: numbers that update as you move a slider, a table, a chart, or numbered options. Moving a slider or changing a number updates the card on your device without asking the agent again. Buttons and choices on the card send your pick as your next message.
