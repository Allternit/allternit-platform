These work the same in Allternit Desktop, on ai.allternit.com (including its phone layout) and in the m.allternit.com app.

## The text box

The text box is a white card. When you reply to something, or attach files on Desktop, a soft grey band appears around it: the reply strip above, the attachment cards below. A thin line separates what you type from the controls:

- **Add files** opens the menu for files, folders, screenshots, GitHub and tools. On a phone it is the round **+** button. See [Attachments](/guides/attachments).
- The **model** name opens the model picker.
- The **microphone** starts voice mode.
- The **send** button stays grey until there is something to send.

## Reply to part of a reply

Choose **Reply** under any reply, or select some text in a reply and choose **Reply** in the bar that appears. A **Replying to** strip shows the quote above the text box. Press **✕** to cancel it.

When you send, the quote goes at the top of your message as `Replying to:` and a quoted block, so the agent sees exactly what you mean. Your message in the conversation shows the quote above your words. The quote is part of the message text, so it stays after a reload and on your other devices. Quotes longer than 600 characters are shortened.

## Actions under a reply

Under every reply you can copy it, reply to it, have it read aloud, and rate it.

- **Retry** (the circular arrow) asks again and replaces the latest reply where it is. The reply it replaced is kept: use the arrows **‹ 2/2 ›** to flip between versions. The next message you send continues from the newest version.
- **Retry with…** (the small arrow next to Retry) opens the model picker. The retry goes to the model you pick, for that one retry only. Your chat's model doesn't change.
- **Thumbs up / down** rate the reply. After a thumbs-down, pick what went wrong: Wrong, Too long, Wrong format or Other. Press the same thumb again to clear it.
- **Follow-ups** appear under the latest reply when the answer suggests a next step (or the model sends suggestions). They're always shown; click one to send it. There are no canned suggestions.

Older versions are kept while the app is open; they aren't saved to your account. A message that included images can't be retried; send it again instead. Retry works in Chat, Cowork, Code and bot threads, on Desktop, ai.allternit.com (including phones) and the m.allternit.com app. Bots that run in their own vendor app don't offer Retry.

## Answering an agent question

When an agent asks you something, the thread shows **Asking a question** and the question card sits just above the text box. It works the same in Chat, Cowork, Code, project threads, bot threads and on phones:

- The top row shows the question, **‹ 1 of 2 ›** when several are asked (the arrows go back and forward), and **✕**, which declines the whole set.
- Each choice is a numbered row with a short explanation under it. The first row starts highlighted.
- Click a choice, or press its number key (**1**–**9**) on Desktop, to answer. The arrow keys move the highlight and **Enter** picks it. A single-choice question is answered as soon as you pick.
- **Something else** at the bottom takes your own words. Press **Enter** to send them.
- **Skip** skips only that question.
- Questions that allow several answers show a tick on each pick and a **Next** or **Send** button.

The agent receives the text of the choices you picked, or what you typed.

For a consequential call the agent can ask a **decision tree** instead: options branch to follow-up questions and outcomes, and you answer with a path. See [Decisions and decision trees](/guides/decisions).

Agents that run on an installed CLI brain (Claude CLI, Codex, Kimi and other CLI agents) ask with the same card. They end their turn when they ask, and your answers are sent as your next message, so they pick up from there.

The thread updates while that next turn runs. If the agent then needs your approval, for example to run a command, Deny and Allow appear on that step in the reply, as they do for any other turn.

## Several cards at once

Questions, approvals and suggestions don't block each other. Each card works on its own:

- An approval for a step in the reply shows on that step, with Deny and Allow. Every other pending approval shows above the text box, not only the first.
- When agents ask more than one question, the question card shows one at a time with tabs to switch. A half-typed answer stays put while you answer another.
- Answering, approving or declining one card changes only that card. The others stay as they are, and the reply keeps running.
- A decided card stays decided. It does not come back as pending when the thread reloads or new text streams in.
- In the Inbox, choosing **Don't send** on an email draft drops only that draft. Nothing in the chat changes.

## While an answer is being made

- The steps show above the reply as they run, then fold into one line such as "Used 4 tools · 12s". See [Agent steps in replies](/guides/agent-steps).
- When the agent keeps a plan, a chip at the top right reads **Step 2 of 3** and opens [Progress](/guides/progress-pane) in the right pane.
- A small rounded pill shows what the agent is doing now, such as "Creating report.csv".
- While the agent writes a file, a grey placeholder in the file's shape holds its place: a table for CSV and spreadsheets, a page for documents, lines for code, a frame for web pages. After 3 seconds a soft light runs around its edge. With reduced motion turned on, nothing moves.

## Sending while the agent is working

You don't have to wait for a turn to end. While the agent is working, the text box says **Add to this turn…** and the **send** button comes back as soon as you type, attach a file or drop one in. With nothing typed, the button is **Stop**.

- **Steer:** in Chat, Cowork and Code, a message you send during a turn goes into that turn. It shows in the thread at once, and the agent reads it at its next step, so it can change course without starting over.
- **Queue:** if the message can't go into the turn (the chat has no saved session yet, the message has images, or the server turns it down), it waits above the text box as **Queued**, and everything queued is sent as one message when the turn ends. Press **✕** on the Queued strip to cancel it.
- Files you attach while the agent is working open in their editor on send, just like at any other time. See [Files open in their editor](/guides/attachments#files-open-in-their-editor).

Bot threads already worked this way. See [Bot threads](/guides/bot-threads).

## Interactive answers

Some replies include a small interactive card: numbers that update as you move a slider, a table, a chart, or numbered options. Moving a slider or changing a number updates the card on your device without asking the agent again. Buttons and choices on the card send your pick as your next message. Cards can also show live data from your connected apps; one that would change something asks you first. Turn cards off in Settings → Response style → **Layout and visuals** → **Text only**. More: [Interactive answers](/guides/interactive-answers).

Every reply follows a fixed layout for its kind of question (answer first, then the details), and Settings → **Response style** sets how long it is. See [How answers are laid out](/guides/interactive-answers#how-answers-are-laid-out).
