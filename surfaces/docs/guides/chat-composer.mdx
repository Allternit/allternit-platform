These work the same in Allternit Desktop, on ai.allternit.com (including its phone layout) and in the m.allternit.com app.

## The text box

At rest the text box is one line: the **+** button, a line to type on, the **mic** and the **voice** button. Click or tap it and it grows to the full card. When you reply to something, or attach files on Desktop, a soft grey band appears around it: the reply strip above, the attachment cards below. Clicking the box does not draw a focus ring. A ring shows only when you reach the box with the keyboard.

The **Runtime · Brain** chip sits on the card's top border at the right. It is there in every mode (Home, Bots, Code and ACI), at rest and when the box is open. It shows the runtime's mark and name, then the brain, with a status dot: green when ready, amber when a step is needed, coral while the agent is running. Tap it to open the **Runtime and brain** selector. The selector has a **Runtime** row and a **Brain** row, each with the provider's logo. In a bot chat it also has a **Bot** row. In Code the chip shows Code's own runtime (the paired computer or coding agent) and its model. With no brain picked yet it reads **Pick a brain**. There is no default brain, so send then keeps your message, opens the selector and says **Pick a brain first**. Once you pick one, it stays your brain on every surface until you change it.

The **Agent mode | Plugins | Artifacts** deck is a tray tucked under the composer card. It shows when you open **Tools**.

The box is open while it has focus or a draft. Then these show:

- **+** is the one add button. It opens the menu for files, folders, screenshots, GitHub and the computer. See [Attachments](/guides/attachments). **Draw a sketch** (on a phone, **Sketch**) opens a white canvas with pen, fine, marker, eraser, colors and undo. **Attach** adds the drawing as an image. The iPhone app uses Apple's drawing tools.
- The **sliders** button opens **Tools**. It shows only while the box is open.
- The **games** button shows only while the box is open.
- The **mic** dictates into the draft; see [Voice](/guides/voice#dictation-on-a-phone). If dictation fails, one plain line says so.
- The **voice** button (the waveform) starts voice mode. While there is text in the box, it becomes the **send** button. Send stays grey until there is something to send.

In Bots mode the **To:** recipient sits on the mode orb's row, to the right of the orb.

There is no Output menu. To ask for a kind of result, pick it in **Agent mode**, which lists every kind (Docs, Sheets, Slides, PDF, Page, Dashboard, Ops sheet, Design, Image, Video, Motion, App, Build, Deep Research) beside Agent (auto) and Agent Swarm. A picked artifact mode shows an **It can** row above the deck tray with the toggles that kind can use, and the placeholder changes to match (for Slides, "Describe the slides…"). Send starts a new artifact session. See [Artifact modes](/guides/artifact-modes).

## Mode orb and the Shelves hub

The round **mode orb** sits at the composer's top left. Tap it and it opens into a pill with the current mode. Hold it for about half a second and the **Shelves hub** opens: a panel with a section for each thing in the mode you are in (for example Recents, Projects, Artifacts, Automation Tasks, Meetings, Customize and **Freeweights** in Home; Main bot, Bots, Group chats, Office and Projects in Bots). Each section shows one item as a card. Open a section for up to four items and a **More** row.

Slide across the modes at the top of the hub to switch mode. The hub is the only mode switch on a phone. The orb is hidden in the HUD composer and in the Bots-only app.

## The New orb

When a session is open, the **New** orb sits at the composer's top right. Tap it and it opens into a pill with two items: the main new item for the mode (**New chat** in Home, **New thread** in Bots and Code, **New session** in ACI), and **Quick task**, which opens a small composer over the screen you are on. In the Desktop app the pill shows the ⌘N shortcut. The orb is hidden while the hub or Tools is open. The phone header has no New button.

## On a phone

These changes apply to the phone layout on ai.allternit.com and in the m.allternit.com app (including the Android app from Google Play). The hub rises from the bottom edge at full width.

- **Tools are behind the sliders button.** There is no bar under the text box. The **sliders** and **games** buttons show only while the box is open. Tap **sliders** to open Tools. Your choice of Agent mode and plugins is remembered on this device.
- **Permission is in +.** The permission pill is gone from the row. Open **+** and choose **Permission · Auto ›** to pick **Auto**, **Ask** or **Read-only**. The choice is the same one as before (see [Chat sessions](/guides/chat-sessions#approval)). The page links to the permission defaults.
- **Add to chat** sheet. **+** opens a sheet that groups the ways to add: files and photos (the **Photos** link in the title row opens the file picker, because a web page cannot read your camera roll), the **Computer** row and **Permission**.
- **Computer chip.** There is no strip at the top. A chip above the text box shows when there is no computer connected (**Connect**), when your computer cannot be reached (**Fix**), while it wakes, or when the compute gate applies. **✕** shrinks it to a dot on **+**; the dot clears when the state changes. The same chip shows in Chat, Bots and Code. Screens with no text box show an empty state with a **Connect** action.
- **Chat ⋯ menu.** **Run on my phone** and **Capture to brain** moved from the sheet to the **⋯** menu at the top of a chat. The menu also has **Pin** and **Archive** for a saved chat.
- **Modes.** **☰** only opens the drawer. The mode icon at the top of the drawer opens the Shelves hub.

On iPhone Safari there is no vibration, so the haptic taps are silent there. See [Motion and haptics](/guides/motion#phone-motion-and-haptics).

On a phone, tapping outside the composer closes the keyboard and the text box shrinks back to one line when the draft is empty. The drawer and the page beside it no longer clip on the left edge.

## The model picker

The picker opens from the **Brain** row of the **Runtime and brain** selector. Each provider shows its logo. It lists every model you can use, grouped by provider. Search narrows the list. Nothing is hidden behind the search.

- **Desktop and the web:** the providers are on the left, each with a ring for its usage limit. The ring is green, then amber at 80%, then red at 95%. The models are in the middle. The model you point at is shown below, with its thinking setting and its account.
- **Phone and the PWA:** search is on top, with the provider rail on the left (each provider with its ring) and its models beside it. The model you point at is shown at the foot with its thinking setting. The iPhone app groups Allternit Cloud models by vendor and adds **Reasoning** and **Local** filter chips and a status dot on each provider.
- Each provider's own default model carries a **Default** tag. Models inside a provider are in the vendor's order, newest and strongest first. The app never picks a model for you. A saved choice of a model that has been retired moves to its replacement.

Each model shows its vendor's own thinking setting, with the vendor's word and levels:

| Vendor | Setting | Levels |
|---|---|---|
| Claude | Effort | Low, Medium, High, Extra high, Max |
| OpenAI (Codex) | Reasoning | low to max, and ultra on some models |
| Google Gemini | Thinking level | low to high |
| DeepSeek | Deep thinking (can be off) | Low, High, Max |
| Z.ai GLM | Deep thinking | Low, High, Max |

Drag the slider, click a level, or use the arrow keys. A model chip in the list, such as **Effort · 5**, says how many levels the model has. A model without a thinking setting says so. The level you pick goes with every message to that model. Picking a different model starts at that model's default. For the full table, see [Thinking levels](/api/providers#thinking-levels).

## Reply to part of a reply

Choose **Reply** under any reply, or select some text in a reply and choose **Reply** in the bar that appears. A **Replying to** strip shows the quote above the text box. Press **✕** to cancel it.

When you send, the quote goes at the top of your message as `Replying to:` and a quoted block, so the agent sees exactly what you mean. Your message in the conversation shows the quote above your words. The quote is part of the message text, so it stays after a reload and on your other devices. Quotes longer than 600 characters are shortened.

## Actions under a reply

Under every reply you can copy it, reply to it, have it read aloud, and rate it.

- **Retry** (the circular arrow) asks again and replaces the latest reply where it is. The reply it replaced is kept: use the arrows **‹ 2/2 ›** to flip between versions. The next message you send continues from the newest version.
- **Retry with…** (the small arrow next to Retry) opens the model picker. The retry goes to the model you pick, for that one retry only. Your chat's model doesn't change.
- **Thumbs up / down** rate the reply. After a thumbs-down, pick what went wrong: Wrong, Too long, Wrong format or Other. Press the same thumb again to clear it.
- **Follow-ups** appear under the latest reply when the answer suggests a next step (or the model sends suggestions). They're always shown; click one to send it. There are no canned suggestions.

Older versions are kept while the app is open; they aren't saved to your account. A message that included images can't be retried; send it again instead. Retry works in Chat, Code and bot threads, on Desktop, ai.allternit.com (including phones) and the m.allternit.com app. Bots that run in their own vendor app don't offer Retry.

## Plus menu, effort level and greeting

- **The plus button** turns into an x while its menu is open. On web the menu grows from the button. The rows do not change.
- **The effort slider** in the model picker marks the default level with a **Default** pill. The top level shows a small animated picture. It stays still with reduced motion. A cost line shows under the slider **only when the model's prices are known** from the server. When there is no price, there is no line.
- **The greeting** reads "Hello, night owl" between 11 pm and 5 am, by your device clock. A soft neutral wash rises behind the empty chat. On a phone the greeting moves to the middle again when the keyboard opens.

## Dictation

Tap the mic in the text box. A panel opens above the box with a thin live line that follows your voice and a glow around the edge. It has three buttons:

- **Cancel** throws the dictation away and puts your draft back as it was.
- **Stop** puts the words in the box.
- **Keep mine** stops and keeps the words exactly as heard, with no undo.

After **Stop**, an **Undo dictation** pill shows for a few seconds. Tap it to restore the draft you had before. It goes away on send. The live line asks for no new permission: if the mic is not already allowed, the line stays flat and calm. Dictation does not rewrite your words.

## Follow-up pills

When a reply ends with a next step, one to three pills sit under the newest reply, on the right. Tap one and it floats up and sends. The pills are hidden while the reply is still coming in and on older replies. **They show only when the reply includes follow-ups.** On iPhone they come from a closing "Next step:" line, and Android does not show them. A pill that fails to send comes back with a short message.

## Answering an agent question

When an agent asks you something, the thread shows **Asking a question** and the question card sits just above the text box. It works the same in Chat, Code, project threads, bot threads and on phones:

- The top row shows the question, **‹ 1 of 2 ›** when several are asked (the arrows go back and forward), and **✕**, which declines the whole set.
- Each choice is a numbered row with a short explanation under it. The first row starts highlighted.
- Click a choice, or press its number key (**1**–**9**) on Desktop, to answer. The arrow keys move the highlight and **Enter** picks it. A single-choice question is answered as soon as you pick.
- **Something else** at the bottom takes your own words. Press **Enter** to send them.
- **Skip** skips only that question.
- Questions that allow several answers show a tick on each pick and a **Next** or **Send** button.

The agent receives the text of the choices you picked, or what you typed.

For a consequential call the agent can ask a **decision tree** instead: options branch to follow-up questions and outcomes, and you answer with a path. See [Decisions and decision trees](/guides/decisions).

Agents that run on an installed CLI brain (Claude CLI, Codex, Kimi and other CLI agents) ask with the same card. They end their turn when they ask, and your answers are sent as your next message, so they pick up from there.

The thread updates while that next turn runs. If the agent then needs your approval, for example to run a command, Deny and Allow appear on that step in the reply, as they do for any other turn.

## Several cards at once

Questions, approvals and suggestions don't block each other. Each card works on its own:

- An approval for a step in the reply shows on that step, with Deny and Allow. Every other pending approval shows above the text box, not only the first.
- When agents ask more than one question, the question card shows one at a time with tabs to switch. A half-typed answer stays put while you answer another.
- Answering, approving or declining one card changes only that card. The others stay as they are, and the reply keeps running.
- A decided card stays decided. It does not come back as pending when the thread reloads or new text streams in.
- In the Inbox, choosing **Don't send** on an email draft drops only that draft. Nothing in the chat changes.

## While an answer is being made

- The steps show above the reply as they run, then fold into one line such as "Used 4 tools · 12s". See [Agent steps in replies](/guides/agent-steps).
- When the agent keeps a plan, a chip at the top right reads **Step 2 of 3** and opens [Progress](/guides/progress-pane) in the right pane.
- A small rounded pill shows what the agent is doing now, such as "Creating report.csv".
- While the agent writes a file, a grey placeholder in the file's shape holds its place: a table for CSV and spreadsheets, a page for documents, lines for code, a frame for web pages. After 3 seconds a soft light runs around its edge. With reduced motion turned on, nothing moves.

## Sending while the agent is working

You don't have to wait for a turn to end. While the agent is working, the text box says **Add to this turn…** and the **send** button comes back as soon as you type, attach a file or drop one in. With nothing typed, the button is **Stop**.

- **Steer:** in Chat and Code, a message you send during a turn goes into that turn. It shows in the thread at once, and the agent reads it at its next step, so it can change course without starting over.
- **Queue:** if the message can't go into the turn (the chat has no saved session yet, the message has images, or the server turns it down), it waits above the text box as **Queued**, and everything queued is sent as one message when the turn ends. Press **✕** on the Queued strip to cancel it.
- Files you attach while the agent is working open in their editor on send, just like at any other time. See [Files open in their editor](/guides/attachments#files-open-in-their-editor).

Bot threads already worked this way. See [Bot threads](/guides/bot-threads).

## Interactive answers

Some replies include a small interactive card: numbers that update as you move a slider, a table, a chart, or numbered options. Moving a slider or changing a number updates the card on your device without asking the agent again. Buttons and choices on the card send your pick as your next message. Cards can also show live data from your connected apps; one that would change something asks you first. Turn cards off in Settings → Response style → **Layout and visuals** → **Text only**. More: [Interactive answers](/guides/interactive-answers).

Every reply follows a fixed layout for its kind of question (answer first, then the details), and Settings → **Response style** sets how long it is. See [How answers are laid out](/guides/interactive-answers#how-answers-are-laid-out).
