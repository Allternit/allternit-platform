You can share one folder (or one file) from the Files drive on your computer with someone who has no Allternit account. They open a link in a browser and see only that folder. Everything else on your computer stays private.

<Note>
Sharing ships dark with the Files drive. It works only when the `files_drive` flag is on for your account and your computer runs the Files drive. The Share button in the Files tab arrives with the Files app on each surface. Until then, shares are made through the API in [Files Drive API](/api/files-drive#sharing-and-the-guest-gateway).
</Note>

## What you can share

- **One folder or one file per link.** The guest sees that item and everything inside it, never its parent or the folders next to it.
- **Only folders you allow.** A folder is shareable when its sharing setting is on. Folders you add start with sharing off. Protected folders (the ones that ask for Face ID or Windows Hello) can never be shared.
- **Your computer's files, live.** The guest reads from your computer through Allternit. Nothing is copied to the cloud, except drops into an upload-only link while your computer sleeps (see below).

## Roles

Each link has one role:

| Role | The guest can |
|---|---|
| View and download | browse the folder, open and download files |
| Comment | everything in view, plus read and add comments |
| Edit | everything in comment, plus add files, make folders and replace files |
| Upload only | add new files, nothing else. They can't see what is in the folder, can't replace a file, and a name clash keeps both files. |

No role can delete files, share again, or let an agent act on the folder. Sharing a folder never turns on agent access to it.

## Make a link

Choose the folder, the role and the options:

- **Expiry** (optional). After this time the link stops working.
- **Passcode** (optional, 4 to 64 characters). The guest types it once when opening the link. Allternit keeps only a slow hash of it, never the passcode itself, so it can't be shown again. If you forget it, make a new link.
- **Let guests wake my computer** (off by default). When it's off and your computer is asleep, the guest sees "The owner's computer is asleep. Try again later." When it's on, a guest's visit wakes your cloud computer, and the time it runs is billed to you like any other wake.

You get a link like `https://ai.allternit.com/s/...`. Send it however you like. Allternit does not email it for you.

## What the guest sees

The guest opens the link and sees the folder name, your display name, and whether your computer is awake. If the link has a passcode, they type it. Then they browse the folder like a small file browser.

- A guest session lasts at most an hour, and never past the link's expiry. It can be renewed without typing the passcode again.
- The guest never sees where the folder is on your computer, your other folders, your devices, or who pinned what.
- Ten wrong passcodes in 15 minutes lock the link for 15 minutes.

### Upload-only links while your computer sleeps

An upload-only drop to a sleeping computer is not lost. Allternit keeps the file in your own cloud storage, waiting for your computer, and records who sent it. Moving waiting files onto your computer when it wakes comes with the Files app. Each link holds up to 100 waiting files, each up to 4 MB.

## See who opened it

Every guest visit is recorded: opening the link, listing, downloading, uploading, commenting and waking, whether it was allowed or refused, and why. You can read the record for each link.

## Turn a link off

- **Turn off the link.** Everyone using it is stopped on their very next click, even with a session already open. The link stays in your list, marked turned off.
- **Stop one guest.** Each person who opens the link gets their own guest record. You can stop one of them and leave the link working for everyone else.
- **Remove the computer.** If you remove a computer from your account, every link to its files stops working.
