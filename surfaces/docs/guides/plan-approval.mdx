---
title: "Edit and approve an agent's plan"
description: "Change, add, remove or reorder an agent's plan steps before it starts, then watch the approved steps run."
---

When an agent plans before it works, it shows you its plan as a card of numbered steps. You can change the plan on the card before the agent starts. The agent then runs the steps exactly as you left them.

## Where the card shows up

The card sits above the text box in Chat, Code, project threads and bot threads, and in **Tasks → Decisions**. It works the same on Desktop, on ai.allternit.com (including the phone layout) and in the m.allternit.com app.

A plan shows up as a card when:

- The agent switched to plan mode (it asks first) and finished its plan.
- The session's model is an installed coding CLI that hands its plan to Gizzi. See [Plan mode with an installed CLI model](/cli/plan#plan-mode-with-an-installed-cli-model).

## Change the plan

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Reword a step | Click the step's text and type. |
| Move a step | Click the up or down arrow on its row. |
| Remove a step | Click **×** on its row. |
| Add a step | Click **Add a step** under the list, then type it. |

Empty steps are dropped when you approve. A plan holds at most 50 steps, and each step holds at most 500 characters.

## Approve or keep planning

- **Approve plan**: the agent switches to building and runs your steps in order.
- **Approve + allow sends**: the same, and the plan's messages and file uploads go out without asking again for this task. Payments, signatures and deletions still ask.
- **Keep planning**: the agent stays in plan mode. Reply with notes to revise the plan.

## Open the full plan

The card shows only the steps. When the plan is also open as a plan page, the card has an **Open full plan** link. The page shows the whole plan with its sections, and it has the same step list to edit. You can answer on the card or on the page. The first answer counts, and the other one closes.

On Desktop, the plan page doesn't open a browser tab by itself. Open it from the card when you want it.

## Watch the steps run

The approved steps become the session's to-do list. The thread's live plan shows each step as pending, running or done while the agent works, and the Console **Plan** tab in Code shows the same list.

If you changed the plan, the agent is told that you edited it and runs your list in place of the plan file's list.

## For developers

- The agent passes `steps` (`[{ title, detail? }]`) to Gizzi's `plan_exit` tool. Without `steps`, Gizzi reads the plan file's top-level numbered items (or bullets if nothing is numbered). Fewer than two items gives the plain question with no step list.
- The question carries `plan: { steps }`. The reply to `POST /api/v1/questions/{id}/reply` may carry `plan: [{ title, detail? }]` with the edited steps. allternit-api forwards only well-formed steps within the limits above, and Gizzi checks them again.
- `POST /v1/question/{id}/plan-page` with `{ url }` on Gizzi links a pending plan question to a page that shows the same plan. Only http and https links of at most 2,000 characters are kept. The link appears as `plan.page` in the question list, and the card shows it as **Open full plan**. On ai.allternit.com, a link to `127.0.0.1` shows only in a browser on the same machine.
- The answer-me plan bridge (a Gizzi plugin) uses this route. It shows the question's `plan.steps` as an editable list on its page, and its reply carries the edited steps as `plan`.
- Clients that don't draw the card (the terminal UI, iOS, bot channels) answer the same question with its options, and the agent runs the steps as proposed.
