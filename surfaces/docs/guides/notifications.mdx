---
title: Notifications
description: Which Allternit events notify you on your phone or in your browser, what they say, and how to turn each one on or off.
---

Allternit can notify you when something needs you, even while the app is closed. It works the same on Allternit Desktop (while it's running), ai.allternit.com and the m.allternit.com phone app. First turn notifications on for each device: see [Call and message notifications](/guides/call-notifications#turn-it-on).

## What notifies you

Each kind of event has its own switch. These are on until you turn them off:

| Event | What you see | Tapping it opens |
|---|---|---|
| **Approval requests** | "Approval needed: Olive wants to: send the invoice email" | The thread, when there is one |
| **Threads that need you** | "Olive needs you", with the question or reason | That thread |
| **New messages** | The sender and the start of the message. Messages in a direct thread with someone you invited show the same way | That thread, or the bot phone for that channel |
| **Ended calls** | "Missed call from Sam", or "Call ended" after your bot finishes a call for you | The bot phone |
| **AI subscription needs sign-in** | "Sign in to (subscription name) again" | Allternit |
| **Usage alerts** | "You've used 80% of your monthly cloud budget" | Allternit |
| **Something needs fixing** | "Something needs fixing: \"Morning briefing\" failed: …", or a routine that was missed or has no bot | The bot, or Allternit |

These are off until you turn them on:

| Event | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Finished agent runs** | "Olive finished a run", or that it failed |
| **Approval decisions** | "Approval approved". It replaces the "Approval needed" notification |
| **New inbox items** | The item's title |
| **AI subscription signed in again** | "(subscription name) is signed in again". It replaces the sign-in notification |
| **New vendor tickets** | "New ticket for your vendor bot" |

**Something needs fixing** is sent by your Allternit API itself, not by an open window, so it reaches you with every window closed. A routine that fails, a routine that was missed because your computer was off or asleep, a routine with no bot, and a run whose result could not be saved each open one problem. Opening the same problem again does not send a second message. Until it is fixed you get the message again an hour later, then every day at 9:00 in your computer's time zone. A problem that needs you right away (a routine flagged critical, or one that paused itself) says "Needs fixing now". Once the next run works, the problem closes and the reminders stop. Every problem is also in the Inbox (as **Needs fixing** items); see [The Needs you inbox](/guides/needs-you-inbox) and [Bot routines](/guides/bot-routines#when-a-routine-fails).

**Text me critical alerts** is off until you turn it on, in **Settings**, then **Notifications**, then **Phone and browser notifications**. When it is on and a problem becomes critical (or an open problem is raised to critical), your bot sends you one text from its own phone number: what is wrong, and to open Allternit. Add your phone number there first; if you have already saved your own number in People it is used. You must have texted the bot's number once so it is allowed to text you, and STOP always wins. You get at most one text per problem every 30 minutes, and none for problems that are not critical. Turn the switch off and nothing is sent.

**Daily summary.** Each day from 9:00 in your computer's time zone, one **Last 24 hours** item in the Inbox lists every routine that failed or was missed and every connection that went down in the last 24 hours. It does not send a push. On a day with nothing to list there is no summary, and the previous day's summary closes when the new one opens.

Incoming calls always ring a device that has notifications on. To stop rings on a device, turn notifications off on it.

You get at most one message notification per conversation every 20 seconds, and at most 40 an hour, so a busy thread doesn't flood you. Something that happened more than 12 hours ago (for example on a computer that was offline overnight) doesn't notify you when it finally arrives. It's still in Allternit.

Permission asks from a coding session on a paired computer notify you through that session's own remote-control notifications on m.allternit.com, not through this list.

## Change what notifies you

Open **Settings**, then **Notifications**, and scroll to **Phone and browser notifications**. Each event above has a switch, with its name and what it covers. A switch you haven't changed says **Default**; one you have changed shows **Reset to default**. A change saves as soon as you flip it. If it can't be saved, the switch goes back and says why.

These switches are for your whole account: they apply to every device you've turned notifications on for. On ai.allternit.com and the m.allternit.com phone app, the same section shows whether this device has notifications on, with a button to turn them on. The switches higher up the page (replies, scheduled tasks, bot threads, approvals, hand-offs, calls, usage limits, banners and sound) only change how Allternit notifies you on the device you're using.

Usage alerts follow your cloud budget's alert setting: you get one when your spending crosses your alert threshold (80% unless you changed it) and one when it reaches your whole monthly budget. Each comes once a month.

For developers: the switches use [Notification preferences](/api/notifications). The text setting is `GET/PUT /api/v1/alerts/sms` on your runtime (`{enabled, phone}`, stored as `alerts.criticalSms` in `runtime_settings`); the sweep in `netops/alerts.rs` sets `attention_items.sms_due`, sends through `phone_outbound` (so the consent gate applies) and keeps a 30-minute gap per item. The summary is a `digest` attention item with id `digest:<local date>`.
