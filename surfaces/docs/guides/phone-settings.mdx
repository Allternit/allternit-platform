The phone Settings screen has 17 rows in these groups:

- **Accounts** card: you and your organizations. See [Switch accounts and organizations](/guides/switch-accounts).
- **Account**: Profile, Billing (shows your plan), Usage (shows the percent used when it is known), Notifications, Privacy.
- **Assistant**: Customize, Memory, Voice and dictation.
- **Bots and computers**: Connections, Computers and devices, Agent rules.
- **App**: **Haptic feedback** and **Reduce motion**.
- **Appearance**: Light, Dark and System tiles.
- **Advanced**: every other settings page, in seven sections. No page was removed.
- **Log out**.

## Icon tiles

Each row has a white tile with an icon in it. The icon moves once when the screen opens and again when you press the row. With **Reduce motion** on, or when your phone asks for reduced motion, the icons stay still. See [AnimatedIcon](/architecture/animated-icon).

## The ⓘ menu

The **ⓘ** button at the top holds the app version and the links to Terms, Privacy, Help and support, and Status. The web version has no Licenses page.

## Haptic feedback and Reduce motion

**Haptic feedback** turns off every vibration in the phone layout. iPhone Safari has no vibration support, so on an iPhone the web app has no haptics either way.

**Reduce motion** replaces the drawer push, the mode fan and the zoom transitions with short fades. It also turns off the movement of the icons in Settings. It applies as soon as the app opens.

## Appearance

Light, Dark and System each show a small picture of the app in that look. Tap one to switch.

Light is the default for anyone who has not picked a theme. If you picked Light, Dark or System before, your choice is kept. Dark uses the graphite colours.

The phone layout has no **Open links in** setting on the web.
