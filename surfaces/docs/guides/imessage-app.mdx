The Allternit iMessage app puts a small panel inside a Messages conversation on your iPhone. It comes with [Allternit for iPhone](/guides/iphone-app). Open a conversation, tap the apps button next to the text field, and pick **Allternit**. It opens on the quick panel.

## What the quick panel does

| Action | What happens |
|---|---|
| Draft a reply | Opens your main bot in Allternit with a request to write a reply. You read it and send it yourself. |
| Research | Opens your main bot with a research request filled in. |
| Create an image | Opens your main bot with an image request filled in. |
| Share an artifact | Opens your main bot with a request to share a saved artifact. |
| Check a task | Shows the status card of the last phone task you started from Messages. |
| Run on my phone | Asks your bot to do a task on your linked phone, then shows its status card. |

Nothing is sent to the conversation on its own. Allternit fills in the request and you decide what to send.

## Run on my phone

1. Tap **Run on my phone** and type what your bot should do, for example "Turn on Do Not Disturb until 9".
2. Tap **Run**. Allternit opens and starts the task on your first phone that is online. The task is marked as started from the iMessage app.
3. Go back to Messages. The status card shows the task: running, needs you, done, denied, timed out, phone locked, phone offline, failed or not started.

A sensitive step (pay, delete, send, call, a password or a security setting) stops and waits for you. The card then shows **Review**, which opens the item in Allternit, where you see the phone's screen before you approve. See [Phone approvals](/guides/phone-approvals).

If your bots are paused, the task does not start and the card shows the reason in plain words.

## When the panel can't help yet

| You see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Sign in to Allternit | You are signed out on this iPhone. | Open Allternit and sign in. |
| Your session ended | The panel's access to your status expired. | Open Allternit once. |
| You're offline | Messages can't reach Allternit. | Try again when you are back online. |
| Phone minutes used up | Your phone minutes for this month are used up. | Tap **See usage**. |

## Privacy

The iMessage app does not read your messages. It reads only what Allternit for iPhone shares with it on this device: a status-only access key and the last phone task you started from Messages. It does not show the phone's screen. You see that in the Allternit app.

## For developers

- Target `AllternitMessages` (bundle id `com.allternit.mobile.messages`) in `surfaces/allternit-mobile/ios`. The panel views live in `AllternitMessages/Panel` and are also built into the app.
- The panel reads the device read token through `SystemSurfaces/SharedAccess.swift`. Cloud accepts that token only for `GET /api/v1/status/summary` and notification content, so the panel uses it to tell signed out, expired session and offline apart.
- **Run on my phone** opens `allternit://phone-task?goal=&source=imessage_ext`. The app starts `POST /api/v1/phones/:serial/task` with its own session and `source: "imessage_ext"`, then keeps the record `surfaces.phone.lastRun` in the App Group current for the panel's card. Contract: [Phone holds API](/api/phone-holds).
