---
title: "Credits and auto top-up"
description: "Buy Allternit Cloud credits, and let your balance refill itself from a saved card."
---

Allternit Cloud models and a computer on credits ([Computer on credits](/guides/credits-computer)) are paid from your **credit balance** (one credit = one US dollar). You add credits in **Settings → Billing** on Desktop, ai.allternit.com and the m.allternit.com app.

## Cloud models need credits

Free accounts don't include Cloud usage. An Allternit Cloud model answers only while your credit balance is above $0. Credits from any source count: a pack, a plan's monthly credits, or credits Allternit added to your account.

- **Plans and keys you connect don't need credits.** A plan you already pay for (Claude, ChatGPT and the others in [Use your plan](/guides/use-your-plan)) and API keys you add keep working with a $0 balance. If you added your own key for the provider behind a Cloud model, that model uses your key and is not locked.
- **Locked models stay in the list.** With no credits, Cloud models in the model picker show a lock and **Needs credits**.
- **The credits window.** Choosing a locked model, or sending a message with one, opens **Allternit Cloud models need credits** (or **Your Cloud credits are used up** once a balance reaches $0). It shows the price of the model you picked per 1M input and output tokens, the $10, $25, $50 and $100 packs, the Plus plan (it includes monthly credits) and **See all plans**. **Not now** closes it. Your previous model stays selected and the message you were typing stays in the composer.
- **After you buy.** When you come back from checkout, the app reads your balance again and selects the model you picked.

It works the same on Desktop, ai.allternit.com, the phone layout (as a sheet from the bottom) and the m.allternit.com app. On iPhone, the sheet shows the packs and links to ai.allternit.com to buy in the US App Store; elsewhere it shows no price and no link. In Gizzi Code, `gizzi models` marks these rows `needs credits`.

### Cloud models with no computer

With credits, you can chat with Allternit Cloud models without a computer. The reply comes straight from Allternit Cloud, and the chat is saved to your account like a plan chat, so it shows in the sidebar on web, the phone app and iPhone. Tools and agents still need a computer. If a computer is connected, Cloud models keep running through it as before.

## Ways to add credits

- **Plans** (Plus, Max, Ultra) add a set amount of credits each month. Unused plan credits roll over up to the plan's limit. Settings → Billing shows your plan as a member card with your name and renewal date, and each plan as a ticket. Plus is graphite, Max is gold, Ultra is platinum. A failed payment shows as a stamp on the card.
- **Credit packs** of $10, $25, $50 or $100 add credits once. Payment is by card or Link through Stripe Checkout.
- **Auto top-up** buys a pack for you when your balance runs low.

When you buy a credit pack, Stripe saves the card to your Allternit billing account so auto top-up can use it later. Auto top-up stays off until you turn it on.

Credits also let a session that paused at your own subscription's usage limit keep going right away: tap **Keep going on Allternit Cloud** on the paused strip. See [When a usage limit pauses a session](/guides/subscriptions#keep-going-on-allternit-cloud).

## Turn on auto top-up

1. Open **Settings → Billing** and find the **Auto top-up** card under the credit packs.
2. If the card says **Add a card**, select it. Stripe opens a page to save a card. No money is taken at this step. If you already bought a pack or have a plan, your saved card shows here instead (for example "Visa •• 4242").
3. Choose:
   - **When balance drops below**: the threshold, $5 or more.
   - **Add**: the amount to buy each time, one of $10, $25, $50 or $100.
   - **Monthly limit**: the most auto top-up can spend in one calendar month (UTC). The default is $200 and the most you can set is $5000.
4. Switch **Auto top-up** on.

New accounts can choose the larger amounts after their first payment has settled, the same rule that applies to the $50 and $100 packs.

## How it works

- After each charge to your balance (a model reply, cloud computer time), Allternit checks your balance. If it is under your threshold, it charges your saved card for your chosen amount.
- The charge runs in the background. Your request is never slowed down or stopped by it.
- Only one top-up runs at a time. The credits appear when Stripe confirms the payment, usually within seconds.
- If a top-up would take you over your monthly limit, it does not run. The limit resets at the start of each month.
- Each top-up shows on your Stripe receipts and in **Manage billing** like any other purchase.

## When a top-up fails

If your card is declined, has expired, or your bank asks you to confirm the payment (which can't happen while you are away), Allternit:

- turns auto top-up **off**, so it does not try again,
- shows the reason on the Auto top-up card,
- sends you a notification and an email.

Your card is not charged for a failed attempt. Fix the card (or add a new one with **Change card**), then switch auto top-up back on.

## If a payment is disputed

If a payment for credits is disputed with your bank (a chargeback), the credits from that payment are taken back out of your balance while the bank decides. Your balance can show below zero until then. Auto top-up is turned off and cannot be turned back on while the dispute is open, or after it is lost.

- If the dispute is decided in Allternit's favor, the credits come back.
- If the bank refunds the payment to you, the credits stay removed.

Contact support if you think a payment was disputed by mistake.

## Turn it off

Switch **Auto top-up** off at any time. Your saved card stays on file for the next time you buy credits. To remove the card, use **Manage billing**.

<Note>
Auto top-up refills your personal Allternit Cloud balance. Credits moved to an organization ledger are not watched by auto top-up.
</Note>

For developers: the routes and webhook events are documented in [Cloud billing API](/api/cloud-billing).
