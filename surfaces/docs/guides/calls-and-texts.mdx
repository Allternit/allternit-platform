title: Calls and texts in threads
description: How a phone number's calls and texts appear in a thread on Desktop, ai.allternit.com and the m.allternit.com phone app.
---

When a bot has a [phone number](/guides/messaging-channels), its calls and texts show up in one thread per contact. A caller who texts and then calls lands in the same thread.

## Texts

A thread on a phone number looks like a text conversation: your contact's messages on the left, the bot's on the right, with a delivery state under each message you send.

- The composer shows a **segment counter**. A text is 160 characters in plain GSM characters (153 per segment when it splits), or 70 (67) if it contains emoji or other non-GSM characters.
- **Pending carrier registration.** New US numbers need carrier registration before texts go out. Until it's approved, a quiet banner says "Texts out are pending carrier registration — replies send once it's approved." You can still send; if the carrier refuses, the reason shows on that message. Calls work during this time.
- **STOP.** If a contact texts STOP, texts to them are blocked and the thread says so until they text START.

## Start a text or a call

You don't have to wait for someone to text first. In the bot's chat, under its conversations, choose **New text** or **Have &lt;bot&gt; call…** (shown when the bot has a number).

1. Type the number. It formats as you go: ten digits are a US number, and `+` starts an international one.
2. For a text, write the message. For a call, say what it's about — that is the bot's brief for the call.
3. Choose **Send text** or **Call**. A text appears in its thread at once and shows **Failed to send** with the reason if it doesn't go. A call opens the call screen showing **Dialing…**; if nothing connects in a minute it says "The call did not connect".

From an existing text thread, **Call** above the thread has the bot call that contact, and the message box sends a text from the bot's number.

**First contact needs permission.** The bot can only reach someone who has texted or called the number, or whom you've added as a contact. Otherwise you'll see "They haven't texted or called this number yet. Add them as a contact to allow the bot to reach them." Choose **Add contact** and Allternit adds them and tries again. Nothing is added unless you choose it.

Developers: the app calls `POST /api/v1/phone/text`, `POST /api/v1/phone/call` and `POST /api/v1/phone/contacts` on the runtime, and lists numbers with the cloud's `GET /api/v1/phone/numbers`. A call the bot can't place without consent returns `403 {"error":"no_consent"}`. Client: `src/lib/gateway/phone-outbound.ts`; UI: `src/components/gateway/phone/NewPhoneConversation.tsx` in allternit-ai.

## Calls

A call appears in the thread as a **call card**: who called, a live timer or the outcome (`Ended · 2:14`), and the last line of the transcript. A live call has **Open call**.

A live call also opens the **call screen** by itself. It shows the number, a call timer, the live transcript, and these controls:

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Mute / Unmute | Mutes the bot's voice on the call. |
| Keypad | Sends touch-tone digits, for phone menus. |
| Speaker | Local output for audio when you take over. |
| Hold / Resume | Puts the caller on hold. |
| Transfer | Sends the call to another number, **cold** (hand off now) or **warm** (introduce first). |
| Take over | Joins the call yourself. |
| Hang up | Ends the call. |

Buttons show what the call is actually doing (confirmed by the voice service), not what you tapped a moment ago, so a button may take a second to change. When a call ends the screen becomes a summary with the reason, duration, and whether it was recorded. Every call begins with a fixed line that says the caller is speaking with an AI and whether the call is recorded.

## Texting and calling out

Your bot can start a text or a call from its own number, and so can you.

- **Ask the bot.** Say "text Sam that I'm running late" or "call the dentist to confirm Friday". Any bot with a phone number has two tools, `phone_text` and `phone_call`.
- **Do it yourself.** The runtime has routes for starting a text or call and adding contacts (see [Phone numbers and SMS (runtime)](/guides/phone-sms-runtime)); the **Text** and **Call** buttons on Desktop, ai.allternit.com and the m.allternit.com phone app are coming in a UI update. A text you start appears in the thread as a message from the bot; a call appears as a call card.

### Who the bot may contact

The bot only texts or calls people who **texted or called this number first**, or that you **added as a contact**. Anyone else is refused, and the bot tells you so ("+1 415 555 0123 hasn't texted or called this number and isn't on your allowed contacts"). To allow someone, add them under the number's contacts; that records your consent for that person on that number.

**STOP always wins.** If a person texts STOP, the bot can't text or call them again, even if they are a contact, until they text START.

### Texting pending carrier approval

US texting needs carrier registration (10DLC). Until your number is approved, texts out are held back and the composer says so. Calls don't need it. Outbound calling is switched on per deployment; where it isn't on yet, the bot says outbound calling isn't available.

## On a phone

On **ai.allternit.com** on a phone and in the **m.allternit.com** app:

- The call screen takes the whole screen, with large round buttons and a bigger keypad. Close it with **✕** at the top; the call carries on and the card stays in the thread.
- Call cards and text bubbles fit a 390 px screen: long numbers wrap and **Open call** is a full-width button under the card.
- Text composers are sized so the phone does not zoom when you tap them.
- **New text**, **Have &lt;bot&gt; call…** and the thread's **Call** button are full-height touch targets, and their fields are sized so the phone does not zoom.

## Connect an Android phone

A separate feature lets your bot operate a **spare Android phone** (see its screen, tap, type, send a text or place a call from the phone's SIM). That needs Allternit Desktop. On a phone, Settings → Computers → Phones shows "Connect a phone from Allternit Desktop." Once connected, the bot can use the phone from any of your devices. Full guide: [A phone for your bot (Android)](/guides/phone-for-your-bot).
