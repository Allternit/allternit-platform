Everything here works the same in Allternit Desktop, on ai.allternit.com (including its phone layout) and in the m.allternit.com app. Longer tasks used to have their own mode; they now run in Chat.

## Approval

The permission pill sits beside **+** in the chat's text box. It sets what the agent may do without asking you:

| Option | What the agent does |
| --- | --- |
| **Auto** | Keeps working. It still asks before it deletes files, pays, signs, or installs software, and it can still ask you questions. This is the default. |
| **Ask before actions** | Asks before it runs a command or edits a file. |
| **Read-only** | Only reads and researches. It does not change anything. |

Chats made before this change that were set to approve edits automatically now show **Auto**.

The choice is saved with the chat and sent with every message, so it holds after a reload and on your other devices. Change it at any time; the next step the agent takes uses the new setting.

## Bottom deck

A new chat opens with the bottom deck under the text box: **Agent mode**, **Plugins** and **Artifacts**, and a gallery of starting points (Doc, Slides, Sheet, Site, Image) over the moving dot background. Once the chat is under way, the deck shrinks to one row. The text box has no project picker; start a project chat from the **Projects** tab.

## Workspace in the + menu

The **+** menu has a **Workspace** section: **Agents** (a team of agents as a pipeline), **Web** (an agent working in the browser), **Routines**, **Loops** and **Sync** (connectors and what they sync). On Desktop each opens in a pane beside the chat; on a phone it opens full screen with a Back button.

## Studio for docs, slides and websites

When a chat is making a document, a slide deck or a website, it opens in the studio: the conversation on one side and the thing being made in panes beside it. You can read and edit the draft while the agent works on it. On a phone, the panes open full screen from the session header.

## Computer

The **computer** button in the chat's session header shows which computer the chat runs on (this computer on Desktop, or the paired computer the web app relays to) and whether it is online. Click it to open the computer's card:

- On Desktop, the macOS permissions computer use needs, **Screen recording** and **Accessibility**. A row that is not granted has an **Allow** button that opens the permission guide.
- Who is in control of the browser run linked to the chat, with **Take control**, **Take over**, **Hand back** or **Ask for control**.
- **Show in pane** puts the computer's screen in the chat's pane. **Open browser** opens the browser view, and **Manage devices…** (or **Connect a computer…** when none is connected) opens device settings.

## Session panel

Every chat has a **session button** at the right of its header. While the agent works through a plan, it is the [progress chip](/guides/progress-pane): a ring that fills as steps finish and **Step 2 of 4**, then **All 4 done**. Otherwise it shows a file icon and how many outputs the chat has made. On Desktop, click it to open the session panel in the chat's right pane, and click it again (or the pane's close button) to hide it. On a phone it opens as a sheet.

The panel has up to three sections. A section only appears once there is something in it.

### Progress

Progress shows the agent's plan as a checklist, not each tool it calls. A plan comes from the agent's task list, from a planned A:// run, or from a browser run going on in this session. A short task with no plan has no Progress section. Its steps are in the conversation instead.

- Finished steps are ticked, the step in progress spins, and a failed step gets a red cross.
- The checklist is saved with the session, so it is still there when you reopen it.

Chat asks the agent to keep a task list for any task of more than two steps, so most longer tasks have a Progress section. The plan is no longer drawn in the conversation; it lives here. See [Progress](/guides/progress-pane).

### Outputs

Outputs shows what the session made as small previews:

- **Artifacts** the agent saved (pages, docs, sheets, slides, designs, diagrams, motion). The preview is drawn from the artifact itself.
- **Files and media** it created, such as Markdown, HTML, SVG, code, images and videos.

One output takes the full width. Two or more sit side by side, and **Show all** appears after six. Click an output to open it in the chat's pane, where it can be expanded, shared or downloaded. On phones it opens full screen.

### Used in this session

This section lists what the agent used to do the work, grouped by type:

| Row | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Project folder | The folder bound to the session. The folder-plus button adds one (Desktop). |
| **Web search** | The searches it ran and the sites it read. The row shows the sites' icons. |
| **Skills** | Skills it loaded, such as `artifact-design`. |
| **Files** | Files it read or searched. |
| **Terminal** | Commands it ran. |
| A connector name | Calls to that connector, such as Google Drive. |
| **Agents**, **Memory Drive**, **Tools** | Subagents, memory lookups and other tools. |

Click a row to see its list. In **Web search**, the list has **Searches** and **Sites read**, with the number of pages read on each site.

- **Click a search, file, command or call** to go to that step in the conversation. The panel closes, the step opens with its input and result, and it is highlighted for a moment.
- **Click a site** to see the pages read on it. Click a page to go to its step, or click the open icon to open the page in your browser.

## In the conversation

Chat shows the agent's work as one stream: the plan line, a "Using N tools" header with the steps on a thin line under it, chips for sources, files and failures, and then one "Used 4 tools · 12s" line you can open into the full trail. The agent's notes between steps are part of the trail. Click a step to see its input and result. Steps in your connected apps show the app's logo and what they found; see [Agent steps in replies](/guides/agent-steps).

## On phones

On ai.allternit.com's phone layout and in the m.allternit.com app, tap the session button (or the progress chip) to open the panel as a sheet from the bottom; drag it down to close it. It is also in the session header's pane menu as **Session**, which opens it full screen. Rows in **Used in this session** open in place, and jumping to a step closes the pane and scrolls the conversation to it.

## Quick task

The Quick task button on the rail's **New** row opens a small composer over the screen you are on. Type a request, pick an approval option and send: it starts a new chat and runs there while you keep working. Several can be open at once. They stack at the bottom right and shrink to chips that say **Running** while the agent works. Click **Open** to go to the chat.

## Tasks on Home

The task board, its runs and drafts are on Home under **Tasks**. See [Tasks](/guides/tasks). Routines and Loops keep their own pages; see [Bot routines](/guides/bot-routines).

## Older sessions

Sessions you started before this change are listed with your other chats and open in Chat. Links to them keep working.
