## Comments

Open **Comments** in the artifact window header. On a phone it covers the
screen.

- Select text, cells, a slide or an element first and the comment attaches to
  it; the thread shows **On …** with what it points at.
- Replies stay in the thread. **Resolve** folds a thread away (**Resolved (n)**
  shows them again; **Reopen** brings one back). You can delete your own
  comments; the owner can delete any.
- Comments refresh every 20 seconds while the window is open.

### Ask Allternit in a comment

Write `@gizzi` or `@allternit` in a comment (or press **Ask Allternit** on a
thread that mentions it). Allternit receives the comment and what it points at
and either changes just that part, saving a new version, or answers. Its
answer appears in the thread as **Allternit**. While it works the thread says
**Waiting for Allternit…**. If it answers in the chat instead of changing the
artifact, the reply stays in the chat.

Who answers depends on where you are:

- **You can edit and have a conversation open:** Allternit works in that
  conversation and can change the artifact, as above.
- **Otherwise** (you can only comment, or the app is closed on a phone):
  Allternit's servers answer in the thread within a few seconds. This reply
  reads the artifact and the thread but doesn't change the artifact; it says
  what it would change, and an editor can ask for it in a chat. It uses your
  AI credits, like any other request you make.

If Allternit can't answer (for example you're out of credits), the reply in
the thread says why.

## What each sharing level can do

| Level | Open | Comment | Edit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Viewer | yes | no | no |
| Commenter | yes | yes | no |
| Editor | yes | yes | yes |

Docs are shared as **Viewer** or **Editor** only, so a Doc has no Commenter.
Viewers can read the comments but not add any; the Comments panel tells them
to ask the owner for more access.

## Presence

The separate live-presence avatars in the artifact toolbar show who else has the artifact open right now. A
dark ring means they're editing. Presence is live: it shows people who have
had the artifact open in the last 45 seconds, wherever they opened it, and
Allternit deletes the entry shortly after they leave. It doesn't keep a
history of who viewed what. Organization admins can turn presence off for everyone in the
org; the avatars then don't appear.

The overlapping avatars **inside** the compact Share control describe saved
access, including the owner. They are separate from live presence and do not
mean those people are currently online.

## Watch agents work

When an agent (Gizzi, or one of your bots) reads or edits a doc or sheet you
have open, you see it working there.

- **In the header.** The agent's avatar joins the presence avatars, with a
  ring while it edits. The label shows how many are here, people and agents
  together (**3 here**), and the agents' model cost so far (**$0.43**) when it
  is known. Hover or focus the avatars for each agent's name and what it is
  doing.
- **Cursors.** A labelled cursor sits on the block or cell the agent is
  working on. It shows the agent's name, its cost so far and a short task
  line, such as "Fixing the date". It moves as the agent moves on, and it
  doesn't move with an animation when your system asks for reduced motion.
- **Suggestions.** When an agent changes content that is already there, the
  change arrives as a suggestion; nothing in your artifact changes yet. The
  spot is highlighted in the agent's colour. Click it, or press Enter on it,
  to see the agent, its reason, the old text struck through and the new text
  underlined, then choose **Accept** or **Reject**. Esc closes it. On a phone
  this opens as a sheet from the bottom of the screen.
- **Accept all.** While suggestions are waiting, the header shows
  **N suggestions · $X · Accept all** (**N · Accept all** on a phone).
  Accept all applies them in order and saves one version, noted with the
  agents' names. A suggestion that no longer fits, because you or someone
  else already changed that text or cell, is marked out of date and skipped.
- **Agent comments.** When an agent asks a question in the comments, the
  comment appears under the agent's name and avatar, not yours.
- **Live refresh.** When an agent saves a new version of an artifact you have
  open, it reloads. If you have unsaved edits it doesn't; a small notice says
  **Updated by** the agent, with **Reload**.

Agents save directly (a new version, no suggestions) when they create new
content, work on other kinds of artifacts, or when you asked them to apply a
change directly. Everything they save is in the version history, so you can
go back. Seeing agents needs you to be signed in, and an agent can only make
suggestions on an artifact you can edit.

In the Docs editor in a Cowork pane, an agent's edits appear as tracked
changes under the agent's name, and you accept or reject them with the
editor's own tracked-changes controls.

## Versions

Every autosave is a version, with who made it (**You**, **A collaborator**, or
**By Gizzi**). Editors open any version from the clock menu. Owners can pin the
version viewers see in the Share window.
