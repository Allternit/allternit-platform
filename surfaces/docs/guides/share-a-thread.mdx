You can share a conversation with anyone by link. They do not need an account.

## Create a link

- **Desktop, web and the m.allternit.com app:** click **Share** in the header of a Chat, Cowork, Code or bot thread. On the phone layout, open the header **⋯** menu and pick **Share link**.
- **iPhone:** pick **Share link…** in the reply bar menu or the thread header.
- **Gizzi in the terminal:** type `/share`. `/share off` turns the link off.

In the dialog, choose **Create link**, then **Copy link**. A new chat can be shared once it has its real id, which is after the first reply.

The link looks like `https://ai.allternit.com/t/<token>`.

## What the link shows

The link shows a **snapshot** taken when you created it, not the live thread. Later messages do not appear until you choose **Update link**.

| Shown | Left out |
| --- | --- |
| Your messages and the replies (text) | Tool inputs and outputs |
| The names of the tools that ran | Thinking |
| Small images (up to 300 KB each, at most 10 per thread) | Attached files |

Long messages are cut at 20,000 characters. Anyone with the link can read it without signing in. Search engines are told not to index it.

## Update or stop

- **Update link** takes a fresh snapshot and keeps the same link.
- **Stop sharing** turns the link off. Opening it then shows that sharing is turned off.

Deleting your account deletes your shared links.

## For developers

The links are stored by Allternit Cloud. See [Cloud API, thread shares](/api/cloud-api#thread-shares).
