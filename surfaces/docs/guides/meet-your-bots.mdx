title: Meet your bots
description: Build your first team of bots, pick who leads it, see and change when they work, and give them a first job, in one card during setup or on your first visit to Bot Mode.
---

**Meet your bots** is a short card that sets up your bots. It is step 4 of [first-run setup](/guides/first-run-setup), and it opens on your first visit to Bot Mode if you didn't finish it in setup. It works the same in Allternit Desktop, on ai.allternit.com and in the m.allternit.com app.

Nothing is created while you go through it. Your team, your main bot, the routines and the first job are kept as a draft until you select **Start the job**.

## The pages

| Page | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Start** | Watch the Bots network: Gizzi in the middle while Claude, Dots, Grok and Muse pass a job along, then the team grows. Select **Build my team**, or **Skip for now**. |
| **Who's on your team?** | Add bots from the [template library](/guides/bot-templates) (all built-in templates, with category filters). |
| **Who leads your team?** | Pick your main bot (setup only). |
| **When your bots work** | See and change the routines your bots come with. |
| **Give a first job** | Pick the first thing your team does, and edit it. |
| **Your team is ready** | See what was set up, and add bots your team hands work to. |

In Bot Mode you already have a main bot, so the card skips **Who leads your team?** and greets you with your main bot's name. **Back** returns to the previous page. **Skip** closes the card at any point.

## Build your team

Each card in the list is a ready-made template: its character, name and what it does. To add a bot:

- **Drag** a card onto the team grid. The grid lights up while you drag over it. On a phone, start the drag from the bot's picture so the list still scrolls.
- **Tap** a card. It goes to the next free spot on the grid.

Drag bots around inside the grid to arrange them. **×** on a bot removes it. Your main bot sits in the middle; until you pick one it shows **Main bot**. Bots you already have from a template start on the grid and are never created twice.

Each template brings its default character (for example Inbox Triage is Modem and Research Analyst is Rimraf), on the template's own colour. The bot is created wearing it, and you can change its look later.

## Pick your main bot

This is the [bot setup picker](/guides/bot-setup-picker): a large stage with the character you picked, its welcome line beside it, and the looks in two rows.

- **Allternit**: **A://** (first, and picked for you), Gizzi, Goldie, Rimraf, Yessir, Spicy and Modem.
- **Bots you already use**: Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Meta AI. Picking one runs that vendor's real connect steps with your own account.
- **+ New bot** and **Import**.

The characters move on their own. Gizzi's eyes follow your pointer, and tapping the stage makes the character jump. **What your main bot can and can't do** shows the main bot's rules.

## Change when your bots work

The routines your bots come with (for example Inbox Triage checks new mail every 2 hours on weekdays) are shown on the same editable calendar as a bot's [Routines tab](/guides/bot-routines#edit-routines-on-the-calendar), opening on **Day**:

- Click an empty time to add a routine there. Choose which bot runs it and what it does.
- Click a routine (on the calendar or in the list) to change who runs it, what it does and when. **Remove** drops it.
- Drag a routine up or down to move it.
- The switch next to each routine in the list keeps it or pauses it.

## Give a first job

Every template comes with a first job that shows it works, and your main bot has one too. Pick one: it opens with the job's text, which you can edit, and a few other things to try. The other side shows who does it (your main bot, or the bot it hands the job to) and what you'll get back. Nothing is sent, posted or paid without your approval.

**Start the job** creates everything, in this order:

1. Your main bot (or connects the vendor bot you picked).
2. Each bot on your team, from its template.
3. The routines you kept, with the times you set. Paused routines are created paused.
4. Thread #1: the job you picked, sent once to the bot that owns it.

## When it's done

**Your team is ready** lists what happened: main bot, team, routines and thread #1. If something didn't work (for example a bot couldn't be created), that line says why, the rest still goes ahead, and **Retry what didn't finish** tries only the failed parts.

**Works well with your team** lists bots that your team hands work to and that you don't have yet. Drag one onto the grid or tap it to add it. Its routines start paused.

In setup, the button goes on to the map of everything you connected. In Bot Mode it opens your bots.

## Shown once

The card is shown once per account. Finishing it or skipping it marks it seen on ai.allternit.com and in the m.allternit.com app. Allternit Desktop remembers it per computer. To see it again, choose **Replay intro** in the Bots rail **⋯** menu, or in the Bots header on a phone.

If you have no main bot when you open Bot Mode, **Set up your main bot** opens first, as before. The card follows once you have one.
