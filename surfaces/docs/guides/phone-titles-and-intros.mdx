This page covers the phone finish. It applies to the iPhone app, the Android app, m.allternit.com and the phone layout of ai.allternit.com. Desktop shows the titles, intros and arrivals but has no drawer push or brain sheet.

## Tab titles

Each tab opens with its name in the A:// block type. The tabs are Projects, Artifacts, Automation Tasks, Customize and Freeweights.

As you scroll, the title moves into the top bar one letter at a time. When the last letter lands, the title flashes coral. The description above it blurs and fades out as you scroll.

With Reduce Motion on, the title fades into the top bar instead. It does not move letter by letter.

## First-visit intros

An intro is a short sheet that explains a screen the first time you open it.

- **Tabs:** Projects, Artifacts, Automation Tasks, Customize and Freeweights each have one.
- **Modes:** Home, Bots, Code and ACI each have one.

Tap **Continue** to close it. The button unlocks after about a second.

Each intro shows once per account, on every device. If you saw it on your iPhone, you do not see it again on the web.

The Bots intro is the same one as **Meet your bots**. You never see two Bots intros.

## Arrivals

An arrival is a short welcome when you switch to a mode or pick a bot.

- **Team arrival:** when you open Bots, your team of bots settles into place.
- **Bot arrival:** when you pick a bot, its avatar appears in its own colour. Then the chat settles.

With Reduce Motion on, arrivals fade in and nothing moves.

## Phone finish

- **Glass buttons.** Round controls are light glass. They shrink a little when you press them.
- **Drawer push.** Tap the menu button. The page slides right, shrinks and rounds its corners. Tap the strip of page to close it.
- **Floating brain sheet.** Pick a brain from a sheet that floats above the composer. It has rounded corners and a small gap on three sides.
- **Haptics.** Opening, closing, picking and docking give a light tap. Android uses the vibration motor. Desktop and iOS Safari have no haptics, so they stay silent. In the iPhone app, haptics follow your device settings.

## Switchers and filters

A switcher changes the view. A filter narrows what you see.

- **Switchers** are segmented controls with a sliding thumb. Examples are List, Graph and Calendar, and Yours and Discover.
- **Filters** are pills. You can turn several on or off.
