title: Connectors
description: Find, connect and check the apps your bots and chats can use as tools, in Customize › Connectors.
---

A **connector** gives your chats and bots tools for another app: search your Google Drive, file a Linear issue, read a HubSpot deal. Connectors run through Allternit's own connector service; you sign in to each app once.

## Find one

Open **Customize › Connectors**. The directory shows:

- **Collections** at the top: sets of connectors for developers, design and operations.
- **Top connectors**: the most used ones, with the size of the whole directory.
- **A section per category** (Communication, CRM, Storage and so on), each with **Show all**, and a cloud of category chips.

## What a connector page tells you

Open a connector to see its description, its **Tools** (green dot: only reads; orange dot: changes data), and a side column:

| Field | Means |
|---|---|
| Made by | Who publishes it. A blue check means the vendor publishes it themselves. |
| Category | Where it sits in the directory. |
| Sign-in | **Required** (you sign in to the app), **API key**, **Local CLI (no sign-in)** or **Not required**. |
| On your phone | The linked phones that have this app installed (Allternit Desktop). See [Phone Link](/guides/phone-link). |
| Connector URL, More info | The connector's address and the vendor's docs, when known. |

Only connect apps from developers you trust: Allternit does not control which tools a developer offers.

## Connect

Press **Connect** on the connector's page or card and follow its sign-in. An API-key connector asks for the key on the same page. A connected app's tools are then available in chats and to bots you allow; attach a connector to a bot from that bot's settings.

## Connectors and the apps on your phone

The phone apps Phone Link looks for use the same ids and categories as these connectors, so one app is one identity across both: Settings › Connections lists it under **Apps** with its phone lanes, and its connector page says which phones have it. A connector and a phone app are two different ways in. The connector calls the app's service with your sign-in; the phone lane drives the app itself on your phone.

## For developers

The directory is `GET /api/v1/connectors` (see [Connectors API](/api/connectors)), loaded once per session by allternit-ai `src/lib/connectors/connector-directory.ts`. The catalog ships in `cmd/allternit-api/assets/open-design/connectors.json`; the Customize screens are `src/views/customize/ConnectorsDirectory.tsx`. The "On your phone" line matches the connector id against `phone.devices` apps from Desktop's phone app catalog (`surfaces/allternit-desktop/src/main/phone-app-catalog.json`).
