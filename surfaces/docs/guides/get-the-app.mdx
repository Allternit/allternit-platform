---
title: Get the app
description: "Where to get Allternit on your phone: the App Store for iPhone, allternit.com/android for Android, and the web app on any phone."
---

Allternit runs on your phone three ways. All of them use the same account as ai.allternit.com and Allternit Desktop.

## iPhone

[Allternit for iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6828841999) is on the App Store. It is free and needs iOS 17 or later.

It is not listed in the EU countries or in China yet. If you can't find it, use the web app below. See [Allternit for iPhone](/guides/iphone-app) for sign-in and a tour.

## Android

Install from [allternit.com/android](https://allternit.com/android). See [Allternit for Android](/guides/android-app) for the steps.

{/* ANDROID_LIVE: when the app is on Google Play, add a line here reading "Get Allternit on Google Play", with the link written as plain text: play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.allternit.app */}

## Any phone

Open [m.allternit.com](https://m.allternit.com) in your phone's browser. Use **Add to Home Screen** to give it an icon. On iPhone Safari, the first-run sheet offers the App Store app first and **Add to Home Screen** second. See [Web phone](/surfaces/web-phone).

## Short links

- `allternit.com/app` sends an iPhone to the App Store, an Android phone to the Android install, and any other device to allternit.com/download.
- `allternit.com/ios` opens the App Store page.

## Desktop

Allternit Desktop is a separate download. See [Desktop](/surfaces/desktop).
