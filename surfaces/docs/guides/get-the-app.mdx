title: Get the app
description: "Where to get Allternit on your phone: the App Store for iPhone, Google Play for Android, and the web app on any phone."
---

Allternit runs on your phone three ways. All of them use the same account as ai.allternit.com and Allternit Desktop.

## iPhone

[Allternit for iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6828841999) is on the App Store. It is free and needs iOS 17 or later.

It is not listed in the EU countries or in China yet. If you can't find it, use the web app below. See [Allternit for iPhone](/guides/iphone-app) for sign-in and a tour.

## Android

[Allternit for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.allternit.app) is on Google Play. It is free and needs Android 8 or later.

To let your bots use the phone's other apps, you need the version with Phone Link from [allternit.com/android](https://allternit.com/android) instead. Allternit Desktop installs it for you when you link the phone. See [Allternit for Android](/guides/android-app).

## Any phone

Open [m.allternit.com](https://m.allternit.com) in your phone's browser. Use **Add to Home Screen** to give it an icon. On iPhone Safari, the first-run sheet offers the App Store app first and **Add to Home Screen** second. See [Web phone](/surfaces/web-phone).

## Short links

- `allternit.com/app` sends an iPhone to the App Store, an Android phone to Google Play, and any other device to allternit.com/download.
- `allternit.com/ios` opens the App Store page.
- `allternit.com/play` opens the Google Play page.
- `allternit.com/android` downloads the Android app with Phone Link.

## From Gizzi Code

Type `/mobile` (or `/ios`, `/android`) in Gizzi Code to show a QR code for the App Store or Google Play page. Press Tab to switch between them. Scan it with your phone's camera.

## Desktop

Allternit Desktop is a separate download. See [Desktop](/surfaces/desktop).
