**Android live view** shows your Android phone's screen in Allternit and lets you tap and type on it from the web (ai.allternit.com), the m.allternit.com app, the Allternit iPhone app and Allternit Desktop. There is no cable and no `adb`. The phone shares its screen through the Allternit app, and one of your own computers relays it.

## What you need

- The **Allternit** app on the Android phone, from Google Play. A build installed from a file works too (see [step 3](#set-it-up)).
- A computer to relay the screen: **Allternit Desktop** on your Mac or PC, or a paid [Allternit Cloud computer](/guides/allternit-cloud-quickstart). Free cloud computers are retired, so an account with neither shows "Your phone's screen goes through one of your computers" in the kit.
- Allternit signed in on the viewer you want to use.

If Desktop already has the phone over a cable or Wi-Fi, [Phone Link](/guides/phone-link) stays the main source: scrcpy over `adb` is sharper and carries phone audio. Android live view is what you get when the phone is not next to Desktop.

## Set it up

1. In Allternit, open the Connect kit and choose **Android phone (screen share)**. On Desktop this is Settings → Connections; on the web and the phone app it is the Connect sheet. Gizzi lists it in `gizzi connect`.
2. Step 1 of the pane, **Install Allternit on the phone**, gives you the install link. The pane then shows a short **code** and a **QR code**. Scan the QR code with the phone, or type the code into the Allternit app. The code works once and expires after 2 minutes. If you ask for too many, the kit says "Too many codes. Wait a few minutes, then try again."
3. In the app, tap **Turn on taps**. The app first shows what Accessibility will do and read, and **Agree and open Settings** takes you to Android's Accessibility settings, where you turn on **Allternit Phone Link**. On Android 13 and later, a build installed from a file can have this switch greyed out as restricted. Open Settings › Apps › Allternit, tap the three dots at the top right, choose **Allow restricted settings**, and try again. A Google Play install does not need this.
4. Approve the pair. The phone and your computer each show a 6-digit code. Check that they match, then approve on Desktop or in the kit (the pane shows "Waiting for your phone…" until the request arrives). Any viewer signed in as you can approve it, so Desktop need not be open.

Setup is done when the phone appears in your phone list as an Android phone. To remove it, revoke it from the same list or turn Accessibility off on the phone.

## Share the screen

Sharing is always the phone owner's choice:

- Open a viewer on the phone. The viewer says **Tap Start on** the phone's name to share its screen, and the phone shows a **Tap to share** notification. You can also open the app and tap **Share screen**, or use its **Share screen** shortcut.
- Android shows its own **Start** prompt. Tap it. A chip appears in the status bar while the screen is shared.
- With nobody watching, the phone **pauses** sending video and keeps the share open. After **10 minutes** with no viewer, sharing stops and the app says "It stopped after 10 minutes with nobody watching."
- Sharing also stops when you tap **Stop sharing** in the app, tap the status-bar chip, revoke the share in Android, lock the phone (Android 15 and later) or restart it. The viewer then says "Sharing stopped on the phone." with **Retry**.

Rotating the phone resizes the picture; the viewer redraws it upright.

## Tap and type

A viewer starts read-only. To act on the phone, choose **Allow taps** in the viewer. This is the same approval Allternit asks for before any bot or viewer drives a device, and the phone must have **Allternit Phone Link** turned on in Accessibility. Taps, swipes, typing, Back, Home and Recent apps then work from the viewer. Accessibility never acts on the lock screen, in Settings or inside the Allternit app.

On the Allternit iPhone app, the live view is for watching and for taps when you allow them; the phone list shows **Screen and taps** or **Screen only, taps off**.

Bots do not use this lane to act. They keep the approval hold described in [Phone approvals](/guides/phone-approvals), and the relay refuses `input.events` on the bot route.

## Limits

- Apps that block screen capture (many banking, video and password apps) show **black** in the viewer. This is Android's rule, not a bug.
- No phone audio on this lane. Use Phone Link over `adb` for audio.
- The computer that relays the phone must be online: Desktop open, or your cloud computer running.
- The picture takes one hop through your relay, so it can lag more than a cable.

## Troubleshooting

| What the viewer says | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Tap Start on your phone to share its screen. | Tap the **Tap to share** notification, or open the app and tap **Share screen**, then tap **Start** in Android's prompt. |
| Open the Allternit app on your Android phone. | The app is not running. Open it once. |
| Sharing stopped on the phone. | You, a lock, a restart or the 10 minute idle stop ended it. Choose **Retry**. |
| The computer that relays your phone went offline. Open Allternit Desktop or start your cloud computer, then Retry. | Bring the relay computer back online. |
| Update Allternit on your computer to share an Android phone's screen. | The relay runs an older Allternit. Update Desktop, or restart the cloud computer after it updates. |
| Your phone's screen goes through one of your computers. Open Allternit Desktop on your Mac or PC, or get an Allternit Cloud computer. | Free cloud computers are retired. Open Desktop or add a paid cloud computer. |
| Turn on taps in the Allternit app on the phone. | Turn on **Allternit Phone Link** in Android Accessibility (allow restricted settings first on a file install). |
| Tap Allow taps to tap and type on the phone. | Choose **Allow taps** in the viewer. |
| Update Allternit Desktop to turn on taps for this phone. | Update Desktop. |
| That request expired. Ask the phone to try again. | A pair request lasts a short time. Start the pair again from the phone. |
| Sign in to Allternit to add a phone. | Sign in on this viewer, then try again. |
