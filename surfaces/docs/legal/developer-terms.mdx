### Billing and payment

- You pay through Stripe, our payment processor. By choosing a plan you authorize us to charge your payment method for the plan and your usage.

- If a charge fails, Stripe retries it and we email the project owner. You can update your payment method in the console at any time.
- While retries are running, your projects keep working. If the payment is still unpaid when retries end, billable actions stop (the API answers `402 payment_method_required`) until the balance is paid.

### Billing disputes
