# Allternit Platform API Acceptable Use Policy

This policy applies to everyone who uses the Allternit Platform API ("the API"), including developers and the end-customer businesses they serve. It is part of the Allternit Developer Terms. Allternit LLC ("Allternit", "we") may suspend a project that breaks it.

## 1. Tell people they are talking to an AI

Every call and conversation an agent starts or answers must say that it is an AI assistant. You may reword the disclosure. You may not remove it, hide it, or tell people they are talking to a human. The API refuses agent greetings that leave it out.

## 2. Consent and opt-out

- Only call or text people who contacted that number first or who gave you recorded consent. Record consent through the API with its source and evidence.
- STOP and similar opt-out words are always honored and cannot be overridden through the API.
- Follow the laws that apply to calls and texts where you and the recipient are, including the TCPA, the CAN-SPAM Act, carrier rules, and do-not-call rules.

## 3. Recording

Call recording is off by default. If you turn it on, you are responsible for getting the consent the law requires. The API plays a consent prompt in all-party-consent states.

## 4. Voices and likeness

Use only the stock voices the API offers. Do not clone or imitate a real person's voice or likeness unless that person has given documented permission that Allternit has reviewed and keeps on file.

## 5. Carrier registration

Each end-customer business that sends texts must register its own brand and campaign through the API. Do not register on behalf of a business that has not authorized it, and do not send traffic that does not match the registered campaign.

## 6. Not allowed at launch

You may not use the API for:

- healthcare services that involve protected health information (Allternit does not sign business associate agreements);
- legal advice or legally privileged communications;
- financial advice;
- debt collection;
- political campaigns or political messaging;
- lead generation, purchased lists, or cold outreach.

## 7. Always prohibited

- Illegal content or activity, fraud, phishing, or impersonation.
- Harassment, threats, hate speech, or sexual content involving minors.
- Spoofing caller ID or sender identity.
- Toll fraud, traffic pumping, or attempts to get around spend caps, rate limits, or concurrency limits.
- Attempts to break, probe, or overload the API or other customers' data, except through a security disclosure process Allternit has agreed to.
- Collecting credentials or payment details under false pretenses.

## 8. Enforcement

Allternit may suspend a key, a number, an agent, or a whole project that breaks this policy, and will tell the project owner why when it can do so lawfully. Repeated or serious violations can end your access. Report abuse to legal@allternit.com.

## 9. Hosted apps and live links

This section applies to apps and links served from allternitusercontent.com: hosted apps at `SLUG.allternitusercontent.com` and live links at `NAME.live.allternitusercontent.com`. Hosted apps run on Allternit servers. A live link reaches an app that runs on the owner's own computer through an Allternit relay. The person who makes the app or link (the owner) is responsible for what it shows and does. Allternit is the host and carrier.

### What an app or link must not do

- Collect passwords, payment details or other sign-in details while pretending to be another service or person.
- Spread malware, or try to break into, scan or overload other systems.
- Show or share sexual content involving minors, or content that is illegal where it is served.
- Harass, threaten or target a person, or publish private information about someone without their consent.
- Infringe copyright, trademarks or other rights, or imitate a person or organization to mislead.
- Sell or promote illegal goods or services, or run scams.
- Use another person's voice or likeness without documented permission.
- Hide what it does from the people who open it, or ask them for more permission than it needs.
- Be used to avoid a limit or a stop we set, for example by remaking a stopped link to the same content.

Each app states its permission level. An app may use only what that level allows.

### Limits

Live links: 3 active at a time and 20 GB of traffic a month per account. Limits can change, and we say so in the product first.

### Reports and takedown

Report an app or link at abuse@allternit.com. Include the link and what is wrong. We read reports on business days.

We may pause, stop or remove an app or link, or limit an account, when we think this policy is broken, when the law or a court requires it, or when it puts other people or our systems at risk. We may act first and tell the owner right after. A stopped link stays stopped. The owner gets a short notice of what we did and why, unless the law stops us from saying.

All apps share one domain, allternitusercontent.com. If a safe-browsing service flags one app or link, that can affect other apps on the domain, and we may take down the flagged one to protect the rest.

Copyright complaints go to the same address. We tell the owner, the owner may reply, and repeat infringers lose their account.

### Owner responsibilities

The owner keeps the app's content and data lawful, keeps their computer secure while a live link is on, and answers abuse reports about their app. The owner can always pause or stop a link.

### What we do not promise

We do not promise that an app or link is always available, fast or reachable from every network. A live link works only while the owner's computer is on and connected. We do not review apps before they go live and do not vouch for apps made by others. Anyone who opens an app does so at their own risk. Apps at the Browser level cannot read viewer data.
