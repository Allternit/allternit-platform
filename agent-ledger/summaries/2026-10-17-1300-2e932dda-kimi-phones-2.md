## What was done

No VNC anywhere in the phone lane; the computer lane's VNC is untouched.

- **Desktop main (platform)**: scrcpy wire protocol pinned to server v5.0.1 (sha256-verified release download, never bundled), `PhoneStreamManager`, `phone:stream-*` IPC + preload contract, `agentBusy` takeover counter, `PhoneUserComms` (user-lane SMS read/send + dial/end/state over `content://sms`/`isms` with OEM-block honest states and an emergency confirm gate) wired to IPC/preload, and `PhoneLinkPhoneSocket` — one cloud socket per linked phone; `viewer_attached` starts scrcpy and forwards meta/binary/end; `viewer_detached` graces 30s.
