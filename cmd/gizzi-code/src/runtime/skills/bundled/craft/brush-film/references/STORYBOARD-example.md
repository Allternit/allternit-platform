# STORYBOARD: "Lost Sock" (an example of the shape; the film itself is made up)

**Logline:** Blot wants its missing sock back, but the dryer keeps eating everything, so Blot climbs inside, and finds
a whole sock kingdom that has crowned the sock its king.
**Turn:** inside the dryer (0:38). **Ending rhymes with the opening:** the same laundry room, the same single sock on
the line, but now it's Blot's crown that hangs there.
**Format:** 16:9, 60 s, plus one vertical cut. **Voices:** Narrator (warm, dry), Blot (two lines), the Sock King (one).
**World and palette arc:** pale morning laundry room (cream, sky, teal) -> dark drum interior (indigo, violet, glow)
-> warm kingdom (ochre, rose). Colour gets warmer as Blot gets braver.
**Motif:** the single sock on the line. **Running gag:** every time Blot turns its back, the dryer swallows something else.
**On-screen words allowed:** VO subtitles only. No signs, labels or captions that repeat the story.
