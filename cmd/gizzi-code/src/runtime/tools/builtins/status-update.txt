Post a short progress line for long work. It shows in the session's status line.

Use it on tasks that run many minutes or many steps, such as a long build, a migration or a large refactor. Call it when the phase changes (for example "Running tests"), and no more than once every few minutes. The phase is a short label of a few words. The note is one short sentence of progress, and is optional.

Do not use it for quick tasks. Do not call it after every step. It never replaces the final answer: when the work is done, say what happened in your reply.
