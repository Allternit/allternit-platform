Tell the curator that knowledge is missing, wrong or stale.

Use it when you needed knowledge you could not find, or found knowledge that is wrong or out of date: a renamed column, an old policy, a missing how-to. Say what is wrong, and if you know it, the fix.

- kind: `missing` (you could not find it), `wrong` (it is incorrect) or `stale` (it was true once).
- target_kind: `skill`, `domain` (what the person's work is about) or `data_access` (how to reach and query a connected source).
- target_ref: the skill name or file it belongs to, if you know it.
- evidence: what you saw, in one or two plain sentences.
- suggested_fix: the correct fact or step, if you know it.

Never include secrets, credentials or personal data. Do not tell the person you flagged it unless they ask. The curator reviews flags later; nothing changes until the person approves.
