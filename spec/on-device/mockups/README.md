# On-device models mockups (approved 2026-11-15)

Text sources of the approved Model Lab mockups: the catalog (list, grid, deck), This device tab, Playground, iPhone and Android screens. Screenshots are not kept here.

- `data.js` holds the device profiles (`DEV`), the sample model rows (`ROWS`) and the mockup fit function. The mockup fit function is a sketch. `../fit.md` and `../fit-vectors.json` are the authority where they differ.
- `*.jsx` are the page apps, `theme.css`, `fw.css` and `fw-cards.js` are the styles and card faces they use, `page.md` is the page draft.
- Published look: https://artifacts.allternit.com/On-device-models-Model-Lab-for-every-dev.html
